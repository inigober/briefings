# News Briefing — 22 November 2026

Spain's final campaign week turns on tactical voting and housing, Germany weighs femicide law and a presidential race, Berlin's coalition talks hinge on one Linke deputy, and an AI sell-off reshapes dealmaking abroad.

## Spain 🇪🇸

* **A survey by the pollster IDRA suggests that taxes on people owning several homes and more public housing could win over right-leaning voters, with half of PP and Vox voters backing higher taxes on multiple-home owners**

Housing is the campaign's central issue, since the fall of the government's housing decrees, which PP, Vox and Junts voted down, triggered the 29 November election. ([eldiario.es][1])

* **EL PAÍS's election model says around 40 seats are still "in the air", depending on whether left- or right-leaning voters concentrate their tactical vote in each province**

The seat model weighs the latest polls province by province, which matters because Spain's electoral system awards seats by province rather than nationally. ([EL PAÍS][2])

* **Spain's unemployment rate fell below 10% for the first time in 17 years in late 2025, and joblessness among Spanish-born men of middle age is now close to frictional levels**

An eldiario.es opinion piece uses the figures to rebut the far right's claim that Spanish workers are being "replaced" by migrants. ([eldiario.es][3])

## Germany 🇩🇪

* **Justice Minister Stefanie Hubig wants it to be easier to classify femicides, killings of women because they are women, as murder under the criminal code**

It is the latest in a series of bills she has proposed to protect women from violence. ([Die Zeit][4])

* **A poll finds most Germans think Ilse Aigner, the likely conservative candidate for federal president, should introduce herself to the AfD and the Left Party**

Aigner has so far said no to presenting herself to either party, though she is billed as a "candidate of the centre". ([Handelsblatt][5])

* **Laboratory-confirmed bird flu cases in Germany are rising again, mainly in the north, though the outbreak is not as severe as last year**

Avian influenza is a viral disease that spreads through wild birds and poultry, and the report says this year's wave differs from last year's in more than its size. ([Tagesspiegel][6])

## Berlin 🏙️

* **The Berlin Left Party's parliamentary group votes on expelling the controversial deputy Vanessa Emde, and a political scientist says the outcome could end coalition talks**

The Left, SPD and Greens have been negotiating a government, and party leader Ines Schwerdtner has backed the expulsion as "consistent". ([Tagesspiegel][7])

* **Berlin could save a three-digit million euro sum every year if its administration used less office space through desk sharing and gave up rented buildings**

The idea matters because the city is trying to shrink a budget hole of about five billion euros. ([rbb24][8])

* **Freenow, the ride-hailing app that stopped its hire-car business two years ago, now wants to arrange cheap hire-car drivers in Berlin again**

A manager says it is "practically impossible" to operate profitably in the sector, yet the company is returning to an industry that remains controversial in the city. ([Tagesspiegel][9])

## World 🌐

* **Mexican soldiers captured Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as "El Chapo Isidro" and one of the FBI's ten most wanted fugitives, in a special forces raid in Sinaloa**

He was arrested with two other alleged gang leaders after a gun battle that caused panic in the city of Guasave. ([The Guardian][10])

* **Amazon is close to buying the AI start-up Decart for $7.5bn, a deal that would help its cloud arm AWS compete with Nvidia in the race for computing power**

The purchase would deepen the contest among big tech groups over who controls AI infrastructure. ([Financial Times][11])

* **Estimates by the World Bank that India has cut poverty sharply are being questioned over the scale of the gains they report**

The World Bank's figures are the benchmark most governments and aid agencies use to track progress against poverty. ([Nikkei Asia][12])
