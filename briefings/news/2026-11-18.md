# News Briefing — 18 November 2026

Spain's campaign turns to courts and pensions while five EU governments, Germany among them, look to Rwanda for asylum returns, and Nvidia's record sales and a fresh wave of Chinese tech ambition keep the AI-chip race at the centre of world business.

## Spain 🇪🇸

* **Deutsche Bank's Spanish arm was the "principal financial partner" of Defex, the state-owned arms exporter, while it ran an Angola deal now tied to corruption investigations, the bank's own audits show**

Internal reviews cited in the Proyecto Rifle investigation flag "supervision deficiencies" after Guardia Civil officers raided the bank's Madrid offices in 2014 over an irregular arms sale. ([eldiario.es][1])

* **Alberto Núñez Feijóo, leader of the conservative Partido Popular (PP), wants his regular columnist, law professor Roberto Blanco Valdés, on the Constitutional Court, the body that rules on the legality of Spanish laws**

Galicia's parliament has nominated Blanco Valdés for the Senate to pick once the 29 November election reshuffles the chamber, even as critics question his impartiality after years of articles praising Feijóo. ([eldiario.es][2])

* **Pensions are the campaign's quiet fault line: the right avoids airing its doubts about the system while the left promises to shore it up despite strained public accounts**

The contrast matters because pension spending is one of the largest items in Spain's public accounts, which are under strain. ([EL PAÍS][3])

## Germany 🇩🇪

* **Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Denmark and Greece plan an offshore "return hub" in Rwanda for people with no legal right to stay in Europe, the first use of the EU's newly agreed returns law**

The pilot is expected to face legal challenges and has been denounced as "a grave moral failing" by critics. ([The Economist][4])

* **The European Union wants to speed up permits for power grids and energy storage, a step aimed at renewable power that cannot reach consumers without new lines**

The plan targets the permitting delays that hold back grid expansion across member states, Germany included. ([Die Zeit][5])

* **The EU's disease agency, the ECDC, warns on European Antibiotic Day that drug-resistant bacteria are spreading across the continent**

The warning is the agency's annual reminder that antibiotics are losing effect across the continent, Germany included. ([Tagesspiegel][6])

## Berlin 🏙️

* **After neighbouring Brandenburg pulled out, Berlin is examining a new site for a World Expo bid**

The Brandenburg refusal leaves the capital searching for alternative land for the world's fair it hopes to host. ([Tagesspiegel][7])

* **Berlin is likely to host talks between Ukraine's partners and envoys of US President Donald Trump on security guarantees for Kyiv, with Russian participation also discussed**

Berlin has become a recurring venue for the diplomacy that shapes the terms of any Ukraine settlement. ([Berliner Zeitung][8])

* **A growing number of 18-year-olds in Berlin lose youth-welfare support early, according to rbb24's reporting on the city's borough youth offices**

Youth welfare (Jugendhilfe) is the state support system for young people who cannot rely on their families, and losing it at the threshold of adulthood is the point of concern. ([rbb24][9])

## World 🌐

* **Nvidia, the dominant maker of AI chips, insists co-founder Jensen Huang will not return as chief executive, while reporting that revenue doubled last quarter and raising its forecasts for next year**

The results show AI-chip demand holding up even as the company's leadership question lingers. ([Financial Times][10])

* **Japan's Daiwa Securities is buying out its local partners to expand its investment-banking business in China**

The move gives the Japanese brokerage full control of its mainland investment-banking operations. ([Nikkei Asia][11])

* **A Costa Rican journalist, Camilo Rodríguez, was shot dead, the first such killing in the country in 25 years**

The murder jolts a nation long regarded as one of Latin America's safest for the press. ([The Guardian][12])
