# News Briefing — 14 October 2026

Spain's campaign is being set by strikers and a PP–Vox non-aggression pact, Germany has a sugar-tax deal, and China's AI export surge plus Mexico's Pacific storms set the outer frame.

## Spain 🇪🇸

* **Madrid's entire education system is walking out**

A strike billed as historic — universities, schools and nurseries together, with even the usually cautious CSIF union on board — begins Wednesday against underfunding of public education in the Community of Madrid, adding a regional labour front to the snap-election street that until now has been read as a housing story. ([eldiario.es][1])

* **Feijóo will not pick a fight with Abascal**

Alberto Núñez Feijóo, leader of the conservative People's Party, is showing "respect" for Santiago Abascal's far-right Vox, declining to demand a PP government on its own and keeping a regular channel between the two headquarters — Génova and Bambú — so that "change" outranks the PP brand. ([EL PAÍS][2])

💡 The right is already practising a coalition without putting the word on the leaflet.

* **The Socialists are trying to win the Spain that is not Madrid**

The PSOE, Spain's main centre-left party, is being forced to make up on the periphery — Catalonia, the Basque Country and other strongholds — for the lead that the PP and Vox now hold across much of the rest of the map. ([EL PAÍS][3])

## Germany 🇩🇪

* **The coalition has a sugar-tax deal — and Hesse is already calling it a price rise**

Union and SPD have agreed a levy on heavily sweetened drinks, due in cabinet on Wednesday after a long fight over the details; Hesse's premier is already branding it a *Teuer-Steuer*, a tax that makes life more expensive. ([Tagesspiegel][4])

💡 A public-health tool is being sold, and attacked, as a cost-of-living question.

* **Galeria can keep the lights on — finding owners is the next test**

Galeria, the department-store chain that entered insolvency again this month, has a going-concern plan, but the administrator now has three months to find buyers who will put in equity; the stores are open, the balance sheet is not fixed. ([Handelsblatt][5])

* **A delayed petrol-car phaseout is also a problem for bees**

Exhaust from combustion engines does not only heat the climate: a new study says it confuses bees and other pollinators, and one fuel blend is especially bad — so putting off the *Verbrenner-Aus* has an ecological cost that is not only about carbon. ([Tagesspiegel][6])

## Berlin 🏙️

* **The finance senator says the city must save through 2030**

Stefan Evers, Berlin's finance senator, is warning that debt, high spending and a heavy personnel bill meet a weak outlook, so the city will have to cut — while he still wants to protect investment. ([rbb24][7])

* **A steel beam killed a construction worker in Tempelhof**

A worker died on a Berlin-Tempelhof building site after being struck by a steel beam. ([rbb24][8])

* **Alba came back to beat Igokea in the Champions League**

Alba Berlin, the city's basketball side, won a Basketball Champions League game against Igokea after a late run. ([rbb24][9])

## World 🌐

* **China's exports jumped 36% on the back of AI shipments**

Chinese outbound trade surged in the latest month as AI-related goods led the rise, while imports were up by about a third because the Middle East conflict has made oil expensive — the boom showing up at the customs dock, not only in chip stocks. ([Financial Times][10])

💡 The AI cycle is now a trade-print story, not just a Nasdaq one.

* **Hurricane Simon and Tropical Storm Rachel killed five on Mexico's Pacific coast**

The two storms brought flash floods, downed power lines and helicopter rescues; among the dead was a 71-year-old man in Guerrero killed when a wall collapsed. ([The Guardian][11])

* **The US arrested a Saudi OpenAI employee over a bug in the office**

Investigators say a recording device was hidden behind a television in a conference room at the AI lab, and have arrested a Saudi employee of the company. ([Financial Times][12])

💡 The leak path was a bug behind a TV, not a model-weight heist.
