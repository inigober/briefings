# News Briefing — 20 September 2026

Election day in Berlin and Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, a housing squeeze that has reached Ibiza's graveyards, and a White House AI Force set against a vanished Hormuz cargo lane.

## Spain 🇪🇸

* **Homeless people are sleeping in Ibiza cemeteries**

Ibiza's housing shortage has pushed people to sleep among niches and headstones at Sant Jordi; the Sant Josep town hall has now locked the cemeteries and a sports hall after neighbours complained. ([eldiario.es][1])

💡 On a tourism island, the overflow shelter is a graveyard.

* **More than 850,000 people are still waiting for an operation**

Spain's health ministry counted about 850,000 people on surgical waiting lists at the end of 2025, with an average wait of 121 days — long enough that a 79-year-old with osteoarthritis now expects a wheelchair before a slot. ([eldiario.es][2])

## Germany 🇩🇪

* **Mecklenburg-Vorpommern elects a new Landtag today**

Voters in the north-east state choose the Schwerin parliament today, in a race framed around the lead candidates and their plans — two weeks after the CDU there was polling near the 5% line. ([Handelsblatt][3])

* **Care workers are still driving to surgeries just to swipe a card**

Germany's Pflege — the long-term-care sector — says overstretched staff still have to visit GP practices so patients' Gesundheitskarten can be read for billing, a leftover of the card-based claims system. ([Handelsblatt][4])

* **Most bystanders still do not know how to restart a heart**

When someone collapses, seconds decide survival, but many Germans remain unsure about chest-compression CPR; a Tagesspiegel explainer puts the remaining hope on teaching it younger. ([Tagesspiegel][5])

## Berlin 🏙️

* **Only one in five Berliners still approves of the Senate**

An Infratest Dimap poll before today's Abgeordnetenhaus vote finds just 20% satisfied with the city government — the lowest pre-election rating for any German state executive in the series. ([rbb24][6])

💡 Voters are being asked to rehire a Senate that almost nobody says is working.

* **A Tagesspiegel essay treats the capital's crisis as unused capacity**

Berlin is stuck in a permanent-crisis mood, the paper argues, but the practical fixes for how the city could run sit closer than the campaign talk implies. ([Tagesspiegel][7])

* **Kreuzberg has a new live-in studio: House of Wunder**

House of Wunder opens in Kreuzberg as an artists' residency that also works as a community space — a small, concrete addition to the city's studio map. ([The Berliner][8])

## World 🌐

* **Trump has announced an 'AI Force' instead of an AI law**

The US president, who has rejected calls to slow or regulate frontier models, said he will appoint a tsar to watch for "bad" behaviour — a staffing answer to a capability problem. ([Financial Times][9])

💡 The White House is building a monitor, not a rulebook.

* **The Hormuz cargo trade has largely disappeared**

The Strait of Hormuz, the Gulf chokepoint that moved everything from helium to corn, has seen that transit trade vanish; firms are still improvising longer routes. ([Financial Times][10])

💡 The military squeeze on the strait is now a missing-goods problem.

* **Russia's Duma vote is ending with only Kremlin parties on the ballot**

A three-day parliamentary election in wartime Russia is wrapping up with solely Kremlin-aligned parties standing; observers have already flagged irregularities, and the point is to bless the war course. ([Handelsblatt][11])
