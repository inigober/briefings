# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-09-18

This week holds two kinds of progressive charge: feverish trance-techno for the floor, and a compilation that restates classic Hooj / Lost Language DNA. Home listening stays in motion — ensemble ambient, a 2015 acid-lounge remaster, and an older machine-house record that still plays like a late-night leave-on.

## Maara — Revenge from the Penthouse EP — *Maara* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0641802891_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Trance, techno, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://maara.bandcamp.com/album/revenge-from-the-penthouse-ep?from=embed"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mLAILIoPz5QaKkzZFuc-Fqhv-NNyXe8Zk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Fast, feverish club music with proggy and psychoactive charge. Resident Advisor frames it as a blend of trance, techno and electro; the Bandcamp page leans on “fast and relentless club sounds.” It sits next to the Morphena & Narciss / Bliss Inc. / UFO Space lane without repeating the recent briefing list — a listen with a temperature, not a pack of tools.

**Dig:** If the feverish trance-techno-electro mix is the hook, stay with [*Revenge from the Penthouse EP*](https://maara.bandcamp.com/album/revenge-from-the-penthouse-ep?from=embed) and play it through.

## Stephen Vitiello, Brendan Canty, Hahn Rowe — Second — *Balmat* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3564238632_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, experimental, dub, kraut-adjacent · **Listen:** <a href="https://balmat.bandcamp.com/album/second"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kk8qQ_lSV02Z0IeJL8Hod-WYR0eOBxBBs"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Slow-opening, artful ambient with ensemble interplay and a subtle groove rather than static drift. It has an art-music / sound-design pull that still leaves room for pulse — compatible with Roméo Poirier, Leif, and West Mineral-adjacent curiosity without assigning homework.

**Dig:** If the slow-opening ensemble ambient is the draw, stay with [*Second*](https://balmat.bandcamp.com/album/second) and leave it running.

## Hypnotised — Hypnotised presents Progressive Trance — *Hypnotised* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2330908180_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive trance · **Listen:** <a href="https://hypnotised.bandcamp.com/album/hypnotised-presents-progressive-trance"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mYO_yfthxpp6k3l_n5B4WVNb5Xkywb3is"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

A 2026 compilation built around Hooj Choons / Lost Language / Hope Recordings lineage — aged DNA in fresh packaging. That is on-brief for a crate that still plays Three Drives, Café Del Mar, Oolong Trance, and Sungod. It is more curatorial than a singular-artist LP, but the through-line is the progressive-trance shape you actually leave on.

**Dig:** If the classic-progressive DNA is the point, keep going through [*Hypnotised presents Progressive Trance*](https://hypnotised.bandcamp.com/album/hypnotised-presents-progressive-trance).

## Milieu — Enjoy Your Stay At The Holograph Lounge [10th Anniversary Remaster] — *Milieu Music* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3715945680_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Acid house, ambient techno, braindance, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://milieumusic.bandcamp.com/album/enjoy-your-stay-at-the-holograph-lounge-10th-anniversary-remaster"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_m_BK2QYWUccfpnXoZTPyn1xlgYEYAThFM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

A sleeper catalogue listen that links ambient home listening to acid, electro, and techno roots without turning into a canon primer. The 10th-anniversary remaster is the aged-well pick this week: personal, replayable, and useful on both the sofa and the crate's electro/ambient edge.

**Dig:** If the acid-lounge / ambient-techno crossover lands, stay with [*Enjoy Your Stay At The Holograph Lounge*](https://milieumusic.bandcamp.com/album/enjoy-your-stay-at-the-holograph-lounge-10th-anniversary-remaster) and leave the remaster running.

**Genre:** Trance, progressive house · **Listen:** <a href="https://cosmicgate.bandcamp.com/album/perspectives"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lxE36thSNSmskFLk2IPSA_sFgSjhrz3dI"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>
