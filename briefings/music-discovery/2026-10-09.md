# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-09

This week sits with the Terekke / Khotin weather — Loidis’s understated Incienso album and Priori’s dream-framed naff record — then steps back onto the floor with Cromby’s own Potency EP and Kassian’s trance treatments. Home listening is collaborative and archival: Natural Information Society with Bitchin Bajas, and O Yuki Conjugate’s film soundtrack.

## Loidis — One Day — *Incienso* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1324216119_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Microhouse, dub techno, minimal house · **Listen:** <a href="https://huercosplonk.bandcamp.com/album/one-day/"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lgk5dTak5MNwFOkueKeacevIcR9ucwLYc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Brian Leeds, better known as Huerco S., stretches the Loidis alias into a full-length on Incienso. Pitchfork treats it as dancefloor music made of small consequential changes in groove and detail — an album-scale counterweight to more declarative rave records, and a listen you’d actually leave running.

**Dig:** Preview the next Loidis album, [*From The Toolkit Of Lovers*](https://huercosplonk.bandcamp.com/album/from-the-toolkit-of-lovers?label=682504772&tab=music), due later this month.

## Priori — This but More — *naff recordings* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2268923792_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient electronics, dub, trip-hop, IDM · **Listen:** <a href="https://prioricamp.bandcamp.com/album/this-but-more"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mW37fxO1PYEp7TyJev2iFvGaUc9ax6iNA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Montréal producer Francis Latreille works as Priori and co-runs naff with Ex-Terrestrial. Bandcamp Daily places this nature-and-dreams album in his move from Jump Source’s grooves toward reflective projects; guests, stories, and illustrations make it a broader home-listening world rather than a beat tape.

**Dig:** Browse [naff’s catalogue](https://naffrecordings.bandcamp.com/music) for Priori’s surrounding collaborations and labelmates.

## Cromby — Love On Tenderhooks — *Potency* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0714213777_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, deep house, trippy tech house · **Listen:** <a href="https://cromby.bandcamp.com/album/cromby-love-on-tenderhooks"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nB_id4nL2TAbgcnisbSrR5mthoVVg69X0"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Belfast-born, Berlin-based Cromby already sits in recent Rekordbox adds; Potency is his own imprint for house, techno, and acid, and this is its ninth release. The EP moves between inward textures, deep grooves, and hands-up moments — an artist-led record with a through-line, not a pack of interchangeable tools.

**Dig:** Play [Potency 001](https://cromby.bandcamp.com/album/potency-001) for the house-and-acid start of the imprint.

## Natural Information Society and Bitchin Bajas — Totality — *Drag City* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2364880435_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ecstatic minimalism, spiritual jazz, drone, kosmische · **Listen:** <a href="https://bitchinbajas.bandcamp.com/album/totality?from=footer-aotd-a3448734962"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nJeez0QWI-9CVOos3YHDuQR7hSaYDW4kc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Joshua Abrams’s Natural Information Society and Bitchin Bajas are two Chicago ensembles; their earlier collaboration also landed on Drag City, the long-running Chicago indie. Bandcamp Daily notes stable rhythm under freely developing colour — guimbri, harmonium, bass clarinet, flute, and electronics — a convergence with a material identity, not a jam collage.

**Dig:** Next play [*Tones / Zones*](https://bitchinbajas.bandcamp.com/album/tones-zones-3) for an earlier view of Bitchin Bajas’ tape-and-synth practice.

## Alex Kassian — Voices — *Test Pressing Recordings* (2023)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2922847944_10.jpg)

**Genre:** New-age trance, progressive house, Balearic electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://alexkassian2.bandcamp.com/album/voices"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mJyOrz0K0CQGFQlAs89p9l5waS9jynVh0"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Berlin producer Alex Kassian already sits in this crate via *Oolong Trance*; *Voices* is TPR001, the opening release on Test Pressing Recordings. Three treatments of the central song, with Thibaut Knapp’s vocals, sit next to “Lifestream,” giving one melodic idea several emotional angles rather than a single peak-time arrangement.

**Dig:** Next play Kassian’s [*E2–E4*](https://alexkassian2.bandcamp.com/album/e2-e4-a-reference-to-e2-e4-by-manuel-g-ttsching-by-alex-kassian-x-mad-professor) reinterpretation with Mad Professor.

## O Yuki Conjugate — Sleepwalker — *auf abwegen* (2019)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1382184629_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Postindustrial ambient, fourth-world electronics, electroacoustic · **Listen:** <a href="https://aufabwegen.bandcamp.com/album/sleepwalker"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kK1YhLY5UaVbQ8T7UpQ-0h48wLImbbojM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Formed in Nottingham in 1982, O Yuki Conjugate is Andrew Hulme and Roger Horberry’s long ambient practice, grown out of post-punk and industrial. German experimental imprint auf abwegen issued *Sleepwalker* as a soundtrack to Hulme’s film: ten pieces from performances across nine countries, with a Keeley Forsyth contribution. Historical depth without a canon-primer homework assignment.

**Dig:** Browse [O Yuki Conjugate’s artist catalogue](https://oyukiconjugate.bandcamp.com/music) for the wider history around *Sleepwalker*.
