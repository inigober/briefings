# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-02

This week stays close to the crate’s recent floor: Maara on Spray’s Punctuality, Dj Babatr’s raptor-house archive, and Eoin DJ going more techno on Berlin’s A7A. Home listening is authored rather than wallpaper — Nala Sinephro’s Warp suite, Meridian Brothers’ tropical character study, and mu tate’s diary-like songs.

## Maara — Drama On — *Punctuality* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1247952363_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic progressive house, trance-informed house · **Listen:** <a href="https://punctuality.bandcamp.com/album/drama-on"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_m4ZEePwsvzfKi3twJSykzeDpuIUQdvasQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Montreal producer Maara already sits in this crate via Kalahari Oyster Cult and Step Ball Chain; *Drama On* is her on Spray’s Punctuality imprint, a recent Rekordbox label rather than a familiar-folder dump. The five-track EP gives progressive and psychedelic house a mischievous, sapphic dancefloor identity — a listen with a temperature, not a pack of interchangeable tools.

## Nala Sinephro — Endlessness — *Warp Records* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3962253974_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient jazz, spiritual jazz, modular electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://nalasinephro.bandcamp.com/album/endlessness"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_ktVJqWV32-rmct0pB-aCF6vu4lk85lWI0"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Composer, harpist and synthesist Nala Sinephro works the overlap of London jazz and electronic composition. *Endlessness* is on Warp; Bandcamp Daily’s Album of the Day notes that the guest musicians serve a unified suite rather than individual display. Ten movements transform a recurring arpeggio across instruments — a long-form listen you’d actually leave playing.

**Dig:** Next play Sinephro’s debut [*Space 1.8*](https://nalasinephro.bandcamp.com/album/space-18?t=1).

## Dj Babatr — Rise of the Raptor — *International Chrome* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1560205095_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Raptor house, Latin rave, techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://djbabatr.bandcamp.com/album/rise-of-the-raptor-2"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kdasjcVtm5jMoE45Tv-3AmgBgCdmZYDQk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Caracas producer Dj Babatr is the founder of raptor house, and recent Rekordbox adds already treat that street-level Venezuelan dance culture as a live lane. International Chrome hosts this archival set: To-K’s first vinyl appearance, three previously unreleased vault selections, and an Amor Satyr remix. It is a document of a movement, not a generic Latin-rave tool pack.

## Meridian Brothers — Mi Latinoamérica Sufre — *Ansonia Records / Bongo Joe* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0822254130_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic tropical music, cumbia · **Listen:** <a href="https://meridianbrothers.bandcamp.com/album/mi-latinoam-rica-sufre-2"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_ksB0qc7VH6DJpIgH6sLQ1u2NuqhTC5mek"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Bogotá’s Meridian Brothers is Eblis Álvarez’s project; he plays the album’s instrumental parts himself. This joint Ansonia Records / Bongo Joe release puts tropical Latin forms next to highlife and soukous guitar traditions in a humorous character study of Junior Maximiliano the Third. It rhymes with the Os Tincoãs / Jorge Ben home lane without assigning a canon primer.

**Dig:** Next play [*Desesperanza*](https://meridianbrothers.bandcamp.com/album/desesperanza) for Meridian Brothers’ earlier surreal reconstruction of salsa and tropical music.

## Eoin DJ — Second Sky (A7AD001) — *A7A* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3084395718_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Cosmic techno, trance, atmospheric progressive electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://systema7a.bandcamp.com/album/second-sky-a7ad001"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kjs1dElBmKbmiR6l8isF3VcZQmzqe9IO4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Eoin DJ is already a crate regular (and a recent Rekordbox add); *Second Sky* takes a more techno-focused direction on Berlin imprint A7A, away from the obvious Punctuality connection. The four-track release links cosmic trance and rhythmic momentum through a conceptual frame of magic realism and natural mysticism — a world attached to the floor, not a pack of cosmic tools.

**Dig:** Hear Eoin DJ on the first [9FINITY compilation](https://9finity.bandcamp.com/album/9finity001-v-a-eoin-dj-dj-life-luca-attanasio-coffintexts-e-talking).

## mu tate — life of mu — *OST* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3010680292_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient electronic songwriting, dub, downtempo · **Listen:** <a href="https://mu-tate.bandcamp.com/album/life-of-mu"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_k-Y4y7JrnIpOpH8m_MPbUJyVsMtO9BkCM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

mu tate moves from spectral ambient electronics toward defined song forms on OST, an unfamiliar imprint rather than a home-listening catalogue filler. Skittering drums, loping bass, and stories narrated by friends create a diary-like frame, with Ben Bondy contributing vocals and guitar to *memory of a memory*. It sits with Terekke / Slowfoam weather while still sounding like songs, not drift.

**Dig:** Next play [*they’re with you always*](https://mu-tate.bandcamp.com/album/theyre-with-you-always), with connections to Space Afrika and Nikolay Kozlov.
