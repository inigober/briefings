# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-23

This week runs from Leeds progressive techno and Detroit acid to Brazilian songwriting and pastoral electronics. Most of it rewards an uninterrupted album side.

## Adam Pits — Synthetic Serenity — *On Rotation* (2023)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1220428208_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive techno; trance; breaks; ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://onrotation.bandcamp.com/album/synthetic-serenity"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nySpy91QtbCWbobfz4FtxKGaXNMbWgJF8"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Adam Pits runs the Leeds label, event series and mix platform On Rotation with Chris I'Anson and Lisene, and this is his second album for the imprint. It is built around a digital environment as a setting, and he stretches his progressive vocabulary through techno and ambient passages that feel like moving between rooms of the same place.

**Dig:** Browse the rest of the [On Rotation catalogue](https://onrotation.bandcamp.com/) for the wider Leeds scene around it.

## Guy Contact — Drinking From The Mirage — *Butter Sessions* (2021)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3938212491_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Acid techno; braindance; progressive electronics; downtempo · **Listen:** <a href="https://buttersessions.bandcamp.com/album/drinking-from-the-mirage"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kuGpZ__-DlihaWg7Ud6O6fTAr9QI6OhXg"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Butter Sessions is the Melbourne/Naarm label run by Sleep D, and Perth producer Guy Contact made his debut long-player there. He wrote much of it in bedroom sessions after leaving full-time work, and the desert-road idea shows in the pacing: acid lines, NORI's vocals and slower, trance-like stretches that drift into one another.

**Dig:** Pretty Girl's [fabric presents](https://pgprettygirl.bandcamp.com/album/fabric-presents-pretty-girl) puts the NORI collaboration in a broader melodic club setting.

## Brian Kage — 909 Days — *Michigander Music* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3261451975_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Detroit house; melodic techno; acid · **Listen:** <a href="https://briankage.bandcamp.com/album/909-days"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kfjDQZEMHpQKc0iocEdo1LOcPFLEs9jBU"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Detroit producer, DJ and mastering engineer Brian Kage runs Michigander Music, the imprint that lists this EP in its catalogue. His original leans on Detroit strings and piano, and Type-303 and Josh Wink then take it into sharper acid territory with their reinterpretations.

**Dig:** Go further into Kage's album-length Detroit sound with [Timeless Times](https://briankage.bandcamp.com/album/timeless-times).

## Dialect — Atlas of Green — *RVNG Intl.* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3344060380_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electroacoustic ambient; pastoral electronics; experimental · **Listen:** <a href="https://dialect-trax.bandcamp.com/album/atlas-of-green"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lm0CB1gUIa9HWThVoTEj_yDwFH-qQBUsc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Andrew PM Hunt, a Liverpool musician and composer, records as Dialect, and RVNG Intl. is the New York imprint that gives his speculative world-building room to unfold. The album follows an imagined protagonist called Green through technological remnants and delicate instrumental fragments, and Bandcamp Daily singled out its warmth and focused compositions.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily's [October 2024 ambient column](https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-ambient/the-best-ambient-music-on-bandcamp-october-2024) lists neighbouring records in the same textured vein.

## Sessa — Pequena Vertigem de Amor — *Mexican Summer* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0624859805_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Brazilian songcraft; psychedelic soul; MPB · **Listen:** <a href="https://sessa.bandcamp.com/album/pequena-vertigem-de-amor?from=footer-bcw-a568392814"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_m4vkAqhwM_KZjCx8CQ5cce8C-O--k-93s"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Sergio Sayeg, a São Paulo musician, records as Sessa, and this is his third album for Mexican Summer, the Brooklyn-founded label with a long run of psychedelic and left-field releases. Becoming a father changed his outlook, and the arrangements widen to match: electric piano, synthesis, wah-wah guitar and primitive drum machines join the guitar that carried his earlier records.

**Dig:** Start from the beginning with [Grandeza](https://sessa.bandcamp.com/album/grandeza-2?from=discover_page), where the songwriting is more stripped back.

## Ana Frango Elétrico — Me Chama De Gato Que Eu Sou Sua — *Mr Bongo* (2023)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2081112197_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Brazilian boogie; MPB; funk-pop · **Listen:** <a href="https://anafrangoeletrico.bandcamp.com/album/me-chama-de-gato-que-eu-sou-sua"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nNb0E3KwM85gZjv3rUWSU-sNIB1MKGjlI"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Mr Bongo is the London label best known for digging up Brazilian and Latin records, which makes it a fitting home for Rio artist Ana Frango Elétrico. Across ten songs she treats Brazilian boogie as living pop, with hooks and groove pulling forward rather than back to the archive.

**Dig:** Pair it with [Sessa's Pequena Vertigem de Amor](https://sessa.bandcamp.com/album/pequena-vertigem-de-amor?from=footer-bcw-a568392814) above for a quieter, more intimate take on contemporary Brazilian songwriting.
