# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-11-13

This week runs from Montreal club psychedelia to a Lisbon-and-London percussion session, with a hardware-ambient classic and a 1995 field-recording record along the way. Two newer house cuts and a Berlin electroclash EP cover the floor.

## Cromby — I’m Coming Back / One More Time — *Life and Death* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3077978598_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Vocal house, progressive house, peak-time house · **Listen:** <a href="https://cromby.bandcamp.com/album/cromby-im-coming-back-one-more-time"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kTNV_kU_7UpQBdUyfx5O4bP8gC_ufAHhg"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Cromby is an Irish producer who has been building a run of club records, and this is his debut on Life and Death, the imprint DJ Tennis runs as a home for melodic, emotionally direct house. The title track is song-first, with NiKi K’s vocal carrying the extended mix. Known Artist’s remix pushes the same material into a later, more hypnotic hour.

## Nídia & Valentina — Estradas — *Latency* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2357321684_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Kuduro-informed electronics, experimental percussion · **Listen:** <a href="https://nidiavalentina.bandcamp.com/album/estradas"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nIAnBS8f1mLR7shj1ILJj60IIo1rqmbuc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Nídia is the Afro-Portuguese producer who came out of Lisbon’s batida scene, and Valentina Magaletti is a drummer and multi-instrumentalist from the London experimental circuit. Latency, the label behind this record, is known for rhythm-led work that sits between club and art-music. Tom Halstead of Raime edited, mixed and produced the album, so programmed beats and live marimba, drums, synth and flute are blended into a single textured space.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily placed the album on its year-end list, and [that entry](https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-of-2024/the-best-albums-of-2024-g-m) adds more on the collaboration.

## Khotin — Hello World — *1080p* (2014)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2634885989_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient house, gentle acid, hardware electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://heart.bandcamp.com/album/hello-world"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mt0h0X0fLz3Eq-PelSibKPtW6uDNx2TdA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Dylan Khotin-Foote is an Edmonton producer who recorded Hello World for the 1080p label; the page you will land on is now hosted under his Khotin Industries banner. He played the sides live on Roland, Korg, Yamaha and Casio hardware, and it sounds like it: soft, slightly wobbly acid and ambient-house grooves that feel like bedroom music with a pulse.

## Ambien Baby — Taste The Bass — *Delicate Records* (2022)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1138869714_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electroclash, EBM, mid-tempo techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://itsdelicaterecords.bandcamp.com/album/taste-the-bass"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_muET8LIVOdCpCTd3t9LrhFDoPDwhquAgY"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Ambien Baby pairs Nap and D. Tiffany, who wrote the record over four days around a shared love of synthesizers. Delicate Records is a Berlin label that leans into dark, vocal-driven electro and EBM. Hints of duet between the two voices run through the machine-driven tracks and give them an unexpectedly intimate edge.

## Maara — Big Whoops — *Ancient Records* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0091185012_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic club music, techno, progressive house · **Listen:** <a href="https://maara.bandcamp.com/album/big-whoops"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l8a0Jh0erPZqF6EV2HmsxlKbBLY_KK1SM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Maara is a Montreal producer, and Big Whoops is the first release on Ancient Records, the label she started herself. The imprint opens with a statement tying its club fantasies to lesbian memory, community and imagined futures. The four tracks are where that idea turns psychedelic.

**Dig:** [The Ancient Truth](https://stepballchain.bandcamp.com/album/the-ancient-truth?t=4) is the album-length world behind these club releases.

## O Yuki Conjugate — Equator (remastered & expanded) — *Aguirre* (2021)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2859825958_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, post-industrial, fourth-world electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://oyukiconjugate.bandcamp.com/album/equator-remastered-expanded"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KrmzjDqsQFQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

O Yuki Conjugate formed in Nottingham in the early 1980s, and Equator comes from their second incarnation, made with co-producer Paul Schütze. It combines performances, samples and location recordings from Vietnam and Morocco. Staalplaat released it in 1995; Aguirre’s 2021 edition adds a substantial amount of extra material.

**Dig:** [New Meridian](https://optimomusic.bandcamp.com/album/new-meridian) brings several Equator-era collaborators back together three decades later.
