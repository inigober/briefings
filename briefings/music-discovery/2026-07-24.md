# Music Discovery Briefing — Week of 2026-07-24

New club 12"s next to records that still hit years later — Love On The Rocks and Klasse Wrecks for the floor, Warp and Roméo Poirier for home, plus Detroit electro that never really left the crate conversation.

## Pancratio — Love Letters — *Love On The Rocks* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1945411167_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Deep house

**Listen:** <a href="https://loveontherocks.bandcamp.com/album/love-letters"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=57_vQIhf4gU"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Rome→Berlin producer long known as Otta Res Lab’s mixer/mastering brain; this is his first spotlight EP for PARAMIDA after a decade shaping other people’s records. Four analog one-takes (*A Never Ending Story*, *Artificial Intelligence*, *I Promise*, *Badass Music*) — house grooves that flip into melancholy pads and break-section drama. LOTR remains Paramida’s Berlin home for emotional, party-forward house.

**Dig:** Follow the circle through [Simone de Kunovich / One Trip to Avyon](https://onetriptotavyon.bandcamp.com/) if the Rome–Berlin thread clicks.

## Elecno Posse — Elecno Posse EP — *Klasse Wrecks* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1672053633_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Electro / acid tekno

**Listen:** <a href="https://klassewrecks.bandcamp.com/album/elecno-posse-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

G.Field, Abraham, and O-Money define “Elecno” across four hard-edged 4/4 acid-tekno cutters on Luca Lozano & Mr. Ho’s Hong Kong imprint. Klasse Wrecks is already a crate label for you (Lozano sits high in Rekordbox artists); this is the 2025 slam side of that catalogue, not a nostalgia reissue.

**Dig:** Browse the wider wreckage via [Klasse Wrecks on Bandcamp](https://klassewrecks.bandcamp.com/).

## Audiojack — Joy EP — *Shall Not Fade* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2173417094_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Deep house / techno

**Listen:** <a href="https://shallnotfade.bandcamp.com/album/joy-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Leeds duo Audiojack return to Bristol’s Shall Not Fade with a two-tracker — title cut *Joy* rides thumping kicks into rolling reece bass and hypnotic 4x4 drive. SNF is in your label folders for a reason: dancefloor-first house/techno without the Berlin-minimal stereotype.

**Dig:** Skim the label’s [recent catalogue](https://shallnotfade.bandcamp.com/) for adjacent UK house/garage energy.

## Drexciya — Journey Of The Deep Sea Dweller I — *Clone Classic Cuts* (2011)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0912212195_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Detroit electro / techno

**Listen:** <a href="https://cloneclassiccuts.bandcamp.com/album/journey-of-the-deep-sea-dweller-i"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=DS11OjP08uc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Remastered early Drexciya (UR orbit, early–mid ’90s material) re-sequenced by Clone so newcomers get a coherent album rather than scattered EPs. Raw aquatic electro that still sounds uncompromising decades on — the kind of “aged well” record your Detroit + electro playlist themes keep pointing at. Not a 2025 drop; a foundation stone. (You already have vols III/IV liked on Spotify — this is the series entry point.)

**Dig:** Continue the series with [Journey Of The Deep Sea Dweller II](https://cloneclassiccuts.bandcamp.com/album/journey-of-the-deep-sea-dweller-ii).

## Nala Sinephro — The Smashing Machine (Original Motion Picture Soundtrack) — *Warp / A24 Music* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3399871707_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient jazz

**Listen:** <a href="https://nalasinephro.bandcamp.com/album/the-smashing-machine-original-motion-picture-soundtrack"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=O5iroWFGfcA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

London harpist/composer after *Space 1.8* and *Endlessness*; first film score (Benny Safdie / A24), eight pieces with Nubya Garcia and James Mollison — cue-length ambient-jazz and kosmische sketches (*Dawn*, *Grand Prix*, the title cue). Sideways into cinema without abandoning her continuum language. Already in your recent Spotify listening.

**Dig:** If you haven’t lived with the studio albums yet, start with [*Endlessness* on Bandcamp](https://nalasinephro.bandcamp.com/album/endlessness).

## Roméo Poirier — Plage Arrière — *self-released* (2016)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3708181767_5.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient / exotica

**Listen:** <a href="https://romeopoirier.bandcamp.com/album/plage-arri-re"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=uRE7FA21rKI"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Brussels composer/collagist; the cult beach-exotica record that made his name before *Hotel Nota* (which you already have saved on Spotify). Granular processing, Balearic haze, Hassell-adjacent brass — aged well as a home-listening postcard rather than a floor weapon. He’s in your recent Spotify artists; this is the earlier chapter.

**Dig:** Then contrast with [*Hotel Nota* on sferic](https://romeopoirier.bandcamp.com/album/hotel-nota) if you want the later, more polished sibling.
