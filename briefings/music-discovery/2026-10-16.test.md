# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-16

This week runs from Leeds prog-trance and Melbourne psy-prog to water-and-wood ambient and a dub collage from Scotland. A couple of older records that have only gotten better sit alongside the new ones.

## Mabel — Pleasure Phonetix — *Step Ball Chain* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3895064552_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psy-prog, techno, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://stepballchain.bandcamp.com/album/pleasure-phonetix?from=discover_page"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_myznuQEDZ1Lf8nK1HNYCd31NRhwuKU3bM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Mabel Hutterer is a producer from Naarm (Melbourne) who lands here on Step Ball Chain, a Berlin imprint with an off-centre take on contemporary dance music. Five tracks move between psychedelic bass, sparse electro and driving psy-prog, and theatrical vocal touches give the EP a personality you can pick out in a set.

**Dig:** Hear the label's softer, song-led side on Peace Of Dream's [Past](https://stepballchain.bandcamp.com/album/past).

## Lisene — Science Friction — *On Rotation* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0324032264_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic progressive house, trance, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://onrotation.bandcamp.com/album/science-friction"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mdZ74mLM4ALM5MhUxXjzLfJO8DeS-MWIA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Lisene co-founded On Rotation in Leeds with Chris I'Anson and Adam Pits, and he's a resident there too, so this debut album reads like years of club nights boiled down. The label is the kind of small, DJ-run outfit where the music comes straight from the dancefloor. Bass-heavy cuts and electro pieces sit next to cinematic stretches, and the psychedelic prog-trance thread keeps pulling the sequence forward from one track to the next.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily's [progressive house album guide](https://daily.bandcamp.com/lists/prog-house-album-guide) maps out the wider revival this record belongs to.

## Sleep D — Rebel Force — *Incienso* (2019)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1839487583_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic house, dub, techno, ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://sleepdincienso.bandcamp.com/album/rebel-force"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nn6EPzGJ8sslSxYwd6WR5ne3H9U7gtYTk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Melbourne duo Maryos Syawish and Corey Kikos, the pair behind Butter Sessions, put their debut album out on New York's Incienso rather than on their own imprint. Incienso gives it room to stretch: the record opens with desert-like chug and rave intensity, then eases into a gentler landing that includes a collaboration with Kuniyuki. It plays well loud at the start and quietly at the end.

**Dig:** Hear how they write albums on [Electronic Arts](https://sleep-d.bandcamp.com/album/electronic-arts), the Butter Sessions record.

## Dip Friso — Dip Friso — *Real Landscape* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3546631443_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Dubwise experimental pop, collage, guitar electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://reallandscape.bandcamp.com/album/dip-friso"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l8s_t_zS3zx4rRq7XLIfd9gO41dB3EooQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Dip Friso is Scottish artist Murray Collier's long-running project, and Real Landscape is the imprint he built around it. Earlier records leaned on samples; here live instruments and guest voices step in, bringing guitar textures, avant-jazz interruptions and a cover of Systematics into a hazy dub frame. It's a strange, warm album that keeps shifting under you.

**Dig:** Follow the project forward with [Poor Image](https://reallandscape.bandcamp.com/album/poor-image), released on Real Landscape in April 2026.

## Suso Sáiz — Distorted Clamor — *Music From Memory* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2303217647_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electroacoustic ambient, drone, transformed acoustic sound · **Listen:** <a href="https://susosaiz.bandcamp.com/album/distorted-clamor"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_msGiVCGQHAsvrEujnnryeYCE6EAZs5LI4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Spanish composer Suso Sáiz has been making music for more than four decades, and this is his eighth release with Music From Memory, the Amsterdam label known for digging up and championing overlooked ambient, balearic and experimental work. Sáiz built the album from water, wood and metal rather than synthesizers, bending their recorded sounds until beauty and distortion blur together. The subject is threatened natural environments, and you can hear it in the way the sounds seem to erode as they play.

## Huerta — Junipero [VYG13] — *Voyage* (2020)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3658246124_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient house, downtempo, melodic breakbeat · **Listen:** <a href="https://huertamusic.bandcamp.com/album/junipero-vyg13"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nUg8bRFDepI8dpNANlOd-aavgOD_xaaJA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Steve Huerta grew up in Los Angeles and now lives in Berlin, and his debut album draws on the Californian landscapes of his childhood. Voyage, the label behind it, favours immersive, album-length listening, and the vinyl edition even ships with a feature-length animation. Recorded in 2019, it moves in one continuous arc through ambient textures, field sounds and gentle house beats.
