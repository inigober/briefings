# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-16

This week’s floor stays in the psychedelic-prog lane: Lisene’s On Rotation debut, Mabel on Berlin’s Step Ball Chain, and Sleep D’s shaped Incienso album. Home listening stays specific rather than drift — Suso Sáiz transforming acoustic rooms for Music From Memory, Huerta’s Voyage journey, and Dip Friso’s collaged pop.

## Lisene — Science Friction — *On Rotation* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0324032264_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic progressive house, trance, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://onrotation.bandcamp.com/album/science-friction"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mdZ74mLM4ALM5MhUxXjzLfJO8DeS-MWIA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Lisene co-founded Leeds imprint On Rotation with Chris I’Anson and Adam Pits, and this is his debut album: years of psychedelic prog-trance condensed into bass-heavy club tracks, electro, and cinematic passages rather than a pile of interchangeable tools.

**Dig:** Then read [Bandcamp Daily’s guide to contemporary progressive house](https://daily.bandcamp.com/lists/prog-house-album-guide).

## Suso Sáiz — Distorted Clamor — *Music From Memory* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2303217647_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electroacoustic ambient, drone, transformed acoustic sound · **Listen:** <a href="https://susosaiz.bandcamp.com/album/distorted-clamor"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_msGiVCGQHAsvrEujnnryeYCE6EAZs5LI4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Spanish composer Suso Sáiz has more than four decades of work; *Distorted Clamor* is his eighth release with Music From Memory. It addresses threatened living environments by transforming acoustic sources with water, wood, and metal rather than synthesizers — process you can hear, not ambient-by-default.

## Mabel — Pleasure Phonetix — *Step Ball Chain* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3895064552_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psy-prog, techno, electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://stepballchain.bandcamp.com/album/pleasure-phonetix?from=discover_page"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_myznuQEDZ1Lf8nK1HNYCd31NRhwuKU3bM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Naarm producer Mabel Hutterer lands on Berlin imprint Step Ball Chain, whose remit is off-centre contemporary dance music. The five-track EP combines psychedelic bass, electro sparseness, driving psy-prog, and theatrical vocal touches — character you’d hunt for a set, not a pack of interchangeable tools.

**Dig:** Next play Peace Of Dream’s [*Past*](https://stepballchain.bandcamp.com/album/past) for Step Ball Chain’s softer, song-led side.

## Huerta — Junipero [VYG13] — *Voyage* (2020)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3658246124_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient house, downtempo, melodic breakbeat · **Listen:** <a href="https://huertamusic.bandcamp.com/album/junipero-vyg13"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nUg8bRFDepI8dpNANlOd-aavgOD_xaaJA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Los Angeles-born, Berlin-based Steve Huerta released his debut album through Voyage. Recorded in 2019, it draws on Californian childhood landscapes for a continuous pass through ambient textures, natural sound, and gentle house; Voyage’s vinyl edition even ships with a feature-length animation, which tells you how the imprint wants this listened to.

## Sleep D — Rebel Force — *Incienso* (2019)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1839487583_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic house, dub, techno, ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://sleepdincienso.bandcamp.com/album/rebel-force"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nn6EPzGJ8sslSxYwd6WR5ne3H9U7gtYTk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Melbourne duo Maryos Syawish and Corey Kikos (Butter Sessions) put their debut album on New York’s Incienso instead of their own imprint. It moves from desert-like chug and rave intensity toward a gentler landing, including a Kuniyuki collaboration — an album with an arc, not a pack of psychedelic tools.

**Dig:** Continue with [*Electronic Arts*](https://sleep-d.bandcamp.com/album/electronic-arts) to hear how Sleep D developed their album writing on Butter Sessions.

## Dip Friso — Dip Friso — *Real Landscape* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3546631443_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Dubwise experimental pop, collage, guitar electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://reallandscape.bandcamp.com/album/dip-friso"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l8s_t_zS3zx4rRq7XLIfd9gO41dB3EooQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Dip Friso is Scottish artist Murray Collier’s longstanding project and the centre of his Real Landscape imprint. This self-titled album pulls more live instrumentation and guest voices into a previously sample-heavy practice — avant-jazz disruption, guitar, and a Systematics cover inside a hazy dub frame.

**Dig:** Next play [*Poor Image*](https://reallandscape.bandcamp.com/album/poor-image), the project’s April 2026 release on Real Landscape.
