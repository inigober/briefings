# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-09-11

This week follows the Australian prog-house revival Bandcamp Daily mapped — Butter Sessions as a node, tape-hazed floor records with a world attached — then swings home into hydrophone ambient, beyond-balearic drift, and a library-music sleeper. The floor half is leftfield and slightly mystical rather than peak-time utility; the home half stays sunlit and specific.

## Yuzo Iwata — Kaizu — *Butter Sessions* (2022)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0523117344_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, techno, balearic, leftfield · **Listen:** <a href="https://buttersessions.bandcamp.com/album/kaizu"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nfMqORq4jk3RvEPOZsIx-0kclZ4oq9Pk0"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

A 2022 leftfield dance LP sitting between balearic drift, Japanese techno eccentricity, and sly floor utility — unusual percussion, environmental textures, and a rhythmic range that still feels like a listen rather than a tool pack. Bandcamp Daily’s 2024 prog-house comeback guide frames Butter Sessions as a key node in that revival; this is the aged-well record from that ecosystem you’d actually leave playing.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily’s 2024 [prog-house comeback guide](https://daily.bandcamp.com/lists/prog-house-album-guide) frames Butter Sessions as a key node in the modern revival ecosystem.

## Mara — At Every Corner — *Pure Space* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0853317611_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, experimental, electroacoustic · **Listen:** <a href="https://maramaramara.bandcamp.com/album/at-every-corner"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kFTUPty5gJj7hCZg7VHjyJDt2cfRzpbBc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Home Listening Series framing, hydrophone and live-recording detail, and an experimental-community angle that points to an immersive listen rather than background drift. It sits with the Roméo Poirier / Leif / ambient-playlist side of the profile — field-informed and patient, not spa wallpaper.

**Dig:** If the hydrophone and live-recording detail is the hook, stay with [*At Every Corner*](https://maramaramara.bandcamp.com/album/at-every-corner) and treat it as a full listen.

## Fader Cap — s2i02 - Fader Cap [World One] — *s2i Recordings* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a4098963393_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, trancey techno, leftfield club · **Listen:** <a href="https://s2irecordings.bandcamp.com/album/s2i02-fader-cap-world-one"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Tape-hazed, arpeggiated, propulsion-heavy club material from the same Australian prog-house continuum Bandcamp Daily singled out. Short-form, but the salvage-process / hybrid-set-from-mystery-tapes framing on the Bandcamp page gives it a through-line that generic DJ ammo doesn’t have — the recent-lane floor pick that still clears the captivation bar.

**Dig:** Connected to the Butter Sessions / Australian prog-house continuum highlighted by Bandcamp Daily — stay with [*s2i02 - Fader Cap [World One]*](https://s2irecordings.bandcamp.com/album/s2i02-fader-cap-world-one) rather than hunting a single tool track.

**Genre:** Progressive trance, psy-tinged house, techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://activemeditationmusic.bandcamp.com/album/self-realization"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lWY8aD_CDHTx7aKJYfByAlKPa8Ks0uuys"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

**Genre:** Balearic, library, ambient, Mediterranean electronic · **Listen:** <a href="https://balearicmusiclibrary.bandcamp.com/album/bml-vol-1"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>
