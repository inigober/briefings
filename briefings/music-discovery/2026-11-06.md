# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-11-06

This week runs from raptor house out of Caracas and modular tech house to Brazilian guitar and West Coast ambient techno. Most of it works on a dancefloor or on a quiet evening, and a few records manage both.

## Boulderhead — Dancing Rain — *TSOL / Limousine Dream* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3935366004_10.jpg)

**Genre:** House, modular tech house, psychedelic house · **Listen:** <a href="https://limousinedream.bandcamp.com/album/dancing-rain"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l1BodF8Bv2Kw3UsnqnfcMffd0X2thP-a4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Boulderhead lands on TSOL through the Limousine Dream storefront run by Gene On Earth, and the record sounds like a modular rig being played as an instrument: arrangements keep shifting, and the title track carries a vocal that stays in front. Two editions exist. "Throughout" is vinyl-only and "Rounded" is digital-only, so the version you hear depends on where you buy it.

**Dig:** Boulderhead's [MINDHELMET 18](https://mindhelmet.bandcamp.com/album/mindhelmet-18) is a six-track look at the same dancefloor vocabulary.

## Dj Babatr — Pedro Le Dino — *Couvre x Chefs* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0744638937_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Raptor house, tribal club, techno, trance · **Listen:** <a href="https://djbabatr.bandcamp.com/album/pedro-le-dino"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_k32qwpSbtgUDS8dnzZ-lskDBANZr_iyT4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Dj Babatr is a Caracas raptor-house pioneer, and the French label Couvre x Chefs connected with him after a 2023 Paris performance. Four compositions from 2004 get their first official release here, alongside contemporary remixes. The result ties Venezuelan club repertoire from two decades ago to a French network that is listening now.

**Dig:** [Flow](https://djbabatr.bandcamp.com/album/flow) is a separate 2026 set of Babatr originals plus an Awesome 3 reinterpretation.

## not even noticed — Eteus — *Fast At Work* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3663782634_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Psychedelic tech house, acid, breaks · **Listen:** <a href="https://fastatwork.bandcamp.com/album/eteus"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kYfgDe1ZnwPlym0gSiPcyurvYduZA8hS4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Carsten Fluck and Lucas Lejeune record as not even noticed, and here they bring the project to Fast At Work, a Los Angeles imprint. The originals are bass-led with spiralling acid lines, and the two remixes take them in different directions: K-LONE on one side and Maara on the other. The EP rewards playing all the way through.

**Dig:** Follow remixer Maara into [Ultimate Reward](https://maara.bandcamp.com/album/ultimate-reward), an earlier release associated with naff.

## Fabiano do Nascimento — Das Nuvens — *Leaving Records* (2023)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3876271804_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Brazilian instrumental music, ambient jazz, acoustic/electronic · **Listen:** <a href="https://fabianodonascimento.bandcamp.com/album/das-nuvens"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mj_gCiSIl_RxJGWGEWsdf3kSriBzY5a2g"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Fabiano do Nascimento grew up in Rio and now lives in Los Angeles, where he pushes Brazilian guitar traditions toward electronics and experimental composition; Leaving Records is the home for that work. He made this one with Daniel Santiago, and the pair layer several kinds of guitar with upright bass and electronics. The rhythm stays understated and a lot of room is left around each phrase.

**Dig:** [Solstice Concert](https://fabianodonascimento.bandcamp.com/album/solstice-concert) plays several related compositions in a different performance setting.

## Spekki Webu — Bootstrap Paradox LP — *Outer Orbit Records* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0591349319_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Illbient, downtempo, leftfield techno, ambient, breakbeat · **Listen:** <a href="https://outerorbitrecords.bandcamp.com/album/bootstrap-paradox-lp"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Spekki Webu is a Dutch producer best known for intense dancefloor work, and this album takes the opposite tack. It is his first release for Outer Orbit, a new New York label that shares a connection with the Mizz Softee party. He revisits the music that formed him: illbient, trip-hop, lighter techno and ambient. Cari Lekebusch contributes a remix on the vinyl edition only, and four digital bonus tracks come with the full-album purchase.

**Dig:** [Ethereo Spatial Rift](https://h-productions.bandcamp.com/album/ethereo-spatial-rift-hpx176) is a separate collaboration between Spekki Webu and Cari Lekebusch.

## Velocette — Discotheque Saudades — *A Colourful Storm* (2020)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2918405277_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient techno, melodic IDM, cosmic electronics · **Listen:** <a href="https://acolourfulstorm.bandcamp.com/album/discotheque-saudades"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mxDzav3pyt_AKEaOGqPHInD_UCHi6FAUc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Jason Williams makes music as Velocette, and his sound took shape around Jonah Sharp's Reflective label and Sharp's own Parallel imprint. Moopie compiled this set for A Colourful Storm to revisit that West Coast meeting point of Detroit-informed rhythm, melodic electronics and kosmische imagination. Sharp's own recollections supply the history, so the record plays as music and as an oral-history document.

**Dig:** [Headtravel](https://organised-noise.bandcamp.com/album/headtravel) is a Pacific Collective compilation that also includes Velocette.
