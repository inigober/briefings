# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-09-25

This week is artist-led rather than imprint-led: SHERELLE writing her own rave record, Warning’s Berlin club sampler, and a community prog-house compilation for the crate’s current favourite genre. Home listening stays hybrid — rRoxymore’s house-to-ambient album, GiGi FM’s ritual electronics, and Logic1000’s DJ-Kicks mix.

## SHERELLE — With a Vengeance — *SHERELLE* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2441738820_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Rave, club · **Listen:** <a href="https://sherelle.bandcamp.com/album/with-a-vengeance"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_ltsWkoqVIvJDWkR1M9YXPEWkTUwE5m71A"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

SHERELLE is a UK DJ and scene-builder whose own record carries extra weight because it comes from someone who already shapes nights. It sits on her artist Bandcamp, and Bandcamp Daily’s April 2025 best-electronic list singled it out. The point is identity beyond high-BPM utility — a first-person rave statement you’d actually leave playing.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily’s [April 2025 best electronic](https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-electronic/the-best-electronic-music-on-bandcamp-april-2025) is the write-up that placed this record.

## rRoxymore — Juggling Dualities — *rRoxymore* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3404841378_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Deep house, techno, ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://rroxymore.bandcamp.com/album/juggling-dualities"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_me52ihdpe5lpt8jJRwfmFYYZB75lyDggk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

rRoxymore works the overlap of house, techno, ambient, and new-age texture — a bridge for someone who likes both dancefloor systems and Terekke / Area 3 weather. The album is framed by her own artist Bandcamp and by Bandcamp Daily’s July 2025 best-electronic list, which is the reception gate. It plays as an authored long-player rather than a functional 12".

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily’s [July 2025 best electronic](https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-electronic/the-best-electronic-music-on-bandcamp-july-2025) is the list that singled this album out.

## WARIOUS2 — WARIOUS2 — *Warning* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0676472179_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Trance, electro, techno, progressive house · **Listen:** <a href="https://warningberlin.bandcamp.com/album/warious2"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_k97z3aRn-9X3a-zN_2JLD4Re3wgjx_9qA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This various-artists compilation sits on Berlin’s Warning, an imprint at the crossover of trance, electro, breakbeat, techno, and progressive house. It is a label sampler more than a single-artist statement — a map of a contemporary leftfield-club ecosystem rather than one auteur’s thesis. That makes it useful crate-finding material for the same lane as recent UFO Space / Bliss Inc. / OSSX adds.

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1905131647_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Experimental electronic · **Listen:** <a href="https://gigifm.bandcamp.com/album/shelter-our-time"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mr5kNPu0DpUXOu8_Wsm4KBB4VFCJ-X9Hc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

GiGi FM works between DJ culture, experimental electronics, and ritual-minded sound. The release is on her own Bandcamp rather than a generic club-label product line, and Bandcamp Daily’s June 2026 best-electronic list is the write-up that clears the reception gate. It is world-building you put on and stay with, not a mood-board of club-adjacent adjectives.

**Dig:** Bandcamp Daily’s [June 2026 best electronic](https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-electronic/the-best-electronic-music-on-bandcamp-june-2026) is the write-up behind this pick.

## Progressive Alliance — Progressive Alliance: 2026 — *Progressive Alliance* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2879616747_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house · **Listen:** <a href="https://progressivealliance.bandcamp.com/album/progressive-alliance-2026"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mfpZpImtl-imEVDdHr83X7fSapJuDHy3I"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Progressive Alliance is a collective and charitable scene node, not a single auteur or a major-label series. This compilation’s value is as a map of a current progressive-house ecosystem — the genre Rekordbox has been adding most in the last 24 months — through a community-led Bandcamp page. Treat it as a crate document, then peel off the cuts that actually move.

**Dig:** Play [Progressive Alliance: 2025](https://progressivealliance.bandcamp.com/album/progressive-alliance-2025) for a second pass through the collective’s recent prog-house ecosystem.

**Genre:** Downtempo, ambient, house · **Listen:** <a href="https://logic1000.bandcamp.com/album/dj-kicks-logic1000"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_ndIu8xahPpxsZsBDPCPMT4Wxv07LEsOSk"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>
