# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-07-31

Berlin-via-Belfast club heat, Caracas raptor-house archives, and the home-listening lane your Spotify rotation is already pointing at — Domenique Dumont, Area 3, and a Terekke deep cut that still earns its keep.

## Cromby — Mandela Hall EP — *Potency* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2127481174_10.jpg)

**Genre:** House / electro / UK club · **Listen:** <a href="https://cromby.bandcamp.com/album/cromby-mandela-hall-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Belfast-born, Berlin-based Cromby names his Potency return after the Shine-era venue where he first raved and became a resident at 18. Four heads-down swedgers braid house, techno, electro, and UK-club breaks — title track *Mandela Hall* is the kind of rolling, jacking pressure your recent Cromby Rekordbox adds already telegraphed. DJ Mag premiered the lead cut; this is the follow-up to last year's *House Flava* on Big Saldo's Chunkers, back on his own imprint.

**Dig:** Trace the Irish thread through [Cromby's Potency catalogue](https://cromby.bandcamp.com/) — *Love On Tenderhooks* drops the same week if you want the newer face.

## UFO Space — 15 Years Together EP — *SPC Discs* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2693929512_10.jpg)

**Genre:** House / techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://spcdiscs.bandcamp.com/album/15-years-together-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Barcelona→Berlin producer Kevin Mored (UFO Space) follows *Clod Soul* — already in your crate — with a third SPC 12". Side A is pure floor: *Un Paseo* and *Power House* are machine-first club cuts; B-side brings the Jaume Xifré acid collab *Can l'Acid* and the title track, a love letter to fifteen years of marriage that still works in a DJ set. Eris Drew, Octo Octa, and Gerd Janson have all played the label; your recent UFO Space Rekordbox binge makes this the logical next vinyl.

**Dig:** Compare the earlier EP energy on [Clod Soul](https://spcdiscs.bandcamp.com/album/ufo-space-clod-soul-ep) if you want the breakbeat-heavier chapter.

## DJ Babatr — Root Echoes — *Hakuna Kulala* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a4166958996_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Raptor house / tribal techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://hakunakulala.bandcamp.com/album/root-echoes"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Venezuelan pioneer Pedro Elías Corro — founder of raptor house, five recent Rekordbox adds for you — curates ten tracks from a 700-deep personal archive: Caracas miniteca-era material (2003–2007) plus newer cuts, pressed by Kampala's Hakuna Kulala. Galloping percussion, serrated synths, goofy vocal hooks — *Now Shout*, *You I Wanna Bass* — music that travelled bootleg-to-Berghain before the West caught up. Resident Advisor and The Quietus both reviewed the set; Bandcamp Album of the Day on release.

**Dig:** Follow the TraTraTrax/Nick León lineage back to [Pedro Le Dino](https://djbabatr.bandcamp.com/album/pedro-le-dino) on Couvre x Chefs for the France connection.

## Domenique Dumont — Deux Paradis — *Antinote* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2736876091_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Balearic pop / exotica · **Listen:** <a href="https://antinoterecordings.bandcamp.com/album/deux-paradis"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l-PN954t83OnCs8Ek0mrYqY55RurBvoLs"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Latvian-French duo Arturs Liepins and Anete Stuce complete their Antinote trilogy with a ten-song day-cycle: candy-striped dub (*Enchantia*), pixel reggae (*La Vie Va*), twilight synth-pop (*The Order of Invisible Things*), baroque exotica (*Deux Paradis*). You already have *People On Sunday* in YT Music; this is the vocal-forward return after that film score. Heavy in your last-month Spotify rotation alongside Jorge Ben Jor — same Mediterranean longing, different circuitry.

**Dig:** If the trilogy clicks, step back to [*Comme Ça* (2015)](https://antinoterecordings.bandcamp.com/album/comme-a) for the origin of the language.

## Area 3 — View — *Khotin Industries* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0206806557_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient / new age · **Listen:** <a href="https://heart.bandcamp.com/album/view"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Edmonton producer Dylan Khotin-Foote revives his Area 3 alias — literally in your Spotify last-four-weeks chart — for six beat-free sketches recorded mostly in bed. Field recordings (*Forest Science Department*), modular meditations (*Deep Seek*, *Grass Turns To Sponge*), and that languorous new-age haze Inverted Audio compared to his beat-driven Khotin work. *Beautiful You* and *Release Spirit* are already liked; this is the 2025 headphone chapter.

**Dig:** Contrast the alias with [Peace Portal](https://heart.bandcamp.com/album/peace-portal) on the same label — downtempo with beats, same KIND-series warmth.

## Terekke — Plant Age — *L.I.E.S.* (2017)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0364958485_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Lo-fi house / ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://liesrecords.bandcamp.com/album/plant-age"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Amsterdam-via-Brooklyn Matt Gardner's debut album for Ron Morelli's L.I.E.S. — eight tracks of cassette-warm, dub-smudged house that Pitchfork called "comfortable and versatile as your favorite sweater." *BB2* is the pillowy club cut; *Mix 91* is pure ricocheting minimalism. You have *Improvisational Loops* saved; *Plant Age* is the beat-driven sibling that's been in your recent Spotify rotation and still passes the "leave it playing" test years on.

**Dig:** Pair with [*Improvisational Loops*](https://music-from-memory.bandcamp.com/album/improvisational-loops) (Music From Memory) for the yoga-mat ambient side of the same brain.
