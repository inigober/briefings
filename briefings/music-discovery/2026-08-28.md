# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-08-28

This week is about club records with a world attached — prog/trance lift, psychedelic techno, mutant breakbeat — and home listening that still carries pulse and texture rather than dissolving into wallpaper. The through-line is immersion: leave it playing, whether the room is a floor or a sofa.

## Seliga — Lush — *Trance Atlantyk* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3650480114_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, tech house, techno, trance · **Listen:** <a href="https://tranceatlantyk.bandcamp.com/album/lush"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nXBWLRT19mr0annHouMnZ42dobDauacIA"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

The clearest crate match in the pool: progressive house, tech house, techno, and trance on one record, with a dreamy Orbital-ish title track and a more direct club cut in *Tech House 3000*. It sits next to the recent Bliss Inc., Eoin DJ, and UFO Space lane without repeating any of them — euphoric enough to hunt for a set, not generic peak-time filler.

**Dig:** If the dreamy prog/trance crossover is the hook, stay with [*Lush*](https://tranceatlantyk.bandcamp.com/album/lush) and play the title track into *Tech House 3000*.

## Purelink — Faith — *Purelink* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1878222548_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, dub techno, glitch · **Listen:** <a href="https://purelink.bandcamp.com/album/faith"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mVAJ34RkNMsM4wNq7fZD8uKj1kCiASVXo"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Quiet, textural patience with small details surfacing over sustained echo and feedback, plus the occasional restrained four-four pulse. That is the right home-side shape for this profile: ambient listening that still rhymes with dub-techno and minimal club ears, closer to Khotin / Terekke weather than to spa ambience.

**Dig:** If the dubby quiet and slow-bloom detail land, put [*Faith*](https://purelink.bandcamp.com/album/faith) on and leave it running.

## Wata Igarashi — Kaleidoscopic — *Wata Igarashi* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0232629589_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Techno, acid, psychedelic techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://wataigarashi.bandcamp.com/album/kaleidoscopic"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mVRJtkZkZCXFJHZANsM01aBbLs6cAuUTg"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

RA called this a refreshing burst of psychedelic techno: 130–145 BPM floor music that still implies a world beyond the tool-function. That is the trance-adjacent lift the crate has been asking for, with acid and immersion instead of anonymous techno utility.

**Dig:** If the visionary-floor energy clicks, start with [*Kaleidoscopic*](https://wataigarashi.bandcamp.com/album/kaleidoscopic) rather than hunting a single tool track.

## Polygonia — Dream Horizons — *Polygonia* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1262821415_10.jpg)

**Genre:** IDM, ambient, techno, psychedelic electronic · **Listen:** <a href="https://polygonia.bandcamp.com/album/dream-horizons"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lFN8Sk-Kga0sJArpuGJ4r7z4OltnvR1Z4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Longform world-building rather than background drift: mutation, texture, and emotional depth, with traces of IDM, ambient, techno, and bass music. It serves the exploratory home lane without turning into generic ambient wallpaper — the kind of record you put on and actually stay with.

**Dig:** If the longform mutation is the draw, take [*Dream Horizons*](https://polygonia.bandcamp.com/album/dream-horizons) as a full listen rather than a skim.

## Ludwig A.F. — Halo — *Ludwig A.F.* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3135358738_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Breakbeat, techno, ambient, trance-leaning IDM · **Listen:** <a href="https://ludwigaf.bandcamp.com/album/halo"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kSWvnxHQdcmXEVYd1CKSzaVuoAglR2Ubs"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

The playful, future-facing club slot: IDM melody, tech-house swing, trance arpeggios, and breakbeat motion in the same record. That hybrid is more captivating than a straight club tool, and it matches the appetite for electro, trance, and oddball floor records with colour rather than another heads-down 4/4 utility pack.

**Dig:** If the mutant-club colour is the point, play through [*Halo*](https://ludwigaf.bandcamp.com/album/halo) for the trance-arp and breakbeat collision.

## Coti K. — Theros — *Coti K.* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3080655699_10.jpg)

**Genre:** IDM, ambient, Balearic, cosmic downtempo · **Listen:** <a href="https://coti-k.bandcamp.com/album/theros"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kkrVKJUrhvUycW62Xl4uX5wWm0nZNHmes"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

A 2024 outing of late-’90s Mediterranean ambient-IDM that still has a distinct cosmic-downtempo character. It is the aged-well home pick: personality rather than a textbook primer, and a clean rhyme with the Ritmo Fantasía / Balearic-home side of the profile.

**Dig:** If the Mediterranean ambient-IDM mood lands, play [*Theros*](https://coti-k.bandcamp.com/album/theros) through instead of cherry-picking one drift track.
