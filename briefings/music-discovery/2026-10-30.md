# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-10-30

This week pairs darker, forward-leaning rave and progressive club records with home listening built on voices, live percussion and melodic drum programming. Alongside the new releases sits one 2020 electro album that still sounds like transmission from somewhere else.

## Blu:sh — U is for U and me — *Punctuality* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1348093368_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house / breaks / EBM-adjacent rave · **Listen:** <a href="https://blush69.bandcamp.com/album/u-is-for-u-and-me"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mwl_574eU95V_nqOGLunzlQ_moOMLRmbg"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Blu:sh is the project associated with Benoit B and Terra Utopia, and this five-track release opened Punctuality, the imprint Spray started as the home for his darker direction. DJ Mag's premiere points to the unsettling laughter, melodic bleeps and warped low end that run through it, a record where the menace sits right next to the tunes.

**Dig:** Browse the [Punctuality catalogue](https://punctuality.bandcamp.com/) to hear the wider direction the label took from this opening release.

## Shanti Celeste — Romance — *Peach Discs / Method 808* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2524134476_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Vocal electronic pop / house / ambient / R&B · **Listen:** <a href="https://shanticeleste.bandcamp.com/album/romance"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kkRr3IxD7cz74HgN8Uh7ntFEvzF19sj1g"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Chilean-born Shanti Celeste co-founded Peach Discs, and here she shares the release with Method 808 while putting her own singing and songwriting at the centre. Batu contributes to three tracks and harpist Miriam Adefris frames the opening and closing passages. Resident Advisor reads it as a candid album about affection, grief and self-acceptance that reaches well past the dancefloor.

**Dig:** Her [Bandcamp page](https://shanticeleste.bandcamp.com/) gathers the rest of her catalogue beyond Romance.

## Escape Artist — Far Away Places EP — *Craigie Knowes* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2562054792_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house / trance-leaning house / breaks · **Listen:** <a href="https://craigieknowes.bandcamp.com/album/far-away-places-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kpaH05PcIV9ePwodRjF1AeQzIzZgF8-lQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Craigie Knowes is a label and DJ duo based in Perth, Scotland, and Escape Artist makes his debut for them here. The EP is pitched between the European festival season and an Australian summer. Four extended tracks climb through expansive progressive material and land on a breakbeat closer, taking their time to build lift.

**Dig:** Polygonia's [Dream Horizons](https://polygonia.bandcamp.com/album/dream-horizons) takes psychedelic dancefloor movement in a more intricate direction.

## Contours — Elevations — *Music From Memory* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3988031566_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Percussion-led minimalism / jazz / acoustic-electronic ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://contoursmusic.bandcamp.com/album/elevations"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kmq-11aS6cUNlFhbhPsX1w3OeKbMU-ndU"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Tom Burford is a percussionist, producer and DJ who records as Contours, linking programmed rhythm to live playing. Music From Memory presents this suite as a journey that starts with the Malian balafon and opens out into synthesis, strings and collaborations recorded around his Manchester home. Friendship and Cumbrian landscapes sit underneath it all.

**Dig:** [Perc EP](https://contoursmusic.bandcamp.com/album/perc-ep) points the same percussion vocabulary toward the club.

## Sansibar — Targeted Individuals — *FTP* (2020)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3350998616_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electro / Detroit-influenced machine funk · **Listen:** <a href="https://ftp4u.bandcamp.com/album/targeted-individuals"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

FTP is a Berlin electro and bass imprint, and this is the debut full-length from Sansibar, the project of producer Sunny Seppä. The label ties the album to Detroit electro and credits Erik Travis with approving the samples on two tracks, which anchors its science-fiction atmosphere in a specific lineage rather than a vague one.

**Dig:** The [FTP shop](https://ftp4u.bandcamp.com/) opens onto the label's wider electro, bass and bootleg world.

## µ-Ziq — Grush — *Planet Mu* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3025477005_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Melodic IDM / jungle / electro / acid · **Listen:** <a href="https://mikeparadinas.bandcamp.com/album/grush"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_n72IqjK51ZG8Wc5GjfUGCJapVF1LW4rZQ"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

µ-Ziq is Mike Paradinas, who also founded Planet Mu. He built Grush from material tested live, shaped by feedback from his touring visual collaborator ID:Mora. It plays like a set: jungle, electro and footwork-like acid pass by with intricate drums set against open-hearted melodies.
