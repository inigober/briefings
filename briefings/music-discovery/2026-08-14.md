# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-08-14

This week leans hard into the sunlit overlap between Balearic home listening and trance-adjacent club music: transportive LPs for the sofa, plus melodic-floor records that make sense if your recent crate has been tilting prog, trance, and euphoric crossover. There is more curation-object energy than auteur singularity in this pool, but the strongest picks still feel like records you would actually leave on.

## Leonidas & Hobbes — Pockets Of Light Double Album (HM024) [BALEARIC, ELECTRONIC, AMBIENT] — *Hobbes Music* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1590147579_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Balearic / ambient / ambient techno / electro / house · **Listen:** <a href="https://hobbesmusicon.bandcamp.com/album/pockets-of-light-double-album-hm024-balearic-electronic-ambient"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kTVWvZa96YqHYefxyGSP5RFUyJlePJD-8"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

The appeal here is range without drift: a double album that can move from uptempo dance cuts to downtempo passages and then out into beatless ambient without losing the thread. That suits the overlap in your recent listening between Domenique Dumont, Khotin, and the softer edges of progressive electronics. It reads less like chill-out wallpaper than a proper home-listening longplayer with enough after-hours motion to keep it from going inert.

**Dig:** Stay with the Hobbes lane via [*Pay It Forward LP*](https://hobbesmusicon.bandcamp.com/album/pay-it-forward-lp-hm017lp-disco-house-ambient-balearic), the label's earlier Balearic-leaning chapter.

## DJ Pippi & Willie Graff — Universal Language — *DJ Pippi & Willie Graff* (2024)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3467729526_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Balearic / chillout / disco / downtempo / Balearic pop · **Listen:** <a href="https://djpippi-williegraff.bandcamp.com/album/universal-language"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mkefvzCr04-5-2mqf6mhpEr-KEGs9eGH0"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This lands squarely in the horizontal, Ibizan end of your taste rather than the heads-down DJ crate: warm, open, and more interested in atmosphere than in proving underground credentials. The "freedom and diversity" line in the inbox copy is the useful one here. If the recent attraction to Balearic synth-pop, boogie, and easygoing ambient-house has legs, this looks like the kind of record that can soundtrack a full afternoon rather than just donate one track to a playlist.

**Dig:** Keep following the duo's own page through [*Universal Language*](https://djpippi-williegraff.bandcamp.com/album/universal-language) if you want the full sequence rather than a single-entry taste test.

## Balearic — Balearic 4 — *Balearic* (2018)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0157326415_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient / Balearic / chillout / cosmic / downtempo / house / world · **Listen:** <a href="https://balearic.bandcamp.com/album/balearic-4"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kJ9_QumWXi-vjATcKtorrhEryU287Jfko"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

An older compilation, but not one that feels like homework. The draw is the breadth: cosmic drift, Spanish-guitar warmth, flamenco-dub touches, and that slightly world-tinged soft-focus mood your saved albums already hint at. It lines up with the gentler home side of the profile without defaulting to obvious canon or museum-piece reissue territory, which is exactly what the aged-well slot should do.

**Dig:** If the compilation logic works for you, keep roaming through [*Balearic 4*](https://balearic.bandcamp.com/album/balearic-4) as a world-and-chill survey rather than a single-track source.

## Hypnotised — Hypnotised presents Balearic Trance — *Hypnotised* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0122275528_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Balearic trance / trance / ambient trance · **Listen:** <a href="https://hypnotised.bandcamp.com/album/hypnotised-presents-balearic-trance"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mjUp81I9i2jJOnV_YV8lhQgTsh4DjiZkM"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This is the most direct hit in the inbox for the recent trance/prog side of the crate: classic-sense euphoric progression, Mediterranean light, and a revivalist bias that feels deliberate rather than cynical. The Chicane/Ferry Corsten/Tiesto coordinates in the source notes are a fair warning that this is not the weirdest or toughest club recommendation here, but if you have been leaning into "sunshine with lift" trance again, that directness is part of the point.

**Dig:** Use [*Hypnotised presents Balearic Trance*](https://hypnotised.bandcamp.com/album/hypnotised-presents-balearic-trance) as a map for that classic-leaning crossover lane and follow whichever cuts feel least museum-like.

## Roger Shah — Magic Island Vol. 13 (Mixed by Roger Shah) — *Black Hole Recordings* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3915141525_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house / trance / Balearic trance · **Listen:** <a href="https://blackholerecordings.bandcamp.com/album/magic-island-vol-13-mixed-by-roger-shah"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kApeEV0jYycAmG8LNNs7_cOuIhUsWbZpw"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Where the Hypnotised compilation works as a revivalist mood-board, this one reads more like a usable survey of current melodic-floor instincts: prog, trance, and house blended into something broad but still coherent. It is not the grittiest club record in the world, and it will not scratch the chunkier Haws/Kalahari part of the brain, but it does rhyme with the recent run of progressive house and progressive trance in Rekordbox well enough to clear the feature bar.

**Dig:** Treat [*Magic Island Vol. 13*](https://blackholerecordings.bandcamp.com/album/magic-island-vol-13-mixed-by-roger-shah) as a survey node when you want current sunlit prog/trance rather than a single peak-time weapon.

## Romain Garcia — EURÊKA! — *Anjunadeep* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3047113866_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Melodic house / progressive house / melodic house & techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://anjunadeep.bandcamp.com/album/eur-ka"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nJYsCADMmbKTXOHPHYZt9Vw6dOEb1qO5w"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This is the polished end of the club half, but that is also why it works better as a feature than another big-label sampler. The inbox notes call out emotive progressive-house credentials, and that feels right: sleek, widescreen, and likely strongest when you want melodic motion without losing the album arc entirely. It sits well beside the recent progressive-house emphasis in the crate even if it is smoother and more studio-burnished than the rougher underground records you often save.

**Dig:** If the polished-side crossover lands, keep digging through [*EURÊKA!*](https://anjunadeep.bandcamp.com/album/eur-ka) as a full-length rather than treating it like anonymous label wallpaper.
