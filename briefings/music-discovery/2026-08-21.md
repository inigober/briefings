# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-08-21

This week's strongest thread is the overlap between precise, crate-ready electronics and home records that still speak club language from a distance. There is one proper aged-well electro pull, a couple of prog-minded floor options, and a home lane that stays tactile and inward rather than dissolving into pure background ambient.

## ReKab — Subtle Beginnings — *Self-released / reissue campaign* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0223569536_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Hardware electro · **Listen:** <a href="https://rekabalbums.bandcamp.com/album/subtle-beginnings"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lyTe-usyVVEuHSc08OH6ERrNEh0_SoSjo"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

The aged-well pick this week is also the most direct hit for the electro side of the crate: emotional hardware programming with enough melodic weight to feel lived-in rather than purely functional. It connects neatly to the recent run of Aphex Twin, UFO Space, and other machine records in the profile, but avoids "canon homework" territory because the appeal is the actual listen, not just the lineage note.

**Dig:** If the remastered electro melancholy lands, keep going through [ReKab's Bandcamp page](https://rekabalbums.bandcamp.com/).

## Ida Urd & Ingri Høyland — Duvet — *Balmat* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2239295709_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, drone, soundscape · **Listen:** <a href="https://balmat.bandcamp.com/album/duvet"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_nQvWhBt20GyuOC66yAFfnRwcBdgPRmD6Y"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This is the softest home pick in the set, but not in a vague way: warm, domestic ambient with a sense of enclosure rather than grand isolation. That suits the recent home-listening pattern around Khotin, Roméo Poirier, and the gentler edge of the saved Spotify albums, and Balmat's curatorial reputation helps it clear the "leave it playing" bar easily.

**Dig:** If that close-miked, quietly detailed mood works for you, roam further through [Balmat's catalogue](https://balmat.bandcamp.com/).

## Loraine James — Detached From The Rest Of You — *Hyperdub* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2420633024_10.jpg)

**Genre:** IDM, ambient, R&B-inflected experimental electronic · **Listen:** <a href="https://lorainejames.bandcamp.com/album/detached-from-the-rest-of-you"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mb3AJ_o5xlKGq16Jx7Id-Vb4phlImQQMY"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This gives the home half some sharper edges: frosty production, clipped rhythm design, and enough spoken or sung material to keep it from reading as another pure-ambient recommendation. It makes sense as a private interior counterpart to the DJ side of the profile, especially if the attraction is detailed electronic architecture rather than genre-purity.

**Dig:** If the mix of intimacy and digital precision clicks, start following [Loraine James on Bandcamp](https://lorainejames.bandcamp.com/).

## Lisene — Convergence [A52001] — *Lisene//Area 52* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1842097019_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, trance, electro, techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://lisene.bandcamp.com/album/lisene-convergence-a52001"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mN93q9YZ7o1B2zQBWiPjV0Lz7aPEuIclo"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Among the current club candidates, this is the clearest match for the recent prog/trance/electro tilt in Rekordbox without collapsing into scene-revival shorthand. The darker psychedelic edge matters: it feels closer to the UFO Space, Bliss Inc., and Eoin DJ lane than to glossy festival uplift, which is why it earns a featured slot instead of just a utility mention.

**Dig:** If you want more of that shadowy progressive crossover, dig through [Lisene's Bandcamp page](https://lisene.bandcamp.com/).

## Yu Su — Foundry — *Short Span* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1686785173_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient, minimal techno-adjacent experimental · **Listen:** <a href="https://yusu.bandcamp.com/album/foundry"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_m_pQ0qpAbUPOwV3GcsLqtuYtnbyT3db7g"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

This is the best bridge record in the pool: clearly a home listen, but built from textures and negative space that still make sense to dance-music ears. If the profile's overlap between ambient playlists and minimal-techno-adjacent listening is real, this kind of inky, half-dissolved structure is exactly the sweet spot.

**Dig:** If that crossover of drift and structure is the hook, keep following [Yu Su on Bandcamp](https://yusu.bandcamp.com/).

## HVL — Formation — *Kiyadama* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0685209728_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Breakbeat, acid, dub techno, club techno · **Listen:** <a href="https://kiyadama.bandcamp.com/album/formation"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

The last featured club slot goes to the record with the strongest "this would actually work in a set" logic outside the prog lane: dub space, acid lines, and rolling breaks, with enough character to avoid anonymous functionality. It sits a little sideways to the recent progressive-house emphasis, but the wider crate already has room for that Bassiani-scale pressure.

**Dig:** If the dubby breakbeat tension is the draw, browse the rest of [Kiyadama's catalogue](https://kiyadama.bandcamp.com/).
