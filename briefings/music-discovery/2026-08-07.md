# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-08-07

Chunker-house pressure, Punctuality peak-time, and the home-listening lane your Spotify rotation is already leaning into — plus a few familiar-label deep cuts parked in More listening for when the floor cools off.

## Papa Nugs — Move It Or Lose It EP — *Big Saldo's Chunkers* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a2423410040_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Chunker house / hip house · **Listen:** <a href="https://sallyc.bandcamp.com/album/move-it-or-lose-it-ep"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

London's Papa Nugs lands the first 2025 cut on Sally C's Big Saldo's Chunkers — four festival-ready rollers that braid early-trance euphoria, turn-of-the-century Ibiza tribal, and the label's signature boshy basslines. *Turn It Down* is the mission statement ("we're here to have a good time"); *Run It (Ruff n Ready Mix)* is the after-hours closer. Your crate already leans Chunkers (Big Saldo's Chunkers sits in recent Rekordbox label adds); this is the missing BSC007 chapter.

**Dig:** Trace the label's arc through [*Big Saldo's Chunker 003*](https://sallyc.bandcamp.com/album/big-saldos-chunker-ep-003) — Sally C's own four-tracker on the imprint.

## Drua — Nightfire — *Punctuality* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1373247297_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Hard house / prog-house · **Listen:** <a href="https://punctuality.bandcamp.com/album/nightfire"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Irish producer Brian Fallon (Drua) delivers four contemporary hard-house tools for Berlin's Punctuality — a label already threading through your recent crate (six Punctuality tracks in the 24-month Rekordbox window). *UP* is the festival-season front-runner (early support from Roza Terenzi, Sally C, Spray, Maara); *Nightfire* is the set-turner — introspective pads dissolving into pulsing subs and hip-catching bass. Peak-time without anonymous utility.

**Dig:** Compare the label's Eoin Ryan–penned write-ups on [Mohajer's *all in*](https://punctuality.bandcamp.com/album/all-in) — the autumn follow-up if *Nightfire* clicks.

## Floorplan — I'm Gonna Sing — *M-Plant* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1552130034_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Gospel techno / house · **Listen:** <a href="https://m-plant.bandcamp.com/album/im-gonna-sing"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Robert and Lyric Hood's first Floorplan transmission of 2025 — six-and-a-half minutes of hands-in-the-air gospel-house pressure on Robert's M-Plant imprint. Your July Rekordbox binge already has *We Magnify His Name* and *Tell You No Lie*; *I'm Gonna Sing* is the 2025 single that completes the sermon. Detroit spiritual techno with actual character, not a functional tool.

**Dig:** Pair with the September follow-up [*Eternal Life EP*](https://m-plant.bandcamp.com/album/eternal-life-ep) — two mixes of the title track plus solo Robert Hood cuts on the flip.

## Bitchin Bajas — Inland See — *Drag City* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1078246609_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient / electroacoustic · **Listen:** <a href="https://bitchinbajas.bandcamp.com/album/inland-see"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Chicago's Bitchin Bajas return to Drag City with their most road-written record yet — four pieces (*Skylarking*, *Reno*, *Keiji Dreams*, the 18-minute *Graut*) recorded live at Electrical Audio with zero added reverb. You have them in Spotify's last-four-weeks chart; *Inland See* is the 2025 headphone chapter after April's NIS collaboration *Totality* (Bandcamp Album of the Day). Post-ambient craft that floats rather than lectures.

**Dig:** Step back to the NIS collab [*Totality*](https://bitchinbajas.bandcamp.com/album/totality) if you want the rhythm-forward half of the same year's output.

## Spray — reSPRAY Vol. 2 — *Spray* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3788342776_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Electro / synthpop · **Listen:** <a href="https://spray.bandcamp.com/album/respray-vol-2"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Jenny McLaren and Ricardo Autobahn's second instalment in the 2025 reSPRAY remix series — deep cuts from *Living In Neon*, *Enforced Fun*, and *Children of a Laser God* given Italo sheen and 8-bit gleam. Four recent Spray Rekordbox adds make the case; this is witty, subversive UK electropop you'd actually leave on, not crate-filler. *I Am Gothic (Synthetik FM's Toccata Fugue Mix)* is the curveball.

**Dig:** Start the trilogy at [*reSPRAY Vol. 1*](https://spray.bandcamp.com/album/respray-vol-1) if you want the Queen-of-Summer / Pet Shop Boys lane first.

## Leif — Collide — *AD 93* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a0895758915_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Experimental ambient / guitar drone · **Listen:** <a href="https://leifsounds.bandcamp.com/album/collide"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

UK producer Leif's return to AD 93 after four years — eight tracks shaped by a childhood Aria Pro II with damaged electrics, mastered by Kassian Troyer. Bleep called it his most texturally present work; *How To Eat An Orange* (parts 1 & 2) and *Filmed Backwards* are the entry points. He's in your six-month Spotify rotation alongside Roméo Poirier and keiyaA — this is the 2025 studio album that isn't a beat-driven Khotin cousin.

**Dig:** Contrast the guitar fracture with his self-released deep-house longplayer [*Taraxacum*](https://leifsounds.bandcamp.com/album/taraxacum) for the same brain, different decade.
