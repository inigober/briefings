# Music Discovery — Week of 2026-11-27

This week pairs long-memory ambient and folk-tinged records for the headphones with club music that tells a story across a whole release, from sunrise trance to Scottish after-hours house. Several names circle each other through remixes and side projects, so one record tends to lead to the next.

## Carmen Villain — Memoria — *Smalltown Supersound* (2026)

![Album cover](https://dn711109.ca.archive.org/0/items/mbid-8962ef76-04f8-4ac9-b23b-f24b27ac58b8/mbid-8962ef76-04f8-4ac9-b23b-f24b27ac58b8-46103706105_thumb500.jpg)

**Genre:** Ambient dub, electroacoustic, experimental · **Listen:** <a href="https://carmenvillain.bandcamp.com/album/memoria"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lYG5I-8CfOqY1wtkKGxdWU2rE5wD1qncI"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Carmen Villain is a Norwegian-Mexican producer who has made Smalltown Supersound, the Oslo label known for stretching the edges of electronic and left-field pop, her regular home. Memoria reassembles new and archived recordings of flute, trumpet and clarinet into slow, dub-weighted pieces about how memory shifts every time it is handled. Pauline Oliveros and Apichatpong Weerasethakul are her stated reference points, and you can hear that patience in the way each wind line is left to decay into the room.

**Dig:** Go back one record to [Only Love From Now On](https://carmenvillain.bandcamp.com/album/only-love-from-now-on) to hear where her acoustic-dub language started.

## Olsvangèr — The Core — *Craigie Knowes* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1363033464_10.jpg)

**Genre:** House, psychedelic techno, breaks · **Listen:** <a href="https://craigieknowes.bandcamp.com/album/the-core?from=footer-cc-a1445291722"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_kd7ErNfs48EMqXgp1bqVKA1DOrdSSGcso"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Craigie Knowes is a Scottish label run by a DJ duo of the same name, and The Core is built as a night in miniature: it opens with buoyant house, turns darker and more psychedelic through the techno tracks, and finishes in breaks. Olsvangèr, who also turns up as a remixer elsewhere this week, keeps a loose, melodic touch even when the kick gets heavier, so the sequence plays like one long set.

**Dig:** Earthly Measures’ [From Earth](https://oathcreations.bandcamp.com/album/from-earth) carries Olsvangèr’s Wildwood Dub in a more percussion-led setting.

## Patricia Wolf — See-Through — *Balmat* (2022)

![Album cover](https://dn710201.ca.archive.org/0/items/mbid-409a742d-72ba-40a8-bb50-d798f4fad43f/mbid-409a742d-72ba-40a8-bb50-d798f4fad43f-33830297934_thumb500.jpg)

**Genre:** Melodic ambient, field recordings, electronic pastoral · **Listen:** <a href="https://patriciawolf.bandcamp.com/album/see-through?from=footer-cc-a2716720416"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_l6B8IgTg1gsGkig4WXw0csloKp8dLYmLc"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Patricia Wolf is a Portland musician, sound designer and field recordist who made See-Through for Balmat as a turn toward curiosity after the grief that shaped her debut. Synthesizer studies sit beside environmental recordings, with the odd rhythm surfacing underneath, and the album earned a place in Bandcamp Daily’s ambient roundup for May 2022. It is a warm, outdoorsy record that rewards being left running.

**Dig:** Her 2026 contribution to the Music To Watch Seeds Grow By series, [009: Patricia Wolf (Yarrow)](https://patriciawolf.bandcamp.com/album/009-patricia-wolf-yarrow), is the natural next stop.

## SpunOff — El Ritmo del Alba [SPLOVE006] — *SpunyLove Records* (2025)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a3726512549_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, neo-trance, Latin-inflected club · **Listen:** <a href="https://spunoff.bandcamp.com/album/el-ritmo-del-alba-splove006"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_mZY6sFxClYdZPBEABmpj-wC_Zr9-O9ETs"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

SpunOff is the alias of Paris-based producer Alexandre Oliviero, and SpunyLove is an imprint with a bright, crossover-minded streak. The release follows a sunrise concept: four originals with Latin rhythmic accents, then remixes by Maruwa, Blu:sh, Olsvangèr and Bliss Inc. that pull the same material toward trance and peak-hour melody. Play the originals first, then hear how each remixer reads them.

**Dig:** Hear remixer Olsvangèr’s own production on [The Core](https://craigieknowes.bandcamp.com/album/the-core?from=footer-cc-a1445291722).

## Baldruin — Mosaike der Imagination — *Quindi Records* (2024)

![Album cover](https://dn710103.ca.archive.org/0/items/mbid-1319e7d3-34ce-4807-9793-9bc108cf0891/mbid-1319e7d3-34ce-4807-9793-9bc108cf0891-40442735117_thumb500.jpg)

**Genre:** Digital folk, experimental electronics, ritual ambient · **Listen:** <a href="https://baldruin.bandcamp.com/album/mosaike-der-imagination"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a> · <a href="https://music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_lIkguqxolDuT8k4ZQjgEhkjPwceh2M620"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=youtube.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> YouTube</a>

Baldruin is Wiesbaden musician Johannes Schebler, whose cassette-era catalogue links into a wider German experimental-folk network. Quindi is better known for cosy reveries, and this album pushes past that: seventeen short scenes of plucked strings, choral experiments, percussion and synthesis, each over before it overstays.

**Dig:** Schebler’s collaborative side is on show in Yayoba’s [A Maze of Glass](https://baldruin.bandcamp.com/album/a-maze-of-glass).

## Rambal Cochet — Magma Resort LP (SNGWAXLP002) — *Sengiley Wax* (2026)

![Album cover](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1801403481_10.jpg)

**Genre:** Progressive house, dream trance, ambient interludes · **Listen:** <a href="https://sengiley.bandcamp.com/album/rambal-cochet-magma-resort-lp-sngwaxlp002"><img src="https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=bandcamp.com&sz=32" width="16" height="16" alt=""> Bandcamp</a>

Rambal Cochet, who is also associated with the Volta Cab name, makes this album for Sengiley Wax, part of the Sengiley Recordings family and its Russian series. His own notes tie the tracks to relocation, remembered travel and early-2000s dream trance, and the album moves between rolling progressive house and quiet ambient interludes.

**Dig:** His racing-game-inspired neo-Goa and drum-and-bass detour is on [Carbon Vibration](https://lumbagorecords.bandcamp.com/album/carbon-vibration).
