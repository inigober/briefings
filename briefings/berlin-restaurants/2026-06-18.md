# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-06-18

Places API verification sharpened this week's pool: two previously listed Chinese addresses are permanently closed, while several Thai and Turkish rooms now carry live ratings and hours. The useful split is between focused specialists (noodles, curries, lahmacun) and institutions that reward ordering discipline rather than menu tourism.

### LIU Nudelhaus — Mitte — €

**Hours:** Mon–Tue 11:30–15:00; Wed–Fri 11:30–15:00 & 17:00–19:30; (closed Sat–Sun)

**Rating:** 4.5 (2401)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=5978374952795598817&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Berlin's most emotionally charged Sichuan noodle address remains a lunch-window cult spot for good reason: the Tianshui noodles and dumplings are built around precise technique and numbing heat, not broad-menu convenience. Lines are real, quantities of signature dishes are limited, and anyone expecting a relaxed dinner will be disappointed. For a focused noodle meal at this price, it still outclasses most Berlin Chinese rooms that charge more for less conviction.

### Chuan Garden 川园 — Mitte — €€

**Hours:** Daily 12:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.7 (334)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=3614477287672776716&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Chuan Garden is the rare Mitte Sichuan room with chef pedigree behind dishes like Zigong rabbit and whole-fish preparations — bold, clean heat that builds rather than bludgeons. The menu is long and uneven, and ordering several oily wet dishes in the same vein can feel repetitive fast. Still, for diners who want regionally serious Sichuan beyond generic mapo tofu, it rewards thoughtful ordering more than cover-to-cover eating.

### Larb Koi — Friedrichshain — €€

**Hours:** Wed–Thu 18:00–22:00; Fri–Sat 18:00–23:00; Sun 03:00–22:00; (closed Mon–Tue)

**Rating:** 4.6 (684)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=1837714750978964420&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Larb Koi straddles polished room and assertive house-style Thai — octopus curry and papaya salad with real heat, not Berlin-default sweetness. The design-forward interior can distract from the food focus, and it is not the deepest regional specialist in the city. Among Friedrichshain's Thai options, though, it delivers more intensity and confidence than most sit-down rooms at this price; better for diners who want robust flavour in a proper dining setting.

### Hanuman Thai Curry House — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Thu 12:00–15:30 & 17:00–22:00; Fri–Sun 12:00–22:00; (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.7 (355)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=10805004978977801651&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Hanuman is a curry specialist in the true sense — a short menu built around regional Thai curries, miang kam, and house sausage rather than a fifty-dish catch-all. The room is compact and will not satisfy anyone who wants a sprawling feast. If your priority is curry depth and regional precision, though, it is one of Kreuzberg's stronger mid-range Thai bets; skip it when you mainly want noodles or quick counter food.

### Pamfilya — Wedding — €€ (good value)

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 08:00–00:00; Fri–Sat 08:00–01:00; Sun 08:00–00:00

**Rating:** 4.0 (2494)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9414198761911637931&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Pamfilya has sustained Wedding's döner credibility for decades: lean veal yaprak, charcoal grill, and a traditional room that still feels local rather than manufactured. Peak hours skew tourist-adjacent, vegetarian options are thin, and innovation is not the point. For a classic, well-grilled döner at fair money — execution over concept — it remains one of the stronger bets in the category.

### Ägäis — Neukölln — €€ (good value)

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 13:00–00:00; Fri–Sat 13:00–02:00; Sun 13:00–00:00

**Rating:** 5.0 (110)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=12452059319223732937&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Ägäis is a rare Turkish fish specialist — dorade and red mullet treated with restraint, mezze that support rather than compete. The menu is sparse, the room is quiet, and the high Google rating rests on a modest review count. If you want clean, technically sound fish grounded in Turkish tradition without ocakbaşı theatrics, this is one of Neukölln's more interesting mid-range meals; not a party address.

### Big Bascha — Moabit — €

**Hours:** Daily 08:30–17:00

**Rating:** 4.8 (2330)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=15970551625816792714&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Big Bascha does one thing well: Lebanese breakfast and lunch classics — fatteh, hummus, manakish — in an unpretentious Moabit room with worthwhile outdoor seating in summer. The window is narrow (morning and afternoon), the setting is casual, and evening seekers should look elsewhere. For technique-first Levantine morning fare at low prices, it is more reliable than most brunch-trend rooms pretending at the same cuisine.

### Adana Grillhaus — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 12:00–03:00; Fri–Sat 12:00–04:00; Sun 12:00–03:00

**Rating:** 4.7 (2319)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=3901475484461481539&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Adana Grillhaus is a Kreuzberg institution that mostly earns its reputation — Adana kebap, lamb chops, and köfte with consistent grill technique across years of local traffic. Service and meat quality can vary by shift and crowd, and the room is functional rather than memorable. Still, among mid-range Turkish grills that survive fame without fully selling out, it remains a credible weekday anchor: better flavor discipline than many broad-menu competitors.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. LIU Nudelhaus — strongest single-category execution at the lowest price
2. Pamfilya — most reliable classic döner/grill value of the week
3. Hanuman Thai Curry House — best focused Thai curry cooking outside last week's broader Thai list
