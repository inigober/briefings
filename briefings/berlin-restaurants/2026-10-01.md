# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-10-01

This week is built around kitchens with a specific job: hand-pulled noodles in Wedding, Zigong Sichuan and Jiangsu dumplings in Mitte, a Kreuzberg lokanta instead of another charcoal grill, pasta on Donaustraße, and one product-led French room in Prenzlauer Berg.

### Mr. Noodle Chen — Wedding — € (good value)

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 11:30–14:00 & 17:00–21:00; Fri–Sun 11:30–14:00 & 17:00–21:30

**Rating:** 4.8 (1261)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=1077236137200459133&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The Wedding branch of the lamian operation, not the Friedrichshain room from mid-August: Willdenowstraße, a noodle master pulling to order, and extras (skewers, roujiamo) that keep it from being soup-only. The chain footprint is the limit — less singular than a family-run specialist, and still a focused bowl meal rather than a long sit-down with a mixed table. Against that Friedrichshain address, Wen Cheng's narrower Simon-Dach evening, or Liu Nudelhaus as the reader's noodle reference, this is the north-west craft option at a true €: same technical job, different geography, easier if you are already in Wedding or want a lunch window. Go for noodle execution; skip it if you need a broad Chinese dinner.

### Chuan Garden 川园 — Mitte — €€

**Hours:** Daily 12:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.6 (356)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=3614477287672776716&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Torstraße Sichuan kitchen arguing from Zigong rather than a generic chili-and-mala template: former Himmelspagode chefs, rabbit dishes, and menu language that is more regional than most Kantstraße rooms. It is new, which means consistency is unproven, and Mitte is already over-served in this briefing's map. Against Ming Dynastie's long Mitte institution from late August, Hi! Chili's modern Prenzlauer Berg Sichuan, or last week's seafood-and-dim-sum Meet You, Chuan Garden is the more specific regional bet — less of a group-proof greatest-hits house, more of a cuisine argument. Worth a first visit if Zigong cooking is the point; treat it as a hypothesis until the kitchen has a longer track record.

### Mercan — Kreuzberg — € (good value)

**Hours:** Mon–Sat 11:00–22:00 (closed Sun)

**Rating:** 4.6 (978)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=11770093415398915802&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Wiener Straße lokanta: daily home-style Turkish cooking, stew-and-vegetable logic, second-generation family continuity — the opposite of last week's Hermannstraße ocakbaşı at Mira or the Skalitzer Adana grill-house sequence two weeks ago. The format is the point and the ceiling: less dramatic than a charcoal specialist, the menu shifts with the day, and Sunday is closed. Against Tadım's skewer craft from late July, Jemenitisches Restaurant's Yemeni rice-and-braise on Karl-Marx-Straße, or Imren around Hermannplatz, Mercan is the canteen you actually use — cheaper, calmer, built for lunch and regular dinners rather than a destination grill night. Strong when you want everyday Turkish cooking; skip it if you came for kebap theatre.

### Ni's Restaurant — Mitte — €€

**Hours:** Daily 12:00–22:00

**Rating:** 4.4 (2378)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9208310204966926796&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Kleine Präsidentenstraße Jiangsu room whose named draws are a giant soup dumpling and meter-long noodles — a clearer regional job than another pan-Chinese menu near Hackescher Markt. The write-up trail is promotional, the 4.4 across a large review base is competent rather than pointed, and this is the second Mitte entry in a week that should not keep circling the center. Against Mr. Noodle Chen's lamian craft above, Fu Li Lai's dumpling-and-noodle canteen in Steglitz two weeks ago, or Lon Men's Kantstraße noodle-and-dim-sum standby, Ni's is the east-of-Mitte specialist with a Jiangsu framing rather than Lanzhou or Xi'an. Useful when soup dumplings and long noodles are the order; it is not the week's strongest evidence base if you already have a noodle default.

### La Bolognina — Neukölln — €€ (good value)

**Hours:** Tue–Sun 17:00–23:00 (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.4 (630)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9807831381490708821&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Donaustraße pasta kitchen — changing ravioli, tagliatelle, gnocchi, strozzapreti — at neighborhood scale, which is closer to this reader's usual spend than another west-side product temple. The published case is praise-forward and light on failures, and the room is better understood as a pasta specialist than as a full Italian survey. Against Common's bakery-to-pizza dual format from early September, Il Calice's ingredient-led Charlottenburg table two weeks ago, or Nini e Petirosso as a personal Italian reference, this is the local craft option: dinner-only, Monday closed, less of a wine-and-product destination, more of a bowl of pasta done with intent. Worth walking to when pasta is the meal; skip it if you want a broader menu or a lunch window.

It sits above the €–€€ logic that should drive most meals here, the 140-review sample is thin for the claim, and Wednesday–Saturday hours make it a planned evening. Against CODA's dessert tasting-menu on Friedelstraße two weeks ago, Horváth's Austrian canal room, or Bieberbau's herb-and-spice Wilmersdorf set, Matthias is the more classical French craft pick — less concept, more product and technique, still a splurge. Book it only if that is the night you want; the rest of this list is the better weekly food.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Mr. Noodle Chen — the clearest craft-and-value case this week: hand-pulled noodles at €, in a neighborhood this list does not overuse.
2. Mercan — the most useful everyday kitchen here, and the one that actually changes the Turkish picture after two weeks of grill houses.
3. Chuan Garden 川园 — the strongest regional thesis, if you want Zigong Sichuan rather than another familiar chili room, with the caveat that it is still new.
