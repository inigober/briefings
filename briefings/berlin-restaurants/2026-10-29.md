# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-10-29

A week of cheap, focused cooking: Thai canteen noodles, veal döner, Balkan charcoal grill and a Central European lunch room, with house-made pasta and Taiwanese wontons for the evenings. Most of it sits well under 30 euros.

### Samakki Talad Thai — Charlottenburg — € (good value)

**Hours:** Daily 12:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.2 (371)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=17791525734688365811&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Thai street-food canteen where you order from separate cooking stations, which feels chaotic the first time and makes sense by the second. Skip the pad Thai and go for boat noodles, nam tok or the papaya salad, and add fried pork belly if you are hungry. The stations are the point: noodles, Isaan salads and curries each get their own cook. Prices have crept up since the early write-ups, so treat the cheap-eats label as a good bet rather than a guarantee.

### Pamfilya — Wedding — €€

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 08:00–00:00; Fri–Sat 08:00–01:00; Sun 08:00–00:00

**Rating:** 4.1 (2597)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9414198761911637931&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The reason to come is the hand-stacked veal yaprak döner, which is leaner and cleaner-tasting than the fatty beef versions most of the city sells. Get it with lavash, raw onion, lemon and chili, and leave the sauces alone. The rest of the grill menu is competent but not the draw, and a seated plate costs noticeably more than a sandwich at the counter. Open nearly around the clock, so it works for an odd hour.

### Salumeria Lamuri — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Tue 18:00–22:00; Wed–Fri 12:00–15:00 & 18:00–22:00; Sat 18:00–22:00; (closed Mon, Sun)

**Rating:** 4.6 (500)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=13554042743700931316&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The tiled room gets photographed more than the food deserves, which is a pity, because the pasta is the real thing: firm, properly al dente and sauced with restraint. Tagliatelle with truffle and Parmesan butter is the kind of dish that has nowhere to hide, and it holds up. The menu changes, so what you order may not be what a friend ate last month. Lunch is the crowded slot; dinner is calmer, and it is closed Sunday and Monday.

### Prizreni Qebaptore — Neukölln — € (good value)

**Hours:** Daily 07:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.8 (671)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=1220766494754305012&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Albanian qebapa off a charcoal grill, including a cheese-and-garlic version, for very little money. Stay on the grilled meats; the burek and baked goods are fine but not why people queue. It is a narrow, meat-led stop rather than a place to linger over a long dinner, and that focus is exactly what makes it good. Same family of cooking as Adana Grillhaus, with a Balkan accent.

### Lon Men's Noodle House — Charlottenburg — €€

**Hours:** Mon 12:00–22:00; Thu–Sun 12:00–22:00; (closed Tue–Wed)

**Rating:** 4.5 (3927)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9563754848875105605&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Order the chili wontons first; they are the best thing on a Taiwanese menu that also covers noodles, dumplings and steamed duck buns. The noodle soups are the weak spot, with broth that can taste thin, so build the meal around the wontons and buns instead. Closed Tuesday and Wednesday, so plan around that.

### Joseph-Roth-Diele — Tiergarten — € (good value)

**Hours:** Tue–Fri 10:00–22:00 (closed Mon, Sat–Sun)

**Rating:** 4.4 (1595)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=4837385953299349406&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A literary old-Berlin dining room that happens to cook honest Central European food: house-made Spätzle, goulash, traditional cold plates. The weekday lunch deal, two dishes for under 8 euros, is the bargain; there is little choice, but little is needed. Dinner is a different, pricier proposition, so judge it separately. A good weekday lunch near Potsdamer Straße, closed at weekends.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Prizreni Qebaptore — for a fast, cheap, charcoal-led lunch where you order one thing and get it right.
2. Salumeria Lamuri — for an evening built around a plate of properly made tagliatelle.
3. Pamfilya — for the veal döner, eaten simply, when you want something leaner than the usual.
