# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-10-15

A cosy-weather week: hand-rolled pasta in two corners of the south, a proper Austrian schnitzel, and one inexpensive vegan set menu for when you want something more composed.

### Lauretta — Tempelhof — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Sun 17:00–23:00 (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.5 (530)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=1808302106466398168&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A neighbourhood Italian that makes its own pasta, which is the whole reason to cross into Tempelhof. It is a pasta-and-pizza menu rather than a tightly regional osteria, so the identity is looser than at the best specialists, and the praise for it is more about the fresh pasta than any one signature dish. Go for the pasta, treat the rest as supporting cast, and remember it only opens in the evening and is closed on Mondays.

### Sissi Restaurant — Schöneberg — €€€

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 17:00–23:00; Fri–Sat 17:00–23:30; Sun 17:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.7 (1377)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=6400383924715615295&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The veal schnitzel is the order: tender meat, a crisp breading, and a warm potato-cucumber salad on the side the way it should be. The Saftgulasch is a solid second choice. This is classic, heavy Austrian cooking rather than anything exploratory, and a full meal pushes you well past the usual weeknight budget. Worth it when you want the old-fashioned version done properly; ignore the themed interior.

### Ripieno — Schöneberg — €€

**Rating:** 4.6 (678)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=4405641568888698269&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Small pasta stop at Gleisdreieck where the sauces are as much the point as the noodles: a gricia that keeps its pepper and pecorino edge, and an oxtail ragù that is rich without turning heavy. The format is narrow, so a table that wants variety will be bored, and not every dish on the menu gets the same raves as those two. Order those two and you will have a good evening. Note that it is closed on Wednesdays.

### HAPPA — Kreuzberg — €€€ (good value)

**Hours:** Tue–Sat 18:00–22:00 (closed Mon, Sun)

**Rating:** 4.8 (462)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=946052033712709760&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A fully vegan, seasonal three-course menu for around €40, cooked with an eye on waste and with Alpine and Mediterranean leanings. That price is what makes it interesting: it is closer to a relaxed set dinner than to a tasting-menu event, and it should be judged that way rather than against Berlin's high-end rooms. The set format leaves little room to choose, so it suits a night when you are happy to be led. Book ahead; it is open only Tuesday to Saturday evenings.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Sissi Restaurant — for a schnitzel night when you want something old-fashioned and well executed.
2. Ripieno — for pasta with real sauces, the gricia and the oxtail ragù.
3. HAPPA — for a composed vegan dinner at a price that does not feel like a splurge.
