# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-10-15

A quieter, south-leaning week: house-made pasta in Tempelhof and Schöneberg, a proper Viennese schnitzel, and one inexpensive vegan tasting menu in Kreuzberg. Little of it is adventurous, but the cooking is built on craft rather than concept.

### Trattoria Lauretta — Tempelhof — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Sun 17:00–23:00 (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.5 (530)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=1808302106466398168&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A neighborhood trattoria whose reason to exist is its own fresh pasta, and a welcome address in a district that rarely makes food lists. The menu spreads across pasta and pizza, so it has less regional focus than a dedicated osteria, and published criticism is thin. Reasonable to treat it as a promising local, closer in spirit to Nini e Petirosso than a destination worth a long trip.

### Sissi Restaurant — Schöneberg — €€€

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 17:00–23:00; Fri–Sat 17:00–23:30; Sun 17:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.7 (1377)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=6400383924715615295&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Austrian classics done with discipline: the veal schnitzel is tender under a crisp breading, and the warm potato-cucumber salad and Saftgulasch back up the Viennese identity. This is a full-price evening, noticeably dearer than the everyday specialists the reader usually favors, and the dishes are heavy and traditional rather than exploratory. Worth it for the schnitzel; the themed interior is not the draw.

### Ripieno — Schöneberg — €€

**Hours:** Mon 04:00–21:30; Tue 12:00–21:30; Thu–Sun 12:00–21:30; (closed Wed)

**Rating:** 4.6 (678)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=4405641568888698269&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A focused pasta stop at the Lokdepot, not a full-service Italian. House-made pasta and sauces carry the concept, with the gricia and the oxtail ragù the best-supported orders. Tasting evidence covers selected dishes rather than the whole menu, and the narrow format suits two people better than a group with mixed tastes. More satisfying than ambitious.

### HAPPA — Kreuzberg — €€€ (good value)

**Hours:** Tue–Sat 18:00–22:00 (closed Mon, Sun)

**Rating:** 4.8 (462)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=946052033712709760&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Chef-led, fully vegan set menus with a low-waste approach, at roughly €40 for three courses. That price makes it the most accessible tasting-style meal in the city, but it is casual set-menu dining rather than a rival to Berlin's high-end kitchens, and the vegan, menu-led format leaves little room to choose. Confirm the current menu and price when booking.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Sissi Restaurant — the clearest execution of a classic, with schnitzel as the concrete reason to go.
2. Ripieno — the tightest concept of the week, pasta made in-house and cooked simply.
3. HAPPA — a complete chef-led meal at a price that is rare for this format.
