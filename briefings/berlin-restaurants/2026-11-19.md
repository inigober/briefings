# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-11-19

Cold-weather food this week: noodles and dumplings, a Thai curry kitchen, burek from a Wedding bakery, and tacos for the nights you can stand a queue. Most of it is cheap and most of it is spread well away from Mitte.

### Shaniu's House of Noodles — Wilmersdorf — € (good value)

**Hours:** Daily 12:00–22:00

**Rating:** 4.5 (2298)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=2072006413172091597&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Home-style noodle cooking with a Shanghainese lean and a few Korean touches, rather than another Sichuan bowl. The noodles and dumplings are made in-house, and the braised pork belly noodles and chili wontons are the orders to start with. Most soups come in around ten euros, which is hard to beat for this quality. The menu wanders beyond one region, and the room fills up, so book if you are going as a group.

### Tianfuzius — Schöneberg — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Fri 12:00–15:00 & 17:30–22:30; Sat–Sun 12:00–22:30; (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.5 (1309)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=9714500519315435327&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A fully plant-based Sichuan kitchen that takes the chili-and-peppercorn seasoning seriously. The hui guo rou, made meat-free and built on black beans, is the dish that shows what the cooks can do with a limited pantry. It is an adaptation, not a complete Sichuan menu, and some of the regional vegetables are hard to get in Berlin, so expect a cleverly reworked version of the cuisine. Worth it if you like Sichuan flavours and are curious how far they go without pork.

### Hanuman Thai Curry House — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Thu 12:00–15:30 & 17:00–22:00; Fri–Sun 12:00–22:00; (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.6 (414)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=10805004978977801651&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The curry list is short on purpose, and the jungle curry is the reason to come: fragrant, with real texture and Thai eggplants that hold their shape. Add miang kam and the homemade Thai sausage and you have a proper meal. One early review found the massaman too sweet, so order the jungle curry first and ask how sweet the massaman is before you commit. A strong choice for a flavour-first dinner on Skalitzer Straße.

### Sarajevo — Wedding — € (good value)

**Hours:** Daily 08:00–22:00

**Rating:** 4.8 (2700)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=11185880011603264770&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Burek is the draw here: thin layers, crisp edges, and fillings of potato, cheese or meat. The ćevapi come with house-made flatbread and are a good second order. Treat this as a focused burek-and-grill stop, not a full Balkan restaurant, and know that the savoury side of the menu is heavy on meat. Open from breakfast, so it works at almost any hour.

### Tacos El Rey — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Thu 17:00–21:30; Fri–Sun 12:00–21:30; (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.6 (874)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=10485830786403003835&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

Tortillas are pressed to order from house-made corn masa, which is the whole argument for the place and it holds up. Birria, ribeye and green chorizo each give you a different filling, and the salsas have punch. The hype brings queues at peak times, and since a meal is several tacos, the bill and the wait both add up. Go early on a weekday evening.

### JAJA — Neukölln — €€€

**Hours:** Tue–Sat 18:00–00:00 (closed Mon, Sun)

**Rating:** 4.2 (730)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=7161051372547181186&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A natural-wine neighbourhood room where the cooking is better than the setting suggests. Mussels with spaghetti and XO sauce and pork belly with red cabbage and horseradish are the plates that stand out. It is loud, and sharing plates plus wine make it easy to spend more than planned. Good for a group that wants to order widely, less so for a quiet conversation.

### 100Brote — Prenzlauer Berg — €

**Hours:** Mon–Sat 08:00–18:00; Sun 09:00–18:00

**Rating:** 3.8 (525)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=3559059768382278200&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A bakery that bakes its own organic rye, spelt and sourdough, and also does proper savoury food. The Kiezbrot with egg, cream cheese, mustard sauce and chives makes a solid lunch, and the warm soups mean it is more than a sweet-pastry stop. The bread is stronger than the sandwiches, and it closes at 18:00, so this is a breakfast or lunch place. The Google rating is modest; go for the loaves.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Sarajevo — for burek at nearly any hour, and a cheap, filling one.
2. Hanuman Thai Curry House — for a jungle curry dinner when you want real heat and fragrance.
3. Shaniu's House of Noodles — for a ten-euro bowl of noodles and a plate of dumplings on a cold evening.
