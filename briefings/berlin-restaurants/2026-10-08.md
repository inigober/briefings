# Berlin Restaurant Briefing — Week of 2026-10-08

This week is kitchens with a single job: Kantstraße Thai with a specials board, handmade khinkali in Charlottenburg, a Black Sea köfte sandwich on Oranienstraße, Kreuzberg pasta and seafood, Pfefferberg tacos after a long closure, and one fermentation-led German room on Oderberger Straße.

### Chon Thong — Charlottenburg — €€

**Hours:** Mon–Thu 12:00–22:00; Fri–Sat 12:00–23:00; Sun 12:00–22:00

**Rating:** 4.5 (1692)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=2216611833988736610&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Kantstraße Thai canteen whose case is still dish-level: duck larb, chicken rice with both fried and boiled chicken, and a specials board that goes past the usual Berlin curry-house list. Some specials are not in English, so the better meal often means asking rather than pointing, and the room is a canteen, not a composed evening. Against Khao Soi's weekend-only northern noodles two weeks ago, Khao Taan's family-style Friedrichshain dinner, Papaya's broader Winterfeldtstraße heat, or Dao's Kantstraße institution from early September, this is the west-side insider table — less of a single-bowl thesis, more of a repertoire if you order with intent. Worth the return if larb and the specials are the point; skip it if you want a translated menu and a leisurely sit-down.

### Sara & Gogi — Charlottenburg — €€

**Hours:** Mon–Fri 18:00–23:00; Sat–Sun 17:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.7 (643)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=8854182376057681088&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Suarezstraße Georgian-Israeli room whose useful argument is khinkali made to order, plus walnut-based Georgian starters and Israeli dips that actually feed a vegetarian table. The hybrid is the limit: this is not a strictly traditional Georgian menu, older published prices should be ignored, and weeknights start at 18:00. Against Genazvale's old-school Wilmersdorf staples from mid-August or Der blaue Fuchs's wine-bar Georgian in Prenzlauer Berg, Sara & Gogi is the west-side dumpling craft option — less institution, more mixed kitchen, easier if you want Caucasus cooking without another charcoal grill. Go for the khinkali; skip it if you came for a purist khachapuri-and-tabaka night.

### Akcaabat Saray Köfte — Kreuzberg — €

**Hours:** Mon–Fri 11:00–20:00; Sat 11:30–20:00; (closed Sun)

**Rating:** 4.8 (599)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=5464399245513953153&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

An Oranienstraße sandwich counter built around Akçaabat-style köfte: thicker, rounder Black Sea patties rather than the usual elongated kebab-shop meatball, best ordered large with everything and chili flakes. The detailed write-up is from 2020, so current execution is a hypothesis, the format is meat-and-bread with no flexibility for mixed diets, and it is done by 20:00, closed Sunday. Against last week's lokanta cooking at Mercan, Mira's Hermannstraße ocakbaşı, or the Skalitzer Adana grill-house sequence, this is the specialist snack that actually changes the Turkish picture — not another full grill meal. Useful as a cheap regional stop; ignore it if you want a table, mezze, and a long evening.

### Zum Heiligen Teufel — Kreuzberg — €€

**Hours:** Mon–Wed 18:00–00:00; Thu–Sat 12:00–00:00; (closed Sun)

**Rating:** 4.4 (522)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=16363010037190264852&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

A Muskauer Straße Italian neighborhood kitchen whose recent case is pasta execution and seafood doneness, with Bolognese and stracciatella as the safe anchors rather than a named regional thesis. The 4.4 across a mid-size review base is competent rather than pointed, coverage is thinner on failures than on praise, and Sunday is closed; Monday–Wednesday is dinner-only. Against last week's Donaustraße pasta at La Bolognina, Il Calice's product-led Charlottenburg table, or Nini e Petirosso as a personal Italian reference, this is the Kreuzberg sit-down: less of a west-side ingredient temple, more of an established room for pasta and fish. Worth it when that is the meal; skip it if you want a clearer regional Italian argument or a lunch window midweek.

### Taqueria El Oso — Prenzlauer Berg — €€

**Hours:** Tue–Thu 12:00–22:00; Fri–Sat 12:00–23:00; Sun 12:00–22:00; (closed Mon)

**Rating:** 4.5 (3121)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=398784497044115211&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The Pfefferberg taco counter, back after a 2025 water-damage closure: al pastor with smoky pork, pineapple, and salsa that is meant to be taken seriously, plus suadero, nopales, and meat or vegan chorizo. Queues are still part of the deal, a full taco run is not a snack, and Monday is closed. Against last week's tortas at Sabor a mí in Friedrichshain, this is the sit-down taco benchmark rather than a sandwich stop — familiar to this reader, which is the point after the closure, not a discovery claim. Go if al pastor is the craving; skip it if you already treat it as a regular and wanted a new kitchen this week.

### Otto — Prenzlauer Berg — €€€

**Hours:** Daily 18:00–23:00

**Rating:** 4.6 (668)

**Maps:** https://maps.google.com/?cid=14110376015699172966&g_mp=Cidnb29nbGUubWFwcy5wbGFjZXMudjEuUGxhY2VzLlNlYXJjaFRleHQQAhgEIAA

The week's only fine-dining entry: reduced seasonal German cooking on Oderberger Straße, fermentation and regional product as the technique, with a sharing menu or à la carte rather than a locked luxury set. The room is small and booked-out, the wine list leans natural and orange, and €€€ is still a planned evening rather than a weeknight default. Against last week's product-led French room at Matthias a few streets away, merold's Brandenburg-ingredient Neukölln kitchen, or Bieberbau's herb-and-spice Wilmersdorf set, Otto is the more fermentation-forward Prenzlauer Berg splurge — less tasting-menu theatre, more product and technique, still not the meal most of this list is for. Book it only if that is the night you want.

### This week's strongest bets

If I were choosing only three from this list:

1. Chon Thong — the clearest dish-level case this week: duck larb, chicken rice, and a specials board that is more specific than another Thai canteen.
2. Akcaabat Saray Köfte — the most useful cheap specialist, and the one that actually changes the Turkish picture after weeks of grills and lokantas.
3. Sara & Gogi — handmade khinkali on the west side, with enough of a Caucasus craft argument to justify the hybrid menu.
