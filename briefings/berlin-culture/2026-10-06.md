# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 7–13, 2026

The week is built from neighbour hangs and from forms that refuse a single hero: Hamburger Bahnhof opens *Next Door* with Dean, Nauman, Demand and the rest of that Rieckhallen circle; Cécile Bally premieres a choreography that treats video-game extras as the plot; Will Guthrie’s Nantes gamelan ensemble takes the Kuppelhalle on Wednesday. Around that: HAU’s *Love is a Verb #2*, Schlingensief at Gropius Bau, and a DEFA night of ecological films the GDR preferred not to show.

### Life is strange, choose your own

**Venue:** FELD Zentrale für junge Performance, Gleditschstraße 5, Schöneberg

**Date(s):** Saturday, 10 October – Tuesday, 13 October 2026 (premiere Saturday; the run continues Wednesday, 14 October at 10:00)

**Time(s):** Sat 18:00 (premiere); Sun 16:00; Tue 10:00. About 60 minutes, no spoken language, from age 12. After the performance the game can be played.

**Short Context:** Cécile Bally’s premiere is live performance plus video game, and the interest is the NPCs — the characters who wait, work, care, and keep a world running without ever being the hero. Asaf Aharonson and Sunayana Shetty take on loops, glitches, and the odd motor habits of digital extras; Leo G. Alonso maps video; Malu Peeters does sound; Lisa Kaschubat built the game with Café Pink in Schöneberg, which is the collaboration that actually justifies the “choose your own” in the title. Coproduction with FELD and C-TAKT; the DOCK 11 digital page is the listing, but the stage is FELD, not Kastanienallee. Saturday 18:00 is the one if you want the first night; Tuesday 10:00 is the early slot if the weekend is already HAU.

**Why It Fits:** Interdisciplinary choreography and participatory performance with named collaborators; a three-date in-week premiere rather than a workshop listing dressed as a show.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 / FELD — Life is strange, choose your own](https://dock11-berlin.de/digital/programm/on-tour-workshops/life-is-strange-choose-your-own)

### Ensemble Nist-Nah + Shouji Hara

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 7 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Will Guthrie’s Ensemble Nist-Nah — Charles Dubois, Mayomi Moreno, Elora Antolin, Sven Michel, Julien Ouvrard, Ellen Pelé — is a Nantes gamelan/percussion group that treats gongs, bowls, and drums as harmonic material, not as an ethnomusicology demonstration. The page names Javanese court time, Jathilan repetition, Dewa Alit’s acoustic glitch, then Roscoe Mitchell and Henry Threadgill, which is the right stack if the night stays a band rather than a lecture. Shouji Hara, Berlin-based, opens on shakuhachi: unnoticed things, neither active nor passive, music as making space. Wednesday is the one appointment; Ryoko Ono on Tuesday is a different night in the same hall, not a second Nist-Nah listing.

**Why It Fits:** Global experimental music, percussion, and free-adjacent ensemble practice with named artists at a priority music venue; a concrete Wednesday rather than a generic world-music billing.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Ensemble Nist-Nah + Shouji Hara](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/ensemble-nist-nah?tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=7&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026&cHash=2d98e8863bb0f5bb3ddd3d6b130bb637)
