# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of June 17–23, 2026

## Top Picks

### Tirailleurs: Trials and Tribulations

**Venue:** Haus der Kulturen der Welt (HKW)

**Date(s):** 21 March – 21 June 2026 (closes Saturday)

**Time(s):** Wed–Mon 11:00–19:00 (closed Tue); confirm with venue

**Short Context:** HKW's year-defining research exhibition traces the history of African soldiers (Tirailleurs) conscripted into European armies — from colonial recruitment through postcolonial aftermath. Archival documents, contemporary art, and scholarly collaboration frame a subject Berlin institutions rarely address at this scale. The final weekend of the run makes this the week's most urgent museum visit.

**Why It Fits:** Colonialism, historical trauma, and research-based artistic practice at a priority venue — closing within days.

**Official Link:** [HKW — Tirailleurs: Trials and Tribulations](https://www.hkw.de/en/program/projekte/tirailleurs-trials-and-tribulations)

### NO LIMIT (Angela Alves – NO LIMIT BALLET)

**Venue:** Hochzeitssaal, Sophiensæle

**Date(s):** 17–18 June 2026 (final performances)

**Time(s):** 20:00

**Short Context:** Angela Alves and NO LIMIT BALLET close a run that treats accessibility not as accommodation but as choreographic method — audio description and sign-language interpretation are integrated into the dramaturgy rather than bolted on. A rare instance of Berlin dance programming where disabled and non-disabled audiences share the same aesthetic frame.

**Why It Fits:** Contemporary dance with explicit accessibility politics; participatory and interdisciplinary performance practice.

**Official Link:** [Sophiensæle — programme PDF (June)](https://sophiensaele.com/de/download/5186/Programm%2004%2005%2006%2007%2008.pdf)

### Polish Art Week & Avant Art Festival Berlin

**Venue:** Multiple venues (opening at Panke Gallery; Silent Green, Arkaoda, Morphine Raum, Kunsthaus KuLe, and others)

**Date(s):** 22–28 June 2026

**Time(s):** Varies by event; opening evening 22 June at Panke Gallery

**Short Context:** The seventh edition pairs Polish Art Week with the Avant Art Festival for a city-wide programme of improvisation, noise, electroacoustic music, and audiovisual performance. The week opens with Women-Antennas' immersive installation *The Gospels of Aerial* at Panke Gallery and a live set by folk-experimental trio Tuleje; Alva Noto closes on 28 June at Zenner. Artists include Evicshen, Chris Pitsiokos, Marta Warelis, and Drew McDowall.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental electronic, sound art, and transmedia performance across priority venues — the week's densest concentration of leftfield music and hybrid formats.

**Official Link:** [Polish Art Week Berlin](https://polishartweek.com/)

### Cruising the Countryside – Queeres Leben auf dem Land

**Venue:** Schwules Museum

**Date(s):** 1 May – 2 November 2026

**Time(s):** Museum hours; confirm with venue

**Short Context:** A group exhibition excavates how queer publications and rural networks sustained identity before social media — mapping desire, isolation, and community across countryside landscapes. Archival ephemera and contemporary artistic responses situate queer pastoral life as a counter-narrative to urban-centric LGBTQ+ history.

**Why It Fits:** Queer history, archives, and diaspora experiences — strong thematic match at a specialist institution.

**Official Link:** [Schwules Museum — Cruising the Countryside](https://www.schwulesmuseum.de/ausstellung/cruising-the-countryside-queeres-leben-auf-dem-land/)

### Susan Sontag – Sehen und gesehen werden

**Venue:** Schwules Museum

**Date(s):** 12 June – 2 November 2026

**Time(s):** Museum hours; confirm with venue

**Short Context:** Opened last week alongside *Cruising the Countryside*, this exhibition stages Sontag's intellectual legacy through photography, correspondence, and critical reception — asking who looks, who is looked at, and how camp functions as aesthetic politics. Timely companion to the rural-queer archival show in the adjacent galleries.

**Why It Fits:** Feminism, queer theory, and visual culture — intellectual history treated as exhibition material rather than biography.

**Official Link:** [Schwules Museum — Susan Sontag](https://www.schwulesmuseum.de/ausstellung/susan-sontag-sehen-oder-gesehen-werden/)

## Film & Screenings

### Meine Frau Weint

**Venue:** Wolf Kino

**Date(s):** Ongoing from 11 June 2026

**Time(s):** Check venue for daily screenings

**Short Context:** Angela Schanelec's Berlinale competition entry follows a crane operator whose wife's hospital confession — involving a dance partner killed in a car accident — unravels their marriage in oblique, precisely framed scenes. Less plot-driven than *Music*, the film investigates how language fails and recovers in urban intimacy. Schanelec's most acclaimed work in years.

**Why It Fits:** Contemporary international art-house cinema with gender and urbanism themes; Berlinale-premiered work still in local release.

**Official Link:** [Wolf Kino — Meine Frau Weint](https://wolfberlin.org/de/programm/filme/meine-frau-weint)
