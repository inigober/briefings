# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of June 17–23, 2026

## Top Picks

### Shilpa Gupta — What Still Holds

**Venue:** Hamburger Bahnhof – Nationalgalerie der Gegenwart

**Date(s):** 27 March 2026 – 3 January 2027

**Time(s):** Tue–Fri 10:00–18:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–18:00 (closed Mon)

**Short Context:** Gupta's solo show turns language into architecture and archive: visitors walk through an out-of-order monumental "TRUTH," browse a library of 100 books published under pseudonyms, and watch split-flap boards cycle through the artist's reflections. The exhibition is staged in dialogue with Joseph Beuys's permanent works — a sustained inquiry into who gets to speak, under what constraints, and at what scale.

**Why It Fits:** Research-based contemporary practice at the intersection of politics, archives, and media — strong match for identity, censorship, and technology-and-power themes.

**Official Link:** [Hamburger Bahnhof — Shilpa Gupta: What Still Holds](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/hamburger-bahnhof/home/)

### Bajo las banderas, el sol + Noites Paraguayas

**Venue:** Arsenal Filminstitut

**Date(s):** Tuesday, 17 June 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00 (Bajo las banderas, el sol); 21:15 (Noites Paraguayas)

**Short Context:** A double bill pairing Juanjo Pereira's new Paraguayan-Argentine feature with Aloysio Raulino's 1982 Brazil-set companion piece. Pereira is present for a post-screening discussion — rare chance to see contemporary Southern Cone cinema in 35mm/DCP programming with filmmaker conversation at Arsenal.

**Why It Fits:** Politically engaged international cinema with live discourse; exactly the kind of under-programmed geographic perspective the film section should surface.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal — June 2026 programme](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/en/cinema/)

### Hrvatska Radiotelevizija: Radio Symphony, Radio Drama, Radio Art

**Venue:** n.b.k. (Neuer Berliner Kunstverein)

**Date(s):** 1 March – 7 July 2026 (closing soon)

**Time(s):** Tue–Sun 12:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Curated by Ute Holl, this survey excavates experimental radio practices from Croatian public broadcasting — a media-archaeological look at sound art, drama, and symphony programming as cultural infrastructure. One of the few Berlin exhibitions treating radio as artistic and political form rather than nostalgia.

**Why It Fits:** Sound art, media art, and Eastern European histories — closing within three weeks, so worth prioritising now.

**Official Link:** [n.b.k. — Hrvatska Radiotelevizija](https://www.nbk.org/en/ausstellungen/hrvatska-radiotelevizija.html)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Cruising the Countryside – Rural Queer Lives

**Venue:** Schwules Museum

**Date(s):** 1 May – 2 November 2026

**Time(s):** Wed–Mon 14:00–18:00 (closed Tue); check venue for holiday hours

**Short Context:** A research-heavy group show on queer life beyond Berlin — zines, flyers, documentary films, and everyday objects chart rural networks from Brandenburg farming collectives to 1980s anti-war camps. Less spectacle than the museum's headline shows, but conceptually sharp on solidarity outside urban queer hubs.

**Why It Fits:** LGBTQ+ history, archives, and diaspora/rural experience — fills a gap the week's event listings often miss.

**Official Link:** [Schwules Museum — Cruising the Countryside](https://schwulesmuseum.de/)
