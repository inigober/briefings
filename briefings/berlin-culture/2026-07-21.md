# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of July 22–28, 2026

Midsummer Berlin thins into a week of finissages and reopenings: PSM closes two summer shows Thursday before Zilberman Unfold and EIGEN + ART Lab relaunch the gallery circuit, while Sinema Transtopia anchors the moving-image calendar with queer diaspora programming and Leyla Bouzid's open-air premiere. The Kyiv Biennial's Saturday overview tour at KW offers the week's most sustained encounter with geopolitical contemporary art.

## Top Picks

**Venue:** SİNEMA TRANSTOPIA

**Date(s):** Thursday, 23 July 2026

### Fiona Mackay — 40's

**Venue:** time is it in the world PSM

**Date(s):** 12 June – 24 July 2026 (closes Thursday)

**Time(s):** Wed–Sat 12:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Fiona Mackay's *40's* unfolds in two parts across PSM, juxtaposing a series centred on the male body with works shaped by the unstable life stage of the early forties — a condition saturated by popular culture, clichés, and social expectation. Mackay seizes familiar codes of power — strength, vanity, aggression, godlike idealisation — without seeking fixed meaning, drifting between figuration and abstraction, attraction and refusal. The exhibition closes Thursday with *Gentle Parenting*; together they mark the end of PSM's summer pair before the gallery calendar shifts toward Unfold week.

**Why It Fits:** Contemporary art, gender, and identity at an established independent gallery; closing-soon urgency paired with Semmer at the same venue.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — 40's](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/44218/40-s)
