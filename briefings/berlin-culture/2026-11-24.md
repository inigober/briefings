# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of November 25–December 1, 2026

Performance and its afterlives run through the week: Ulay and Marina Abramović arrive at the Neue Nationalgalerie with a live *Imponderabilia*, while HAU stages two works that wire the body to technology and myth. The Bienal de São Paulo's Berlin edition at HKW runs through early December, and a Kreuzberg artist talk and two screenings round out a film-and-conversation-heavy week.

## Top Picks

### Ulay, Ulay & Abramović

**Venue:** Neue Nationalgalerie, Potsdamer Straße 50

**Date(s):** Opening Friday, 27 November 2026, 19:00; on view 28 November 2026 – 2 May 2027

**Time(s):** Tue, Wed, Fri–Sun 10:00–18:00; Thu 10:00–20:00; closed Monday. *Imponderabilia* is performed live daily, 15:00–16:30

**Short Context:** Marking fifty years since Ulay's 1976 action at the Nationalgalerie, in which he took Carl Spitzweg's *The Poor Poet* to the home of a guest-worker family in Kreuzberg, the show follows his early photography and his twelve-year partnership with Marina Abramović (1976–1988). Videos, photographs, drawings and letters sit alongside the live re-presentation of *Imponderabilia* (1977). The museum flags explicit nudity and disturbing material.

**Why It Fits:** Performance and conceptual art read through institutional power, migration and the body, in a museum that is itself the subject of the founding gesture.

**Official Link:** [Neue Nationalgalerie — Ulay, Ulay & Abramović](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/nationalgalerie/exhibitions/detail/ulay-ulay-abramovic/)

### Interrobang — Enter the Blackbox

**Venue:** HAU3, Tempelhofer Ufer 10

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 25 November (premiere) – Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Time(s):** 25 Nov 20:00 (German); 26 Nov 19:00 (German); 27 Nov 20:00 (German); 28 Nov 20:30 (English); about 75 minutes

**Short Context:** A participatory performance in which the audience becomes part of a human–machine AI system. Spectators act as "tokens", the small units AI systems compute with, and steer the system through movement and responses. Bring a fully charged smartphone, or borrow a tablet on site.

**Why It Fits:** Interdisciplinary, interactive performance that puts the social stakes of artificial intelligence into the audience's own bodies rather than onto a screen.

**Official Link:** [HAU — Interrobang: Enter the Blackbox](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/programme/pdetail/interrobang-enter-the-blackbox/)

### Not All Travellers Walk Roads—Of Humanity as Practice

**Venue:** Haus der Kulturen der Welt, John-Foster-Dulles-Allee 10

**Date(s):** On view 12 September – 6 December 2026

**Time(s):** Opening hours not listed on the exhibition page; check HKW before visiting

**Short Context:** The abridged Berlin edition of the 36th Bienal de São Paulo, and the first outing of the Bienal in Germany. Installations, performances, sound works and spatial interventions by 34 artists, among them Forensic Architecture, Lynn Hershman Leeson, Cevdet Erek, Theo Eshetu, Julianknxx and Wolfgang Tillmans, treat humanity as a practice that can challenge colonial and extractive inheritances. The final fortnight is the last chance to see it.

**Why It Fits:** A large international group show with a decolonial and ecological frame, with sound and performance carrying as much weight as objects.

**Official Link:** [HKW — Not All Travellers Walk Roads](https://www.hkw.de/en/programme/not-all-travellers-walk-roads-of-humanity-as-practice)

## Film & Screenings

### Dua

**Venue:** SİNEMA TRANSTOPIA, Lindower Straße 20/22, Haus C

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 25 November 2026

**Time(s):** 20:00

**Short Context:** Blerta Basholli's 2026 Kosovo–Switzerland–France production follows 13-year-old Dua in late-1990s Pristina as war approaches. Friendship and first love give way to a changed view of the violence around her, while her family weighs leaving Kosovo. It screens in the venue's SİNELECTION programme.

**Why It Fits:** New international cinema from a director whose work centres women's lives under political pressure, shown at an independent venue.

**Official Link:** [SİNEMA TRANSTOPIA — Dua](https://sinematranstopia.com/en/sinelection/dua)

### KİM MİHRİ (Who Is Mihri)

**Venue:** SİNEMA TRANSTOPIA, Lindower Straße 20/22, Haus C

**Date(s):** Saturday, 28 November 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00; doors one hour earlier; registration via the event page

**Short Context:** Berna Gençalp's 89-minute documentary (Turkish with English subtitles) traces Mihri Hanım, the painter who founded the first art school for women in the Ottoman Empire and worked across three continents. It asks why she is remembered for her aristocratic background and marriages rather than her work. A talk with Dr. Gülşah Stapel and Ayça Bayrak Uluğ follows, with discussion continuing at HANE.

**Why It Fits:** Feminist art history and the recovery of a sidelined woman artist, in the "Kİm? Women Artists Reframed" series.

**Official Link:** [SİNEMA TRANSTOPIA — KİM MİHRİ](https://sinematranstopia.com/en/kim-women-artists-reframed/kim-mihri-who-is-mihri)

## Performing Arts

### Kat Válastur — Dive into You

**Venue:** HAU1, Stresemannstraße 29

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 25 November 2026 (re-run)

**Time(s):** 19:00; about 60 minutes

**Short Context:** A solo in which Válastur sets archaic female oracles beside the photosynthesis and growth of trees. Percussive choreography draws on the deep tones of the semantron, with music by Aho Ssan and Válastur's own vocals. The staging uses strobe lights, fog and loud music.

**Why It Fits:** Contemporary choreography with an electronic-music crossover, mixing myth and science-fiction imagery.

**Official Link:** [HAU — Dive into You](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/programme/pdetail/kat-valastur-dive-into-you/)
