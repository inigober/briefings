# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of November 18–24, 2026

Sound and improvisation carry the week: Horse Lords and Angel Bat Dawid at silent green, and a church-hall performance by guitarist Stian Westerhus with A K Dolven. Daniela Comani's rewritten library opens at the Kulturforum, and a dance festival centred on queer, trans and BIPoC artists brings Ishmael Houston-Jones to DOCK 11.

## Top Picks

### Psychic Liberation Night: Horse Lords + Audrey Chen & Nick Klein

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Betonhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 18 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Horse Lords, formed in Baltimore in 2010 and partly based in Germany since 2021, fold noise, leftfield rock and minimalist and electronic composition into one quartet sound. Their latest album is *Demand To Be Taken To Heaven Alive!* (RVNG Intl.). Audrey Chen and Nick Klein open with improvised sound work that moves between organic and inorganic material.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental and improvised music with an electronic edge, in the venue's most adventurous series; the double bill sets a composed-leaning band against free improvisation.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Psychic Liberation Night](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/psychic-liberation-night-horse-lords-audrey-chen-nick-klein)

### Here/Now/There/Then

**Venue:** DOCK 11, Kastanienallee 79

**Date(s):** Thursday, 19 – Sunday, 22 November 2026

**Time(s):** 19:30 each evening (60 minutes); artist talk in English after the 19 November performance

**Short Context:** Ishmael Houston-Jones directs an evening of experimental choreography built on improvised scores, with movement and spoken improvisation drawing on personal biography and social justice. He works with Tis Aly, Skyla Miles, Yusa, Renan Manhães and Vivian Ngozi Aghmelu. It is part of the Emerging Change Dance Festival, which centres queer, trans, Black, Indigenous and BIPoC dance artists.

**Why It Fits:** Improvisation-based performance by a central figure of New York experimental dance, with a strong identity and politics dimension; the opening-night talk adds context.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Here/Now/There/Then](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/theater/program/calendar/here-now-there-then)

### Daniela Comani. Orlando's Library

**Venue:** Kunstbibliothek, foyer, Kulturforum, Matthäikirchplatz

**Date(s):** On view 17 November 2026 – 8 March 2027 (opened Tuesday)

**Time(s):** Free admission; special exhibitions open Tuesday–Sunday, closed Monday (Kunstbibliothek hours: Monday–Friday 09:00–20:00, Saturday and Sunday 10:00–18:00)

**Short Context:** Comani's site-specific installation for the Kunstbibliothek foyer is part of her *Orlando's Library* series, begun in 2021. It consists of photographed bookshelves of novels and monographs on which she has subtly altered the titles on the covers, proposing other possible literatures.

**Why It Fits:** A quiet intervention into the literary canon and who gets to shape it, directly in line with feminist and cultural-memory interests, and it is free.

**Official Link:** [SMB — Daniela Comani. Orlando's Library](https://www.smb.museum/en/museums-institutions/kulturforum/exhibitions/detail/daniela-comani-orlandos-library/)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Crosstown — Robert Rauschenberg

**Venue:** BASTIAN, Taylorstraße 1, Dahlem

**Date(s):** On view 10 September – 21 November 2026 (closes Saturday)

**Time(s):** Tuesday–Saturday, 11:00–17:00

**Short Context:** The centrepiece is Rauschenberg's large panoramic work *Crosstown* (1997), made by transferring many photographic images onto a polylaminate surface with vegetable dyes and combining them with painted elements. The gallery frames it as an early allegory of an endless multitude of images, and places it in the context of his 1964 Venice Biennale Grand Prize.

**Why It Fits:** Image-saturation and the layering of urban photographs are the theme; this is the last chance to see it.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Crosstown](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45037/crosstown)

### Sound Bodies – Variations on a Theme of Art

**Venue:** Bode-Museum, Monbijoubrücke

**Date(s):** On view 24 November 2026 – 7 March 2027 (opens Tuesday)

**Time(s):** Opening day 24 November, 10:00–17:00

**Short Context:** A musical intervention that connects sculpture, instruments, sound and movement across the museum's collection. Antonio Canova is named through an exhibited work; no contemporary artist line-up has been announced.

**Why It Fits:** A historical take on sound and the body in sculpture, a useful counterpoint to the week's contemporary sound programme, though more art-historical than experimental.

**Official Link:** [SMB — Sound Bodies](https://www.smb.museum/en/exhibitions/detail/sound-bodies-variations-on-a-theme-of-art/)

## Music

### Angel Bat Dawid

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Tuesday, 24 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** The Chicago composer, clarinettist, pianist and conductor blends classical composition, gospel, jazz and avant-garde improvisation with speculative aesthetics and archival research. She leads The Brothahood and Sistazz of the Nitty Gritty, plays in Damon Locks' Black Monument Ensemble, and has appeared at the Barbican, the Elbphilharmonie and Jazzfest Berlin. Further performers and repertoire have not been announced.

**Why It Fits:** Afrofuturist, archive-driven improvisation rooted in Black music history, an artistic match for the week's sound theme.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Angel Bat Dawid](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/angel-bat-dawid)

**Venue:** St. Matthäus-Kirche, Matthäikirchplatz

**Date(s):** Thursday, 19 November 2026

**Time(s):** 20:00

**Short Context:** A concert-performance in dialogue with Norwegian artist A K Dolven's exhibition *four hundred million years of sunrise from the dark*, with the experimental guitarist Stian Westerhus and Dolven herself. The venue lists no further programme details.
