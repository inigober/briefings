# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of November 4–10, 2026

A quieter week that leans on memory and quiet sound: Susan Hiller's dream-and-archive survey at KW, a documentary trilogy on the poet Gertrud Kolmar timed to 9 November, and two silent green concerts built on repetition and drone. Smaller entries round it out with Lithuanian shorts and a photography show that closes on Saturday.

## Top Picks

### Susan Hiller — Bad Dreams

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00; closed Tuesdays. English-language overview tour Friday, 6 November, 17:30–18:30 (also covers the Koo Jeong A and Rafik Greiss shows). Free Thursday on 12 November, 16:00–21:00.

**Short Context:** Hiller's first Berlin survey, built around collective imaginaries, cultural memory, mass media, dreams and altered consciousness. Last month's opening week was covered here; the overview tour makes this a good week to see it with a guide.

**Why It Fits:** Conceptual, media-based work on memory and the unconscious, from an artist whose archival methods anticipate much current practice.

**Official Link:** [KW — Susan Hiller: Bad Dreams](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/susan-hiller-bad-dreams)

### Ana Roxanne + Clarice

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Betonhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Monday, 9 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Ana Roxanne, a composer and singer now based in New York, makes slow, melancholic music that blends jazz, minimalism, folk and ambient textures; her album *Poem 1* is her most personal so far. Clarice (Emma Weber), joined by Finn Kraft, works in minimalist experimental folk that treats voice, sound and silence as equal material.

**Why It Fits:** Ambient and drone-adjacent songwriting from named artists, with two distinct approaches to restraint.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Ana Roxanne + Clarice](https://www.silent-green.net/programm/detail/ana-roxanne-1?cHash=a988557a4c9204f306b476d431f478a3&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=9&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=11&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

### Herzbergmaschine — documentary trilogy, with Sven Boeck

**Venue:** ACUDkino, Kino 2, Veteranenstraße 21

**Date(s):** Monday, 9 November 2026

**Time(s):** 18:00; 140 minutes, followed by a talk with the director. German version.

**Short Context:** Sven Boeck's 2019 trilogy follows the poet Gertrud Kolmar, a forced labourer in a Berlin cardboard factory from 1941 to 1943, through the city's places; then her sister Hilde Wenzel's dream diary in Swiss exile; and finally Kolmar's niece Sabina Wenzel recalling the family's exile in Paraty, Brazil. Boeck attends.

**Why It Fits:** Documentary on archives, exile and Berlin history, screened on the anniversary of the 1938 November pogroms.

**Official Link:** [ACUDkino — Herzbergmaschine](https://acudkino.de/Programm/herzbergmaschine/19621/58966)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Carina Brandes — Hauntings

**Venue:** BQ, Weydingerstraße 10, Mitte

**Date(s):** 11 September – 7 November 2026 (closes Saturday)

**Time(s):** Gallery hours not confirmed; check the listing.

**Short Context:** Brandes combines photography, performance and collage, using her own body and self-timer actions to test physical and symbolic limits. She cuts up photographic surfaces and reassembles them so that her body remains visible in the gaps, producing dreamlike, cinematic sequences.

**Why It Fits:** Photographic and performative work on the body and the uncanny; a short window to see it, and it pairs with Hiller's dream themes.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Hauntings](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45044/hauntings)

## Film & Screenings

### Special Programme "Come Closer"

**Venue:** ACUDkino, Kino 1, Veteranenstraße 21

**Date(s):** Thursday, 5 November 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00; about 90 minutes, followed by a Q&A. Original versions with English subtitles.

**Short Context:** Six Lithuanian shorts from 2019–2024 for the 16th Litauisches Kino goes Berlin, mixing documentary and animation: Laurynas Bareiša's *Dummy*, Birutė Sodeikaitė's puppet film *In Perpetuum*, Saulė Bliuvaitė's *Limousine*, and Saulius Baradinskas's *Techno, Mama*, in which a teenager dreams of Berghain.

**Why It Fits:** A compact look at a new generation of Baltic filmmakers working in short and hybrid forms.

**Official Link:** [ACUDkino — Special Programme "Come Closer"](https://acudkino.de/Programm/special_programme_come_closer/20577)

## Music

### goat (jp) + Camila Nebbia

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Betonhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 4 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** The Japanese quintet goat, formed in 2013 by Koshiro Hino, swaps instruments within songs and builds near-trance repetition from muted sounds and tones outside the twelve-tone scale. Berlin-based saxophonist Camila Nebbia presents her new solo record on Kassiani Records.

**Why It Fits:** Minimalist repetition and free improvisation on one bill.

**Official Link:** [silent green — goat (jp) + Camila Nebbia](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/goat-jp?cHash=fa8983364cd5c68d5c957c597e0d73d1&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=4&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=11&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)
