# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of September 16–22, 2026

Art Week’s booth crawl is over; the spatial argument remains. Vilanismo turns SAVVY into a Wedding echo of São Paulo’s peripheries, Hoyoung Im premieres a four-night interdisciplinary run at DOCK 11, and Burnt Friedman takes Jaki Liebezeit’s odd-meter practice into the Kuppelhalle. Around that triangle: geological light in a Kulturforum church, a Weißensee cosmology opening, and a shorts programme about systems that have already lost their grip.

## Top Picks

### ARCHITECTURAL ECHOTONES. A Poetics Of The Surrounds

**Venue:** SAVVY Contemporary, Wedding

**Date(s):** On view 11 September – 22 November 2026 (opened 10 September)

**Time(s):** Thursday–Sunday 14:00–19:00; free entry, donations welcome

**Short Context:** SAVVY hands its Wedding rooms to Vilanismo — a São Paulo brotherhood of ten Black artists (Ayọ̀kàndé, Carinhoso, Denis Moreira, Diego Crux, Guto Oca, Rafa Black, Ramo, Renan Teles, Robson Marques, Rodrigo Zaim) joined here by Helcio Barros and others — as a laboratory rather than a vitrine. The cue is the 36th São Paulo Biennial’s travelling programme and AbdouMaliq Simone’s *Surrounds*: peripheries as sites of invention, not as a German *Brennpunkt* caption. Installation, painting, video, and on-site performance occupy the same rooms as a working studio; the HKW’s Berlin edition of the biennial (*Not All Travellers Walk Roads*, 12 September – 6 December) is the institutional sibling, not a second listing. Thursday–Sunday hours are the appointment; the opening-week activations are already behind you.

**Why It Fits:** Research-based contemporary art, urbanism, and diaspora practice at a priority venue; named artists working spatial politics rather than a generic post–Art Week group hang.

**Official Link:** [SAVVY Contemporary — ARCHITECTURAL ECHOTONES](https://savvy-contemporary.com/en/events/2026/architectural-echotones/)

### Burnt Friedman pres. Secret Rhythms

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Saturday, 19 September 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Friedman brings *Secret Rhythms* into the Kuppelhalle as live synthesizer and mixing-desk work over a multichannel system: odd-meter grooves and sequences that were developed with Can drummer Jaki Liebezeit across a seventeen-year collaboration, presented as physically felt movement rather than as a krautrock tribute set. The claim is that complex cyclical patterns can read as natural relationships once they leave style and idiom behind — trance as structure, not as décor. Saturday is the one appointment; Carla dal Forno on Tuesday is a different night, not a second Friedman listing.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental electronic practice with a named artist at a priority music venue; rhythm as electroacoustic space rather than a generic DJ billing.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Burnt Friedman pres. Secret Rhythms](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/burnt-friedman-pres-secret-rhythms?tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=19&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=9&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026&cHash=b93a718ca3cdc7b0db6800406f7ad376)

## Film & Screenings

### Kontrollverlust — Shorts Attack September 2026

**Venue:** ACUDkino, Kino 1

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 16 September 2026

**Time(s):** 21:15; introduction by interfilm Berlin; OmU; 85 minutes

**Short Context:** The 285th *Shorts Attack* is not a repeat of July’s edition: nine films under the heading *Kontrollverlust*, built around system error, overwork, and control that has already slipped — at work, in restaurants, in reproduction, in the democratic room, in finance. Fridtjof Stensæth Josefsen and Morten Borgestad’s waiter-who-will-not-come grotesque sits next to Gentoku Yamada’s shutdown of a “human farm,” Suncana Brkulj’s animated commons, a 2015 Jean-Paul Frenay still-life of technological dependence, Elias Weinberger and Ferdinand Ehrhardt on a fascist smear in the hometown, Gerardo Del Razo’s one-shot protection-money run, Jon Frickey’s moth-and-vector-graphics loop, Xandra Popescu’s post-catastrophe reproduction tech, and a two-minute ping-pong catastrophe. Winking, then stringent; the theme is the reason to go, not the monthly brand.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental and socially pointed short film with a named monthly programme and a post-screening frame; political cinema as a set of incidents rather than a single feature.

**Official Link:** [ACUDkino — Kontrollverlust](https://acudkino.de/Programm/kontrollverlust/20534)

### RAGE Thormbones & Etienne Nillesen | Ryan Packard

**Venue:** KM28, Karl-Marx-Straße 28

**Date(s):** Saturday, 19 September 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 20:00; start 20:30; entry by donation

**Short Context:** Weston Olencki and Mattie Barbier (RAGE Thormbones) with Etienne Nillesen on extended snare: two trombones as organic air compressors, sculpting high-density mass instead of brass virtuosity, against snare overtones and interference patterns that change with where you stand. Same Saturday as Friedman at silent green — geographically and sonically a different room. If you want free-improvisation physicality rather than multichannel groove, this is the one.

**Why It Fits:** Free improvisation, sound art, and electroacoustic-adjacent listening with named artists; a donation-door KM28 night rather than a second silent green listing.
