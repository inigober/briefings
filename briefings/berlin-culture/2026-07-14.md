# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of July 15–21, 2026

Mid-July concentrates into three overlapping currents: Radialsystem's Forecast Festival as the week's interdisciplinary anchor, Bjørn Melhus's apocalyptic media archaeology opening at Silent Green, and a pair of gallery finissages on Saturday before the visual-art calendar thins further. Migration politics bookend the week — Adam Hanieh's KW lecture Wednesday, Wolf Kino's Lampedusa documentary premiere Sunday.

## Top Picks

### Forecast Festival 2026

**Venue:** Radialsystem

**Date(s):** Thursday, 16 July – Sunday, 19 July 2026

**Time(s):** Thu/Fri from 19:00; Sat from 17:00; Sun from 11:00 — confirm daily schedule with venue

**Short Context:** Forecast returns to Radialsystem as Berlin's most sustained platform for emerging performance, experimental music, and interdisciplinary practice. The four-day festival gathers LINA fellows and alumni alongside international mentors — Heinali, Vidura Bandara Rajapaksa, Lucy McRae, Almagul Menlibayeva, Tom Cassani, Keren Cytter, Emilie Largier, and Hsiang-Sheng Teng among them — in a format that treats presentation, workshop, and audience activation as coequal. Thursday opens with Lucy McRae and Tom Cassani's nominee presentations; Friday shifts to Almagul Menlibayeva and Heinali; Saturday to Keren Cytter and Vidura Bandara Rajapaksa; Sunday closes with LINA fellow activations. Less a conventional festival bill than a laboratory for how performance research travels between mentorship networks and public encounter.

**Why It Fits:** Interdisciplinary performance, experimental music, and participatory formats at a priority venue; international programming with named artists across contemporary dance, sound art, and expanded performance.

**Official Link:** [Radialsystem — Forecast Festival 2026](https://www.radialsystem.de/en/veranstaltungen/forecast-festival-2026/)

### Bjørn Melhus: LOST IN FINITY

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier (Betonhalle; opening evening across Betonhalle and lawn)

**Date(s):** Opening Thursday, 16 July 2026; exhibition 17 July – 23 August 2026

**Time(s):** Opening doors 19:00, start 19:30 (16 July); exhibition Tue–Fri 14:00–20:00, Sat–Sun 11:00–20:00

**Short Context:** Silent Green dedicates its summer Betonhalle to a major Bjørn Melhus solo — an immersive installation staging the medial unconscious of the 20th and 21st centuries through recombined excerpts from 35 years of video work. Religious imagery collides with pop-cultural end-time visions and technoid exit strategies in a circuit Melhus describes as responding to multicrisis with melancholic tragicomedy. Central is *REVELATION* (2024), created at Villa Massimo and shown in Berlin for the first time, weaving Book of Revelation motifs with contemporary visual codes. The opening evening launches a larger programme of artist talks, apocalyptic film screenings (*Melancholia*, Joshua Oppenheimer's *The End*, *The Wizard of Oz*), and lecture-performances running through August.

**Why It Fits:** Video art, sound art, and research-based media practice at a priority music venue; apocalyptic narratives, technology and power, and environmental crisis as explicit thematic material.

**Official Link:** [Silent Green — Bjørn Melhus: LOST IN FINITY](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/bjoern-melhus-lost-in-finity?tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=17&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=7&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026&cHash=23e6da090cfa4998b5d5332562aea309)

### Deutschlandpremiere: Only Ghosts in the Waves + Gespräch

**Venue:** Wolf Kino

**Date(s):** Sunday, 20 July 2026

**Time(s):** 18:30 (screening and discussion)

**Short Context:** Wolf Kino hosts the German premiere of a documentary addressing the anonymous graves on Lampedusa — the Mediterranean island where migration routes converge and where unidentified bodies accumulate without adequate memorial or forensic attention. The screening is followed by a discussion, treating the cinema as a forum for migration politics rather than a passive screening slot. Wolf's programme consistently favours research-driven documentary over festival-circuit packaging; this premiere fits that curatorial line.

**Why It Fits:** Socially engaged documentary, migration, and borders; post-screening discussion format at a priority arthouse cinema.

**Official Link:** [Wolf Kino — programme](https://wolfberlin.org/de/programm)

### Anne Duk Hee Jordan — Riders on the Storm

**Venue:** alexander levy

**Date(s):** 1 May – 18 July 2026 (closes Saturday)

**Time(s):** Wed–Sat 11:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Anne Duk Hee Jordan's second solo at alexander levy treats ecosystems as dynamic, interwoven processes rather than stable objects of contemplation. The multisensory installation immerses visitors in ecological relations that unfold relationally — autonomous forms that remain conceptually entangled, mirroring how natural events cascade rather than occur in isolation. Jordan's practice has long worked at the intersection of installation, ecology, and feminist materialism; this show closes Saturday, making it the week's most urgent gallery appointment.

**Why It Fits:** Research-based artistic practice, ecology, and interdisciplinary installation; closing-soon urgency at an established gallery.

## Wildcards

### Adam Hanieh: The Middle East in an Age of War and Climate Breakdown

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 15 July 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00–20:30

**Short Context:** Political economist Adam Hanieh delivers a lecture at KW on how war and climate breakdown intersect across the Middle East — a topic that connects geopolitics, resource extraction, and environmental justice in a single analytical frame. Hanieh's scholarship treats the region not as a conflict zone to be explained away but as a laboratory for understanding how late capitalism reorganises territory under conditions of permanent crisis. KW's Auguststraße complex provides the institutional frame for public intellectual exchange at the intersection of art and political economy.

**Why It Fits:** Geopolitics, climate, and decolonial thought in a research-lecture format at a priority contemporary-art institution.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Adam Hanieh lecture](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/44911/adam-hanieh-the-middle-east-in-an-age-of-war-and-climate-breakdown)
