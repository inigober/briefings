# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 28–November 3, 2026

A week organised around sound and the machinery of attention: Hamburger Bahnhof gives Ryuichi Sakamoto's installations a full retrospective, silent green pairs an acoustic Ulla set with a spatialised percussion piece, and HAU3 stages theatre inside a video game about exhaustion. Around them, two gallery shows close within days and a Maya Deren screening at KW rounds out the film side.

## Top Picks

### Ryuichi Sakamoto — seeing sound, hearing time

**Venue:** Hamburger Bahnhof – Nationalgalerie der Gegenwart, Invalidenstraße 50

**Date(s):** On view 11 September 2026 – 20 June 2027

**Time(s):** Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday 10:00–18:00; Thursday 10:00–20:00; Saturday and Sunday 11:00–18:00; closed Mondays. From 1 December the Thursday late opening ends.

**Short Context:** The first major European retrospective of the composer and artist Ryuichi Sakamoto (1952–2023): seven works in five large-scale installations across roughly 1,500 square metres of the Rieckhallen. Many were made with Shiro Takatani, Carsten Nicolai and Apichatpong Weerasethakul. One installation turns a piano damaged in the 2011 tsunami into a sound object, driven by converted seismological data from earthquakes worldwide.

**Why It Fits:** Sound art and audiovisual installation at a major institution, with a run long enough to visit on a quiet weekday; Thursday is the late evening.

**Official Link:** [Hamburger Bahnhof — Ryuichi Sakamoto](https://www.smb.museum/museen-einrichtungen/hamburger-bahnhof/ausstellungen/detail/ryuichi-sakamoto)

### Temple of Faitiche pres. U.e. (Ulla) + Martin Brandlmayr

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Thursday, 29 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Ulla, known for ambient music with gliding electronics, plays a solo set using only acoustic instruments for the first time, following the release *Hometown Girl* with its clean-toned guitar, woodwinds and piano. Martin Brandlmayr, a drummer and composer working between improvisation and composition, presents a new electroacoustic work for a spatially distributed ensemble of percussion instruments.

**Why It Fits:** Electroacoustic and ambient work from named artists, with two different answers to the question of what an acoustic instrument does in an electronic context.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Temple of Faitiche pres. U.e. (Ulla) + Martin Brandlmayr](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/temple-of-faitiche-pres-ulla-martin-brandlmayr?cHash=7554cae0ffb597752975ad8839c480b3&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=29&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

### Let's Play I Am Old and Tired — Total Refusal & Theater im Bahnhof

**Venue:** HAU3, Tempelhofer Ufer 10 (third floor, stairs only; not barrier-free)

**Date(s):** Wednesday 28 and Thursday 29 October 2026

**Time(s):** 28 October 19:00; 29 October 20:00. About 90 minutes. German and English; €15, reduced €9. A German-language artist talk with Total Refusal, Theater im Bahnhof and Heimo Lattner, moderated by Sarah Reimann, follows the 29 October performance.

**Short Context:** A game-and-performance hybrid in which gaming laptops and live direction stage theatre inside a video game. Its hero is an exhausted, ageing figure who wants a quiet life in the game's mountains rather than another heroic quest. A pointed counter-image to productivity culture, though a format that may feel more concept than drama.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental theatre working in gaming, with a clear critical position on burnout and escapism.

**Official Link:** [HAU — Let's Play I Am Old and Tired](https://www.hebbel-am-ufer.de/en/programme/pdetail/total-refusal-theater-im-bahnhof-lets-play-i-am-old-and-tired/)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Lúcia Koch — New Systems (For Relationships)

**Venue:** carlier | gebauer, Markgrafenstraße 67

**Date(s):** 10 September – 28 October 2026 (closes Wednesday)

**Time(s):** Tuesday–Saturday 11:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Freestanding structures designed around viewers' bodies rather than a particular architectural space, developed by the São Paulo-based artist since 2022. Koch treats architecture as a language, adding or subtracting filters, images and colour so that each visitor's movement produces new combinations of colour, perception and proximity.

**Why It Fits:** A last-day chance for a participatory, perception-based installation; Wednesday is the final opening day.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — New Systems (For Relationships)](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45051/new-systems-for-relationships)

### Salomé Chatriot — Void is Seductive

**Venue:** OFFICE IMPART, Waldenserstraße 2–4

**Date(s):** 10 September – 30 October 2026 (closes Friday)

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Friday 15:00–18:00, and by appointment

**Why It Fits:** Video and sound-led work in a small project space, with only three opening days left.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Void is Seductive](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45195/void-is-seductive)
