# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of June 10–16, 2026

KW anchors the week with the Kyiv Biennial opening — the city's most consequential contemporary-art event — while Arsenal and Wolf Kino programme essay film and political cinema that matches the same urgency.

## Top Picks

### Kyiv Biennial – A Bird That Cannot Land

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art

**Date(s):** 11 June – 13 September 2026 (opening week)

**Time(s):** Wed–Mon 11:00–19:00 (closed Tue); opening Wed 10 June, 19:00–22:00

**Short Context:** The nomadic Kyiv Biennial occupies all of KW with over 40 intergenerational voices — Hito Steyerl, Hiwa K, Mona Hatoum, Dana Kavelina, and many others — threading post-Soviet Eastern Europe with Central and Southwest Asia. Live concerts, listening sessions, and discursive talks (including Hito Steyerl on pipelines, 13 June) sit alongside a building-spanning exhibition on exile, coloniality, and broken continuities of meaning.

**Why It Fits:** Geopolitics, migration, decolonial thought, and sound as social practice — the week's most consequential opening for research-based, politically urgent contemporary art.

**Official Link:** [KW — Kyiv Biennial A Bird That Cannot Land](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/kyiv-biennial-a-bird-that-cannot-land)

### Afterlives

**Venue:** Wolf Kino

**Date(s):** Thursday, 11 June 2026 (German cinema release)

**Time(s):** Evening screening; confirm time with venue

**Short Context:** Kevin B. Lee's first feature-length desktop documentary traces how images of extremist violence circulate from museum archives to AI reconstructions. Medusa becomes a through-line for asking whether looking at violence implicates the viewer — Lee, artist Morehshin Allahyari, and researchers appear on screen as the film moves between virtual and physical space.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental moving image with explicit technology-and-power framing; socially engaged essay form with the director present in Berlin on release day.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal distribution — Afterlives](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/archiv-distribution/kinostarts-und-programme/afterlives/)

### Ein Abend für Hartmut Bitomsky (Bresson & Bitomsky Shorts + Talk)

**Venue:** Kino Arsenal

**Date(s):** Sunday, 14 June 2026

**Time(s):** 18:00 & 20:30

**Short Context:** Arsenal honours Hartmut Bitomsky — co-founder of the German Film and Television Academy and a key figure in West German political cinema — with an evening pairing Robert Bresson shorts and Bitomsky's own work, followed by conversation. A rare programmatic tribute to a filmmaker whose essayistic, institution-critical practice shaped generations of Berlin moving-image artists.

**Why It Fits:** Political-historical cinema, archival repertory, and critical discourse in one evening — core Arsenal territory with genuine historical weight.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal June 2026 calendar (PDF)](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/assets/Kino/PDFs/26-06_Kalendarium-Plakat_arsenal_A1_cmyk_Web.pdf)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Holly Herndon & Mat Dryhurst – Starmirror

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art

**Date(s):** 27 June – 11 October 2026 (opens late month)

**Time(s):** Wed–Mon 11:00–19:00 (closed Tue)

**Short Context:** Herndon and Dryhurst's interdisciplinary installation probes AI, collective agency, and technological power — continuing their long-running collaboration at the intersection of sound, software, and critical media theory. Opens at KW days after the Kyiv Biennial chapter, making the institute the week's dominant address for technology-and-politics art.

**Why It Fits:** Sound art, media art, and research-based practice interrogating how platforms shape subjectivity — a strong thematic bridge from this week's biennial opening.

**Official Link:** [KW press release — Starmirror (PDF)](https://www.kw-berlin.de/files/250730_KW_Herndon-Dryhurst_Press-Release_EN.pdf)

## Film & Screenings

### Magical History Tour – Something Different by Věra Chytilová

**Venue:** Kino Arsenal

**Date(s):** Friday, 12 June 2026

**Time(s):** 17:00

**Short Context:** Chytilová's 1963 debut — banned in Czechoslovakia for its formal audacity — follows two young women who respond to a world of deadening conformity by declaring everything "spoiled" and acting accordingly. Arsenal's Magical History Tour strand recovers feminist Eastern European cinema that mainstream repertory rarely programmes.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental international cinema with gender politics and anti-conformist form; a concise counterpoint to the week's longer documentary and biennial programming.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal June 2026 calendar (PDF)](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/assets/Kino/PDFs/26-06_Kalendarium-Plakat_arsenal_A1_cmyk_Web.pdf)

## Advance Radar

### Abdruckkörper

**Venue:** Ballhaus Naunynstraße

**Date(s):** 17–21 June 2026

**Time(s):** See venue for performance schedule

**Short Context:** Film director Yatri Niehaus makes his theatre debut with the eighth afrodeutsche drama developed at the Ballhaus — a protagonist navigates whiteness as architectural constraint, bodily stake, and inherited dream. Text born in the house's Unconventional Signs writing workshop; stroboscopic lighting noted.

**Why It Fits:** Post-migrant identity, embodied memory, and experimental theatre from Berlin's most consistent stage for diasporic dramaturgy — opens the day after this briefing week ends.

**Official Link:** [Ballhaus Naunynstraße — Abdruckkörper](https://ballhausnaunynstrasse.de/play/abdruckkoerper?language=en)

### Fragile Freiheit – Georgian Documentary Program

**Venue:** Arsenal

**Date(s):** Saturday, 27 June 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00

**Short Context:** Curator Salomé Jashi assembles an essayistic programme on youth, women, and family in Georgia — with Tamuna Karumidze's work and a post-screening discussion. Politically charged documentary from a region whose cultural visibility in Berlin remains thin outside festival circuits.

**Why It Fits:** Socially engaged documentary with curator conversation; geopolitical framing aligned with the week's KW biennial themes.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal — Fragile Freiheit](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/kino/filmvorfuehrung/fragile-freiheit-5340/)

### Sonnenbrand & Mückenstich – shorts attack Kurzfilmprogramm

**Venue:** ACUDkino

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 17 June 2026

**Time(s):** 21:15
