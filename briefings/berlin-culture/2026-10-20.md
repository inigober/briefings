# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 21–27, 2026

A week of installations that work on the senses and evenings built around listening: KW opens Koo Jeong A’s multisensory OUSSSEAN to the public, silent green gives Friday to ambient drone and then to Julius Eastman, and DOCK 11 turns dance writing into a four-night conversation. Around that, a feminist film programme from East Germany’s 1980s underground and a performance that treats pleasure and pain as language.

## Top Picks

### KOO JEONG A — OUSSSEAN

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00; closed Tuesdays. Wednesday 21 October, 20:00: concert “Vaseline”. Saturday 24 October, 16:00: English tour of both current exhibitions.

**Short Context:** Koo Jeong A’s first major solo show in Germany, from an artist who lived in Berlin in the 1990s and 2000s. About sixteen works from past decades are reconfigured for KW’s building: shaking walls, magnet towers, drawings and phosphorescent paintings. Force, magnetic field, light, scent, sound and temperature are combined into environments that ask for patient looking and test where vision ends.

**Why It Fits:** Sound- and sensation-based installation at a priority institution, with a closing date months out so it can be seen on a quiet weekday.

**Official Link:** [KW — KOO JEONG A: OUSSSEAN](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/koo-jeong-a-ousssean)

### HALF LIGHT — Abul Mogard, Marja de Sanctis, Rafael Anton Irisarri, Concepción Huerta

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Betonhalle

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** An audiovisual evening presented as one continuous progression. Concepción Huerta opens with magnetic tape, feedback and filtering; Abul Mogard and Marja de Sanctis premiere a new audiovisual work made for the Betonhalle, extending Mogard’s 2025 album *Quiet Pieces*; Rafael Anton Irisarri closes solo with bowed guitar and reverberant drones from *Points of Inaccessibility*.

**Why It Fits:** Ambient and experimental electronic work with named artists, a new commission for the room, and a thread of memory and alienation in the sound itself.

**Official Link:** [silent green — HALF LIGHT](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/half-light-abul-mogard-marja-de-sanctis-rafael-anton-irisarri-concepcion-huerta?cHash=a14cede126b8d95ed4bf4ad3b5f099b5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=23&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

### Cranky Choreographing Books — Peter Pleyer & Gäste

**Venue:** DOCK 11, Kastanienallee 79

**Date(s):** Thursday 22 – Sunday 25 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:30 each evening. Saturday 24 October has live music by Zsolt Sőrés with Eszter Gál; other nights use a prerecorded track.

**Short Context:** Twenty years after his solo *Choreographing Books* (2005), Peter Pleyer asks how writing about dance, and the dance that grows out of that writing, have changed. Each night he works through books and magazines in dialogue with a guest (Eszter Gál, Sappho Bogataj, Maciej Sado), takes audience questions, dances with the guest, and ends with an open Contemplative Dance Practice score.

**Why It Fits:** Conceptual choreography and dance discourse in a participatory format; four dates make it the easiest performing-arts slot of the week to fit in.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Cranky Choreographing Books](https://dock11-berlin.de/theater/programm/spielplan/cranky-choreographing-books)

### Julius Pristauz with Elena Francalanci — sketch for a conversation on pleasure and pain

**Venue:** Kunst Raum Mitte, Auguststraße 21

**Date(s):** Thursday, 22 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00

**Short Context:** Pristauz, with Francalanci, stages a performance within and around his installation *sketch for an assembly*, using it as the stage. Sexual gestures, dance movements and poses blend with intimacy and confrontation, drawing on pop culture and the performers’ own choreography. The body is treated as a place where language can seduce, interrupt or fail. The page gives no running time.

**Why It Fits:** Performance art on the body and communication, in an installation context rather than a theatre; a single dated evening.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — sketch for a conversation on pleasure and pain](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46008/sketch-for-a-conversation-on-pleasure-and-pain)

### Acephalic Headspace pres. ladr ache + Lésions Graves Sextet plays Julius Eastman

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** An abrasive evening: ladr ache and the Lésions Graves Sextet play an Eastman composition rearranged for electric guitars and drums, alongside polyphonic vocals, drones and amplified objects, with Tim Tetzner’s *Flicker Holes* video loop. The programme addresses racism and homophobia directly and leans into political provocation. The Eastman piece is not named in advance. It starts at the same hour as HALF LIGHT in the Betonhalle, so choose one.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental music with named artists and an explicit political charge, in a repertoire (Eastman) that rarely gets live airings in Berlin.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Acephalic Headspace / Lésions Graves Sextet plays Julius Eastman](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/acephalic-headspace-pres-ladr-ache-lesions-graves-sextet-plays-julius-eastman?cHash=a14cede126b8d95ed4bf4ad3b5f099b5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=23&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)
