# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 21–27, 2026

The week pairs slow, immersive work with abrupt endings: Koo Jeong A fills KW with scent, sound and temperature, silent green stages a night of drone and tape on Friday, and two gallery shows close within a day of each other. Around them sit a dance revisited after twenty years, a feminist film programme from the GDR underground, and Tanja Ostojić opening at Bethanien.

## Top Picks

### Koo Jeong A — OUSSSEAN

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays; one Thursday a month open until 21:00. Wednesday 21 October, 20:00–21:00: concert "Vaseline". Saturday 24 October, 16:00–17:00: English-language tour of this show and Susan Hiller's *Bad Dreams*.

**Short Context:** A multisensory installation in which sound, scent, temperature, magnetic fields and phosphorescence are reconfigured for KW's architecture. It is the kind of work that asks for time in the room rather than a quick circuit, and it opened only days ago. The Saturday tour also takes in Hiller's survey in the same house.

**Why It Fits:** Immersive installation, sound and perception at a priority institution; a long run, so there is no rush, but the Wednesday concert and Saturday tour give a reason to go this week.

**Official Link:** [KW — Koo Jeong A: OUSSSEAN](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/koo-jeong-a-ousssean)

### HALF LIGHT — Abul Mogard, Marja de Sanctis, Rafael Anton Irisarri, Concepción Huerta

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Betonhalle

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** An audiovisual evening built as one continuous progression. Abul Mogard and Marja de Sanctis premiere a new work extending his 2025 album *Quiet Pieces*, made for the Betonhalle's architecture. Rafael Anton Irisarri plays bowed-guitar drones from *Points of Inaccessibility*, and Concepción Huerta opens with magnetic tape, feedback and filtering.

**Why It Fits:** Ambient and drone practice tied to archives, memory and alienation, at a priority music venue, with a premiere rather than a touring repeat.

**Official Link:** [silent green — HALF LIGHT](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/half-light-abul-mogard-marja-de-sanctis-rafael-anton-irisarri-concepcion-huerta?cHash=a14cede126b8d95ed4bf4ad3b5f099b5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=23&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

### Cranky Choreographing Books — Peter Pleyer & Gäste

**Venue:** DOCK 11, Kastanienallee 79

**Date(s):** Thursday 22 – Sunday 25 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:30 each evening

**Short Context:** Peter Pleyer returns to his 2005 solo *Choreographing Books* twenty years on, asking how writing about dance, and the dance that grows out of it, have changed. With a rotating guest each night he talks through books and magazines, takes audience questions, dances with the guest, and closes by inviting the room into a Contemplative Dance Practice score. On Saturday 24 October Zsolt Sőrés plays live with Eszter Gál; other nights use a recorded track.

**Why It Fits:** Conceptual choreography and dance writing as a lived archive; four evenings, each with different guests, so the choice of night matters.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Cranky Choreographing Books](https://dock11-berlin.de/theater/programm/spielplan/cranky-choreographing-books)

### Strawalde — Parade

**Venue:** Galerie Michael Haas, Niebuhrstraße 5

**Date(s):** 10 September – 23 October 2026 (closes Friday)

**Time(s):** Opening hours are not on the listing.

**Short Context:** Work by Strawalde (born 1931), the painting name of the documentary filmmaker Jürgen Böttcher. In the GDR his painting was branded formalist and he was expelled from the artists' association, which kept it largely out of view until after reunification. Friday is the last day.

**Why It Fits:** Archives and suppressed histories in East German art, with a figure known as a filmmaker first; closing this week.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Strawalde: Parade](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45110/parade)

### Julius Pristauz & Elena Francalanci — sketch for a conversation on pleasure and pain

**Venue:** Kunst Raum Mitte, Auguststraße 21

**Date(s):** Thursday, 22 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00

**Short Context:** A performance staged inside the installation *sketch for an assembly*. Sexual gestures, dance and poses mix with intimacy and confrontation, and pop-culture references meet the performers' own choreography. Language is treated as something that can seduce, interrupt or fail.

**Why It Fits:** Body-centred performance and installation in one room, one night.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — sketch for a conversation on pleasure and pain](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46008/sketch-for-a-conversation-on-pleasure-and-pain)

### Acephalic Headspace pres. ladr ache + Lésions Graves Sextet plays Julius Eastman

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; concert 20:00

**Short Context:** An abrasive programme in which Lésions Graves Sextet rearrange a Julius Eastman composition for electric guitars and drums, alongside ladr ache's polyphonic vocals, drones and amplified objects, and Tim Tetzner's video loop *Flicker Holes*. The evening addresses racism, homophobia and political provocation directly. It runs in the same building and on the same night as HALF LIGHT, in the other hall, so choose one.

**Why It Fits:** Eastman's legacy and confrontational experimental music with named artists.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Acephalic Headspace](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/acephalic-headspace-pres-ladr-ache-lesions-graves-sextet-plays-julius-eastman?cHash=a14cede126b8d95ed4bf4ad3b5f099b5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=23&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)
