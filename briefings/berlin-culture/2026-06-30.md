### Philipp Gufler — Imitationen von Paul

**Venue:** BQ

**Date(s):** 2 May – 4 July 2026 (closes Saturday)

**Time(s):** Tue–Sat 11:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Philipp Gufler's fourth solo at BQ enters dialogue with Paul Hoecker (1854–1910), a Munich professor forced from his post in 1898 after scandal over a male model posing as the Virgin Mary. On two 12.5-metre fabric panels Gufler screen-prints motifs drawn from archival research into Hoecker's scattered oeuvre and the destroyed image archive of Magnus Hirschfeld's Institute for Sexual Science. Companion ceramics extend the inquiry into queer historical erasure. The show closes this week — last chance before the run ends.

**Why It Fits:** Research-based artistic practice, queer history, archives, and historical trauma; strong match to identity and memory priorities.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Imitationen von Paul](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/42826/imitationen-von-paul)
