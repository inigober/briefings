# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 14–20, 2026

The week is organised around a last night and a first survey: Arthur Jafa’s 2026 film closes Julia Stoschek Foundation’s Berlin venue on Thursday, and KW opens Susan Hiller’s first Berlin retrospective the following evening. Around that: Alpha Maid in the Kuppelhalle on Wednesday, an ecosexual ritual at HAU on Sunday, and a sabar night at HKW that treats polyrhythm as politics rather than as décor.

## Top Picks

### Susan Hiller — Bad Dreams

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027 (opens Friday, 16 October)

**Time(s):** Opening Friday 19:00–22:00. German-language overview tour Saturday 17 October, 16:00–17:00. Regular visiting hours are not on the exhibition page.

**Short Context:** First Berlin survey of Hiller (1940–2019), a city that shaped her work in the early 2000s. Emma Enderby and Liberty Adrien, with Lara Scherrieble, stage five decades of ghosts she actually meant: cultural discards, UFO testimony, near-death voices, children’s wallpaper, holy water, and the internet treated as a psychic archive rather than as a medium upgrade. The uncanny is never far from the toy kitchen. Same Friday 19:00 opening as Rafik Greiss and Koo Jeong A in the same house — one KW night, not a three-stop crawl; Greiss is the other listing this week, Koo Jeong A is not a third. Saturday’s German tour is the appointment if you want the rooms without the opening crowd.

**Why It Fits:** Memory, archives, and research-based multimedia at a priority institution; a dated run with a closing date rather than another opening-only invitation.

**Official Link:** [KW — Susan Hiller: Bad Dreams](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/susan-hiller-bad-dreams)

### Arthur Jafa — Niggas in Omelas

**Venue:** Julia Stoschek Foundation Berlin, Leipziger Straße 60

**Date(s):** Thursday, 15 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:30. About 60 minutes. Jafa attends. Free admission, limited capacity.

**Short Context:** European premiere of Jafa’s 2026 film, and the last public event in this Stoschek building. It played once, in Los Angeles, inside the public programme for *What a Wonderful World*; it has not been shown since. The title borrows Le Guin’s Omelas — a city whose happiness is purchased off-stage — and puts the slur back in the room rather than in a footnote. Thursday 19:30 is the one hour; do not invent extra Stoschek dates from Saturday–Sunday gallery hours. Haus am Waldsee opens the same evening at 19:00; C/O’s militarized-landscape talk starts at 18:00. Pick one.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental moving image and political cinema with the artist present; a single dated screening rather than a repertory cluster.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Arthur Jafa at Julia Stoschek Foundation](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46063/n-s-in-omelas-2026-by-arthur-jafa)

### Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 14 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Alpha Maid — South-East London guitar, AD 93, the 2025 LP *Is this a queue* — treats dub (Jamaican heritage, Windrush) and the unknown 96% of the universe as the same problem: the official story is too small, so the present tense has to be rebuilt in public. Dylan Kerr (Berlin, voice, microtonality, extended technique) and Madeline Stepien (New York, sound as perceptual manipulation) open as a two-person machine: Kerr is the source, Stepien processes live until the voice is diffracted rather than accompanied. Wednesday is the one Kuppelhalle appointment; DUST on Saturday is a different night in the same hall, not a second Alpha Maid listing.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental electronic, electroacoustic, and diaspora-grounded guitar practice with named artists at a priority music venue; a concrete Wednesday rather than a generic DJ billing.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/alpha-maid?cHash=1ae0a27da0bf240222b075c344e69ce5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=14&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)
