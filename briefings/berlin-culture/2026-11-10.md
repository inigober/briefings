# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of November 11–17, 2026

A week built around sound and memory: Sophie Calle's first Berlin solo show in two decades opens at Hamburger Bahnhof as the museum marks its 30th anniversary, while silent green hosts two very different takes on electronics, one rooted in Persian percussion and one in Japanese song. A factory-floor horror film from Indonesia and a night of emerging choreographers add sharper edges.

## Top Picks

### Sophie Calle — Is It Better?

**Venue:** Hamburger Bahnhof – Nationalgalerie der Gegenwart, Invalidenstraße 50

**Date(s):** On view 13 November 2026 – 4 April 2027 (opening event Thursday, 12 November)

**Time(s):** Opening Thursday, 12 November, 19:00. Regular hours: Tuesday, Wednesday, Friday 10:00–18:00; Thursday 10:00–20:00; Saturday and Sunday 11:00–18:00; closed Monday. The museum's 30th-anniversary weekend (13–15 November) includes a continuous 30-hour opening with free admission around the clock from 14 to 15 November.

**Short Context:** Calle's first solo exhibition in Berlin in over 20 years gathers six bodies of work from three decades across photography, text, installation and video. It begins with *The Detachment* (1996), photographs of former GDR symbols in the city, and includes the large-scale installation *Les fantômes d'Orsay* (2022). She is also making a new work to be donated to the museum's collection.

**Why It Fits:** Memory, loss and the traces of history in public space, handled through archival and text-based methods; the GDR-symbol series ties directly to Berlin's urban memory.

**Official Link:** [SMB — Sophie Calle: Is It Better?](https://www.smb.museum/en/exhibitions/detail/sophie-calle/)

### Yasuaki Shimizu + Merma Suelo

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Friday, 13 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Saxophonist, composer and producer Yasuaki Shimizu, whose career spans nearly 50 years of crossover pop, film scores and collaborations with Ryuichi Sakamoto and Björk, performs new arrangements of songs from his catalogue in a duo with his sister Mieko Shimizu. His 1982 album *Kakashi* and the band Mariah's *Utakata No Hibi* found a wider Western audience through reissues. Poet and sound artist Merma Suelo layers processed samples, field recordings, synths and voice.

**Why It Fits:** A bridge between Japanese pop-song idioms and experimental electronic production, paired with a sound artist working in processed field recordings; it also follows the Sakamoto exhibition shown at Hamburger Bahnhof this autumn.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Yasuaki Shimizu + Merma Suelo](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail?cHash=9accc7bd0a5496b5b6f31594114b76c3&tx_news_pi1%5Baction%5D=detail&tx_news_pi1%5Bcontroller%5D=News&tx_news_pi1%5Bnews_preview%5D=3984)

### World Cinema Fund x Wolf | Celebrating Cinema Together: Sleep No More

**Venue:** Wolf Kino, Weserstraße 59

**Date(s):** Friday, 13 November 2026

**Time(s):** 18:30; 96 minutes, Indonesian with English subtitles

**Short Context:** Edwin's *Sleep No More* (*Monster Pabrik Rambut*) is a horror story set in a factory whose owner pushes her workers into punishing shifts; a daughter works nights there to test her theory about what drove her mother to suicide. Cineuropa calls it "a perfect, surprisingly effective satire". The screening launches Wolf's partnership with the Berlinale World Cinema Fund.

**Why It Fits:** Politically engaged genre cinema from Southeast Asia that uses the supernatural to talk about labour and exhaustion.

**Official Link:** [Wolf Kino — Sleep No More](https://wolfberlin.org/en/programme/events/world-cinema-fund-x-wolf-celebrating-cinema-together-sleep-no-more)

## Exhibitions Radar

### KOO JEONG A — OUSSSEAN

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** 11:00–19:00; closed Tuesdays, including 17 November. Thursday, 12 November: free entry 16:00–21:00 and an English tour 16:00–17:00. Saturday, 14 November: German tour 16:00–17:00.

**Short Context:** Koo Jeong A's multisensory installations work with sound, vibration, scent, magnetic fields and phosphorescence. The free Thursday evening and the guided tours this week are a good way into a show that rewards slow looking.

**Why It Fits:** Immersive installation and sound art in a single room-scale practice.

**Official Link:** [KW — KOO JEONG A: OUSSSEAN](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/koo-jeong-a-ousssean)

## Performing Arts

### 7 STARS! BERLIN 2026 — With all our senses

**Venue:** DOCK 11 EDEN, Breite Straße 43, Pankow

**Date(s):** Thursday, 12 and Friday, 13 November 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00 on both evenings

**Short Context:** A platform for seven emerging choreographers from Berlin's independent dance scene, curated by Lia Pavlidis and Brigitte Bretternitz. The line-up includes The Darvish, Malou Hendel, Quincy Junor, Sasha Monusova, Panagiotis Tasoulis and Camila von Hein, with styles ranging from quiet and tender to wild and powerful. Tickets are €15–20 on a sliding scale, cash at the door; reservations through the event page.

**Why It Fits:** Contemporary dance and choreography from the independent scene, in a format that shows many voices in one evening.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — 7 STARS! BERLIN 2026](https://dock11-berlin.de/tanzschule/programm/kalender/7-stars-berlin-2026)

## Music

### Sintonia 2026 Attunement II: Nnux + Cinna Peyghamy

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle, Gerichtstraße 35

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Mexico City-born Nnux splits and reassembles her processed voice over heavy beats and layered synths, drawing on her album *Metamorfosis*. Paris-based French-Iranian percussionist Cinna Peyghamy builds his own instruments, from DIY synthesizers to motor-driven mechanical structures, and combines them with traditional Persian percussion in improvised sets.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental electronic and electroacoustic work built around the voice and self-made instruments.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Sintonia 2026 Attunement II](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/sintonia-2026-attunement-ii)

## Wildcards

### Carsten Nicolai — WAVE WEAVE & SONO OBI

**Venue:** Galerie EIGEN + ART Berlin, Auguststraße 26

**Date(s):** Thursday, 12 November 2026 (opening)

**Time(s):** 17:00–20:00

**Short Context:** An opening for new work by Carsten Nicolai, the artist behind the alva noto sound projects, whose practice sits between sound, image and measurement. The listing gives no further description, so treat this as a chance to see the work first-hand rather than a programmed event. The gallery is a short walk from KW, which makes an easy pairing with the Koo Jeong A free Thursday.

**Why It Fits:** Sound art from one of its best-known Berlin practitioners.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Carsten Nicolai: WAVE WEAVE & SONO OBI](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46191/wave-weave-sono-obi)

### Editing Encounters — presented by The Invisible Archive

**Venue:** New Toni, Immanuelkirchstraße 15

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 11 November 2026

**Time(s):** 18:00–22:00

**Short Context:** A book launch with Jakob Urban and Yon Natalie Mik for a new Invisible Archive publication on embodied writing and editing, which treats friendship, affection, care and partiality as valid ways of knowing. The launch is accompanied by an exhibition.

**Why It Fits:** An archive-minded publication project that questions scholarly objectivity, in an unusual launch-plus-exhibition format.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Editing Encounters](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46538/editing-encounters-presented-by-the-invisible-archive)
