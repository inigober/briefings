# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of August 19–25, 2026

This is a week of concentrated signals rather than calendar sprawl: queer archive work, post-reunification film, and a run of small but pointed openings do more than the thinner festival grid around them. The strongest picks lean toward memory, visibility, and how bodies move through institutions, streets, and public discourse.

### FORMER EAST / FORMER WEST

**Venue:** Arsenal Filminstitut, Berlin

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 19 August 2026

**Time(s):** 12:00–13:30

**Short Context:** Shelly Silver's screening and conversation at Arsenal takes post-reunification Berlin as both subject and argument, using the city as a testing ground for what democracy, capitalism, and socialist memory look like after the wall's afterlife hardened into policy and real-estate logic. The summer-school framing should not be mistaken for an academic footnote: Arsenal is strongest when it lets film operate as a civic thinking tool. In a very sparse cinema week, this is the one screening that feels genuinely urgent.

**Why It Fits:** Political cinema, Berlin history, and public discussion at a priority film venue; a direct hit on the brief's interests in democracy, memory, and urban transformation.

**Official Link:** [Arsenal summer school 2026 programme](https://www.arsenal-berlin.de/campus/summer-school/2026/)
