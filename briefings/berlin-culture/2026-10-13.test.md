# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 14–20, 2026

KW Institute opens two major exhibitions on Saturday, one tracing dreams and the uncanny, the other a desert-set cinematic essay with its own soundtrack. Around them, silent green anchors a week of voice, dub and audiovisual electronics.

## Top Picks

### Susan Hiller — Bad Dreams

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027 (opening reception 16 October)

**Time(s):** Opening reception 16 October, 19:00–22:00; German-language overview tour 17 October, 16:00–17:00; regular hours Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00

**Short Context:** The first survey of Susan Hiller's work in Berlin spans five decades of multimedia practice around dreams, trance, UFO accounts and near-death experiences. Ghostly images and levitating bodies sit beside children's wallpaper, toy kitchens and holy water. It also revisits her early use of the internet as an artistic medium.

**Why It Fits:** A direct match for memory-and-archives interests: Hiller treats collective testimony as material and asks how we give form to what we cannot explain.

**Official Link:** [KW Institute — Susan Hiller, Bad Dreams](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/susan-hiller-bad-dreams)

### Rafik Greiss — The City and the City

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00 (KW regular hours)

**Short Context:** Greiss's first institutional solo show centres on a newly commissioned film of figures waiting in vast desert landscapes for an event that may never arrive. A soundtrack by 7038634357 extends into the gallery, alongside black-and-white photographs of objects and abandoned places stripped of context. The refusal to supply geography or history is the point, though it may frustrate viewers wanting an anchor.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental moving image and audiovisual installation in one room, with sound treated as a spatial element.

**Official Link:** [KW Institute — Rafik Greiss, The City and the City](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/rafik-greiss-the-city-and-the-city)

### Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 14 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Two contrasting acts share the bill. Alpha Maid, a South East London guitarist and songwriter, presents *Is this a queue*, blending dub, rooted in her Jamaican heritage, with indie-style songwriting. Dylan Kerr's voice and microtonal techniques are distorted and reshaped live by Madeline Stepien.

**Why It Fits:** Extended vocal technique and real-time processing meet diasporic dub lineage, which is a strong electroacoustic match.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/alpha-maid?cHash=1ae0a27da0bf240222b075c344e69ce5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=14&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

## Wildcards

### Butoh Intensive “Materiality”

**Venue:** EDEN, Studio 150.1, Breite Strasse 43, 13187 Berlin

**Date(s):** 17–18 October 2026

**Time(s):** 10:00–17:00 each day, including lunch break

**Short Context:** A participatory two-day workshop led by Natsuko Kono, using material imagery to transform the body rather than mime it. It is a workshop, not a public performance; €200, taught in English and open to all.

**Why It Fits:** Embodied practice for visitors who want to take part rather than watch.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Butoh Intensive “Materiality”](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/tanzschule/program/workshops/butoh-intensive-materiality)
