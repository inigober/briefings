# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 14–20, 2026

KW turns the whole building over to its fall program on Friday the 16th: Susan Hiller's first Berlin survey and Rafik Greiss's first institutional solo open together the next morning and run to January, and between them they cover most of what this week has in images, archives and sound. Around that the Kuppelhalle takes Wednesday and Saturday, one Charlottenburg gallery is in the last month of a two-month hang, and the only dance on offer is dance you do yourself — two weekend intensives, no stage, no film programme worth the trip.

## Top Picks

### Susan Hiller — Bad Dreams

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69, Mitte

**Date(s):** 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027 (opens Saturday 17 October)

**Time(s):** Fall Program opening Friday 16 October, 19:00–22:00. German-language overview tour Saturday 17 October, 16:00–17:00. Regular hours Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays; one Thursday a month open until 21:00 with free admission.

**Short Context:** The first survey of Hiller's work in the city she worked in during the early 2000s, curated by Emma Enderby and Liberty Adrien with Lara Scherrieble. Hiller spent five decades treating dreams, trances, hallucinations and other uncanny experience as evidence rather than as decoration — material that mass media and then the internet keep circulating without ever admitting what it is. The range on show is the point: ghostly images and levitating bodies at one end, children's wallpaper and toy kitchens at the other. It runs to January, so there is no urgency to Saturday; Friday is the one night the whole house is on.

**Why It Fits:** Memory, archives and the uncanny as a research practice, at a priority institution; a named survey with a published run rather than an autumn group-show cluster.

**Official Link:** [KW — Susan Hiller: Bad Dreams](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/susan-hiller-bad-dreams)

### Rafik Greiss — The City and the City

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69, Mitte

**Date(s):** 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027 (opens Saturday 17 October)

**Time(s):** Fall Program opening Friday 16 October, 19:00–22:00. Regular hours Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays.

**Short Context:** Greiss's first institutional solo, built around a newly commissioned film of the same title: figures waiting in desert landscapes for an event that may never arrive. A soundtrack by 7038634357 carries the film's atmosphere out of the screening situation and into the galleries, and black-and-white photographs of objects, artefacts and empty sites are hung stripped of the context that would explain them — which is either the method or the hedge, depending on how the room reads. Same curators, same house, same opening night as Hiller: one visit, two arguments, and this is the one for moving image rather than for archive.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental moving image, sound art and audiovisual installation inside a single commissioned work; a first institutional solo with a published run, not a group-show cameo.

**Official Link:** [KW — Rafik Greiss: The City and the City](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/rafik-greiss-the-city-and-the-city)

### Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 14 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Alpha Maid brings *Is this a queue* (AD 93, October 2025), a South East London guitar record that runs dub and emo through an interest in particle physics without treating any of the three as a costume — the dub is explicitly about Jamaican heritage and the Windrush Generation, and the organising line is "We only know 4% of the universe." Opening are Dylan Kerr and Madeline Stepien, who divide the work cleanly: Kerr (Berlin — process-based method, microtonality, extended vocal technique) is the sound source, Stepien (New York — sound as perceptual and emotional manipulation) is the "sound obscurer", processing him live. Two sets, one hall, no festival umbrella; Saturday in the same room is a different bill, not a second night of this one.

**Why It Fits:** Electroacoustic real-time processing, extended vocal technique and diaspora-grounded guitar music on one bill; named artists at a priority venue on a concrete Wednesday rather than a DJ listing.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Alpha Maid](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/alpha-maid?cHash=1ae0a27da0bf240222b075c344e69ce5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=14&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

### Hal Busse — Werk und Wirken

**Venue:** Galerie Volker Diehl, Niebuhrstraße 2, Charlottenburg

**Date(s):** 12 September – 13 November 2026

**Time(s):** Tuesday–Friday 11:00–18:00; Saturday by appointment only

**Short Context:** A single-artist survey for Hal Busse, titled *Werk und Wirken* — work and effect — hanging since mid-September and open through the whole briefing week. ### Andriy K. & Tharim Cornelisse pres. DUST + mu tate pres. life of mu

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Saturday, 17 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Two audiovisual projects on one bill. DUST — Andriy K. with Tharim Cornelisse on image — is built on presence and sudden disappearance: texture that arrives, registers, and then is not there. mu tate's *life of mu* works the other face of the same problem, melodies dissolving while the rhythm keeps moving underneath, density as the thing you are actually listening to. In a domed former crematorium both sets get a room that argues back. This is the week's second and last Kuppelhalle night; Wednesday is the other, and the rest of silent green's October is not this week.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental electronic and audiovisual work with named artists at a priority venue; a dated Saturday rather than a club listing.

**Official Link:** [silent green — DUST + life of mu](https://www.silent-green.net/programm/detail/andryi-k-tharim-cornelisse-pres-dust)

## Wildcards

### In Companionship of Birds – Die Vogelgefährten

**Venue:** DOCKdigital at DOCK 11, Saal 4, Kastanienallee 79, Prenzlauer Berg

**Date(s):** Saturday 17 – Sunday 18 October 2026

**Time(s):** Saturday 10:00–19:00; Sunday 10:00–18:30

**Short Context:** The Chemistry of Courage spend two days inside Attar's *The Conference of the Birds* — the twelfth-century Persian poem in which thirty birds cross seven valleys to find their king and find themselves — working it as narration, improvisation, play and conversation rather than as a staged reading. Say the obvious thing plainly: this is a workshop you take part in, €290, in German, English or both, not a performance you buy a ticket to watch. In a week with no stage programme it is also the most substantial thing on offer in the form.

**Why It Fits:** Participatory practice and storytelling built on a non-European source text; a date-verified weekend with named facilitators rather than an open call.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — In Companionship of Birds](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/tanzschule/program/workshops/in-companionship-of-birds-die-vogelgefaehrten)

### Butoh Intensive “Materiality”

**Venue:** EDEN, Studio 150.1, Breite Straße 43, Pankow

**Date(s):** Saturday 17 – Sunday 18 October 2026

**Time(s):** 10:00–17:00 each day, including a lunch break

**Short Context:** Natsuko Kono's two-day intensive works from material imagery — the body becoming the substance rather than miming it, which is the distinction that separates Butoh training from mime and the one most weekend workshops lose by lunchtime on day one. Open to all, taught in English, €200. Same weekend as the birds at Kastanienallee and the same category: practice, not programme. Pick one; both run the full Saturday.

**Why It Fits:** Embodied performance practice with a named teacher and a date-verified weekend; the closest this week comes to contemporary dance.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Butoh Intensive “Materiality”](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/tanzschule/program/workshops/butoh-intensive-materiality)

### Tanja Ostojić — Encounters: On Bodies, Borders, Resistance

**Venue:** Mariannenplatz 2, Kreuzberg

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** 17:00

**Short Context:** Ostojić has made twenty-five years of work out of EU borders, visa regimes and the paperwork of belonging — *Looking for a Husband with EU Passport* is the piece most people know, and it was never the joke it got reported as. *Encounters* puts bodies, borders and resistance in the title and nothing else on the listing, which from her is usually enough to go on.

**Why It Fits:** Migration, borders and feminist performance from an artist with a long body of work on exactly that; a dated Kreuzberg afternoon.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Encounters: On Bodies, Borders, Resistance](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/45515/encounters-on-bodies-borders-resistance)

### Cornelia Sollfrank — Revisiting Feminist Art

**Venue:** Siemensstraße 27, Moabit

**Date(s):** Friday, 23 October 2026

**Time(s):** 17:00

**Short Context:** Sollfrank built the net.art generator and, in 1997, entered hundreds of fictional women artists into a Hamburg net-art competition to demonstrate that the jury would not check. Her subject here is what the feminist art of the 1970s still has to teach, which coming from her will not be a nostalgia talk. Exactly the same hour as Ostojić, the other side of town — this is a choice, not a double bill.

**Why It Fits:** Feminism, archives and net-culture critique from a practitioner rather than a surveyor; a dated Moabit afternoon.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Cornelia Sollfrank: Revisiting Feminist Art](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/46104/cornelia-sollfrank-revisiting-feminist-art-what-we-can-learn-from-early-feminist-art)
