# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of October 14–20, 2026

KW Institute opens its autumn programme with two exhibitions that treat dreams, cities and images as unstable archives, while silent green and the Butoh studios keep the week anchored in sound and the body. A compact week, strongest on Friday and Saturday.

## Top Picks

### Susan Hiller — Bad Dreams

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027 (opening reception 16 October)

**Time(s):** Opening reception Friday 16 October, 19:00–22:00; German-language overview tour Saturday 17 October, 16:00–17:00. Regular hours Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays.

**Short Context:** A new Berlin survey of Susan Hiller's work on personal and collective imaginaries, drawing on images, objects, mass media and popular culture. It opens KW's autumn programme and gives the artist's dream-and-archive practice a rare extended showing in the city.

**Why It Fits:** A direct match for the memory-and-archives thread: Hiller treats cultural leftovers as evidence of what a society cannot say aloud.

**Official Link:** [KW Institute — Susan Hiller, Bad Dreams](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/susan-hiller-bad-dreams)

### Rafik Greiss — The City and the City

**Venue:** KW Institute for Contemporary Art, Auguststraße 69

**Date(s):** On view 17 October 2026 – 10 January 2027

**Time(s):** Wednesday–Monday 11:00–19:00, closed Tuesdays; open until 21:00 on selected Thursdays.

**Short Context:** The first institutional solo exhibition by Rafik Greiss centres on a newly commissioned cinematic essay, a soundtrack that extends into the gallery, and black-and-white photographs. Sound and moving image are treated as one spatial material.

**Why It Fits:** Experimental moving image and audiovisual installation, in a single room-sized work rather than a screening.

**Official Link:** [KW Institute — Rafik Greiss, The City and the City](https://www.kw-berlin.de/en/exhibitions/rafik-greiss-the-city-and-the-city)

### Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 14 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** Dylan Kerr and Madeline Stepien combine extended vocal techniques, microtonality, psychoacoustics and live processing. Alpha Maid adds guitar-led music with dub influences tied explicitly to Jamaican heritage and the Windrush Generation.

**Why It Fits:** Electroacoustic voice work paired with a diasporic, dub-inflected set: two ways of treating sound as inherited material.

**Official Link:** [silent green — Alpha Maid + Dylan Kerr & Madeline Stepien](https://www.silent-green.net/en/programme/detail/alpha-maid?cHash=1ae0a27da0bf240222b075c344e69ce5&tx_news_pi1%5Bday%5D=14&tx_news_pi1%5Bmonth%5D=10&tx_news_pi1%5Byear%5D=2026)

## Exhibitions Radar

### Hal Busse — Werk und Wirken

**Venue:** Galerie Volker Diehl, Niebuhrstraße 2, Charlottenburg

**Date(s):** On view 12 September – 13 November 2026

**Time(s):** Tuesday–Friday 11:00–18:00; Saturday by appointment only

**Short Context:** A gallery solo show by Hal Busse, a quieter counterweight to KW's openings. The title points to an artist's body of work and its effects over time.

**Why It Fits:** A commercial-gallery show that rewards a weekday visit, with a fixed end date well after this week.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Werk und Wirken](https://www.indexberlin.com/exhibitions/list/45083/werk-und-wirken)

## Music

### Andriy K. & Tharim Cornelisse pres. DUST + mu tate pres. life of mu

**Venue:** silent green Kulturquartier, Kuppelhalle

**Date(s):** Saturday, 17 October 2026

**Time(s):** Doors 19:00; start 20:00

**Short Context:** An experimental electronic and audiovisual evening. DUST (Andriy K. and Tharim Cornelisse) works with textural sound and imagery around presence and sudden disappearance; mu tate follows with dissolving melodies, shifting rhythms and dense, immersive sound.

**Why It Fits:** Audiovisual electronic work in a venue suited to it, with artists who build atmosphere rather than dance-floor momentum.

**Official Link:** [silent green — DUST + life of mu](https://www.silent-green.net/programm/detail/andryi-k-tharim-cornelisse-pres-dust)

## Wildcards

### Butoh Intensive "Materiality"

**Venue:** EDEN, Studio 150.1, Breite Strasse 43, 13187 Berlin

**Date(s):** 17–18 October 2026

**Time(s):** 10:00–17:00 each day, including lunch break

**Short Context:** A two-day participatory workshop with Natsuko Kono. Participants use material imagery to transform the body rather than mime it. Taught in English, open to all; €200. This is a workshop, not a public performance.

**Why It Fits:** An embodied-practice option for readers who would rather take part than watch.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — Butoh Intensive "Materiality"](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/tanzschule/program/workshops/butoh-intensive-materiality)

### In Companionship of Birds – Die Vogelgefährten

**Venue:** DOCKdigital, DOCK 11, Saal 4, Kastanienallee 79

**Date(s):** 17–18 October 2026

**Time(s):** 17 October 10:00–19:00; 18 October 10:00–18:30

**Short Context:** A hybrid participatory workshop by The Chemistry of Courage that combines narration, improvisation, play and conversation around Attar's *The Conference of the Birds*. In German, English or both; €290. Also a workshop, not a performance.

**Why It Fits:** Storytelling as a shared practice, with a classical Persian text as the frame.

**Official Link:** [DOCK 11 — In Companionship of Birds](https://dock11-berlin.de/en/tanzschule/program/workshops/in-companionship-of-birds-die-vogelgefaehrten)

## Advance Radar

### Sabar, Polyrhythm, and the Politics of Body Movement

**Venue:** John-Foster-Dulles-Allee 10, 10557 Berlin

**Date(s):** Wednesday, 21 October 2026

**Time(s):** 19:00

**Short Context:** Led by Nago Guèye Koité and SAF SAP, a session on Senegalese sabar drumming and how its polyrhythms relate to the movement of the body.

**Why It Fits:** Rhythm, diaspora and the body in one session, just after this week's window closes.

**Official Link:** [INDEX Berlin — Sabar, Polyrhythm, and the Politics of Body Movement](https://www.indexberlin.com/events/list/45713/sabar-polyrhythm-and-the-politics-of-body-movement)
