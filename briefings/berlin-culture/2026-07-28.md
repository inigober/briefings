# Berlin Culture Briefing — Week of July 29–August 4, 2026

Late July thins into a week of finissages and festival fragments: two major gallery closings bracket the visual-art calendar before August's reopening cluster, while Radialsystem's SENSE series and DOCK 11's b12 summer anchor the performing-arts week. Arsenal keeps the film programme alive with artist-led discussion cinema and a Kubrick repertory screening — the strongest picks concentrate in dance, queer performance, and closing-soon contemporary art rather than blockbuster openings.

## Top Picks

### THE PRESSING

**Venue:** Radialsystem

**Date(s):** Thursday, 30 July – Sunday, 2 August 2026

**Time(s):** Thu–Sat 20:30; Sun 19:30 (artist talk after Saturday performance)

**Short Context:** Dani Brown's solo performance returns to Radialsystem within the SENSE festival frame — a vulva-centred choreographic proposition that reclaims the word 'CUNT' from vulgarity and treats the female sexual organ as a speculative artistic entity. Brown works from the neural connections between brain, clitoris, and carnal knowledge, accompanied by Justin F. Kennedy's immersive queer sound design and Jan Fack's Vanitas-inspired stage setting with live moths curated by Project Cultivation. The performance contains explicit nudity and runs approximately 60 minutes across four evenings; Saturday's show is followed by an artist talk moderated by Maja Zimmerman.

**Why It Fits:** Queer themes, gender, and body-political contemporary dance at a priority performance venue; interdisciplinary staging with named artists across choreography, sound, and scenography.

**Official Link:** [Radialsystem — THE PRESSING by Dani Brown](https://www.radialsystem.de/en/veranstaltungen/the-pressing-dani-brown/)

### Bernhard Martin — Holobionten

**Venue:** DITTRICH & SCHLECHTRIEM

**Date(s):** 1 July – 31 July 2026 (closes Friday)

**Time(s):** Tue–Fri 11:00–18:00

**Short Context:** Bernhard Martin's *Holobionten* closes Friday at DITTRICH & SCHLECHTRIEM's new Linienstraße headquarters — a 25-metre wall frieze the artist worked on from 2020 to 2025, described by the gallery as his opus magnum. The monumental scale and holobiont framing (organism-as-ecosystem) position the work at the intersection of painting, ecology, and multispecies thinking rather than conventional gallery-scale display. This is the week's most urgent contemporary-art appointment before the gallery calendar shifts toward August openings.

**Why It Fits:** Research-based contemporary art, ecology, and multispecies thinking at an established gallery; closing-soon urgency with significant artistic scale.
